# Lunch Meeting Unpaid Break

## User

If my employer has a meeting during lunch where they buy lunch, am I still entitled to my unpaid lunch time?

## Assistant

Whether you're entitled to unpaid lunch time during a lunch meeting where your employer provides lunch can depend on various factors, including your local labor laws and company policies. In some cases, if you're required to attend the meeting and it occurs during your usual lunch break, you may still be entitled to take that break later or receive compensation for the missed break. It's best to consult your company's HR department or review your employment contract to understand your rights in this situation.

## User

I'm in North Carolina. Does that change anything?

## Assistant

In North Carolina, there is no state law requiring employers to provide meal or rest breaks to employees over the age of 16. However, if your employer chooses to provide meal breaks, they must follow certain guidelines, such as allowing you to have an uninterrupted meal period of at least 30 minutes. 

In the scenario you described, where your employer schedules a lunch meeting during your usual lunch break and provides lunch, it's essential to review your employment contract or company policies to determine if you're entitled to unpaid lunch time or if the lunch meeting counts as part of your working hours. If your contract or policies don't address this situation, you may want to discuss it with your HR department to clarify your rights and obligations.
