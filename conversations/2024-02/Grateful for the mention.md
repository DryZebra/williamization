# Grateful for the mention

## User

How do I respond when someone mentions me in the comments section of a TikTok? It's a good TikTok, and it's about liking your friends so this person clearly thinks I'm a good friend

## Assistant

Responding to a mention in the comments section of a TikTok can be simple yet meaningful. You could reply with something like, "Aw, thanks! 😊 You're an awesome friend too!" or "Appreciate the shoutout! 🙌 Let's keep spreading the love!" This acknowledges the mention and reinforces the positive message of the TikTok.
