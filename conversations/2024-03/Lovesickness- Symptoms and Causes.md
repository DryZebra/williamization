# Lovesickness: Symptoms and Causes

## User

What is lovesickness?

## Assistant

Lovesickness is a colloquial term used to describe the emotional and physical symptoms experienced when someone is deeply in love or infatuated with someone who may not reciprocate those feelings. Symptoms can include longing, sadness, obsession, loss of appetite, difficulty sleeping, and overall distress. It's often seen as a form of unrequited love or romantic obsession.
