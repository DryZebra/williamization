## Assistant

That mix likely upset your stomach due to **chemical and physical irritation** from the combination of **DayQuil + coffee + dairy fat** on an empty (or nearly empty) stomach. Here’s what’s probably happening:

---

### 1. **DayQuil’s Ingredients**
DayQuil contains:
- **Acetaminophen** – mild stomach irritant in some people.
- **Dextromethorphan** – can cause nausea or dizziness on an empty stomach.
- **Phenylephrine** – a decongestant that constricts blood vessels, raising heart rate and sometimes triggering acid reflux or nausea.

When combined with **caffeine**, the stimulant effect is amplified, increasing stomach acid and potentially leading to **acid reflux** or **esophageal irritation** (that “burning chest” feeling).

---

### 2. **Coffee + Milk + Butter**
This mix is similar to “bulletproof coffee.” While some people tolerate it fine, others experience:
- **Increased bile release** (due to butter fat)
- **Delayed gastric emptying** (from fat)
- **Excess acid production** (from coffee)

All of which can cause **nausea, burping, or the urge to vomit**, especially with medication already irritating the stomach lining.

---

### 3. **Your Symptoms**
- **Excessive saliva** and **swallowing repeatedly** → classic pre-vomit reflex or **acid reflux** response (your body’s attempt to neutralize acid).
- **Burning chest** → likely **acid reflux or mild esophagitis** from stomach acid rising.
- **No vomit** → your stomach is trying to push back the contents but not fully emptying.

---

### 4. **What to Do Now**
- **Don’t lie down.** Stay upright for at least an hour.
- **Sip water** or **warm ginger tea** slowly.
- **Avoid dairy and caffeine** for the rest of the day.
- You can take an **antacid (like Tums or famotidine/Pepcid)** to calm acid production.
- If symptoms persist more than a few hours, or if you start vomiting, develop **chest tightness, or black/tarry vomit**, seek urgent care.

---

### 5. **Next Time**
If you need to take DayQuil:
- Eat a **light meal** (like toast or oatmeal) first.
- Wait 30 minutes before coffee.
- Avoid mixing **DayQuil + caffeine + butter/fat** together.
