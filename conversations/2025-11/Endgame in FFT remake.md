Here’s what’s really going on under the hood:

---

### ⚔️ 1. “Rend” vs. Real Damage  
Knight *Break* skills (Rend Weapon/Armor/Shield/Helm) use a **success chance**, not a damage formula. They’re control tools, not DPS.  
- Each Break uses **PA × Weapon Power** for accuracy vs enemy PA, so even with high stats they often miss.  
- Enemies rarely survive long enough to make equipment breaks tactically matter, so yeah — they *feel* useless outside special fights.

That’s why many players treat **Knight** as a *stat-farm class*: good HP/PA growth and access to heavy armor, not its actual skillset.

---

### ✨ 2. Why “Holy” Hits So Hard  
“Holy” damage = **(Faith × MA × Spell Power)** with an absurdly high spell power constant.  
- Faith on both caster and target multiplies it again (Faith / 100 × Faith / 100).  
- So a White Mage with 90 Faith will blast 200–400 damage, especially if the target also has high Faith.  
- MP cost and charge time are steep, but it’s basically the first *nuke* spell in the game.

It’s not your imagination — “Holy” is *ridiculous* because it scales on *both* sides of the equation.

---

### 🧘 3. Why Chakra Feels Weak on Mages  
Chakra = **(PA × X)** for both HP and MP restore (X ≈ 5 in most versions).  
White Mage PA ≈ 6 – 7 → heals 30 – 35 HP.  
High-level Monk PA ≈ 12 – 14 → heals 60 – 70 HP.  

So Chakra is entirely *physical-stat-based*. That’s why it feels amazing on Monks, mediocre on mages. It doesn’t use Faith, MA, or any magic modifier.

---
