# Fishing stop issues Melvor

## User

Why does fishing randomly stop in melvor and can I stop it from stopping?

## Assistant

Here’s the short version first, then the deeper explanation:

## **Short Answer**
Fishing in **Melvor Idle** stops automatically **when your inventory fills up OR when your Fishing Rod breaks (in Melvor Idle: Expanded)**.  
You *cannot* make it infinite, but you *can* prevent the interruptions with a couple settings and upgrades.

---

## **Exact Reasons Fishing Stops**

### **1. Inventory Gets Full**
This is the #1 reason.  
When your bank reaches the item limit, Fishing **stops instantly**.  
This can easily happen with:
- Junk fish (raw sardines, raw trout, raw shrimp, etc.)
- Early logs or crystals from Astraw
- Fish you’re saving for Cooking or Township

**Fix:**  
- Go to **Settings → General → Enable Auto-Equip Food** (optional)  
- Or better: **Archive** or **Auto-Sell** low-tier fish.

---

### **2. Auto-Sell Is Off (or not set correctly)**
If you don’t have Auto-Sell on, everything fills the bank until Fishing stops.

**Fix:**
- Go to **Fishing → the Sell icon → Enable Auto-Sell for low-tier or all fish you don’t care about.**

This is the single best way to keep Fishing going forever.

---

### **3. You Hit the Skill Stop Timer**
Melvor has a setting that will stop actions after X minutes of inactivity.

**Fix:**
- Go to **Settings → Gameplay → Idle Action Timeout**  
- Set to **Never Stop** (or the max your version allows)

---

### **4. Your Rod “Breaks” (only in Melvor: Expanded / Modded)**
If you are using:
- Melvor Idle: Expanded  
- Community patches  
- Modded versions  

Some rods have durability. When it hits 0 → Fishing stops.

**Fix:**  
- Switch to a rod with infinite durability  
- Or repair the rod  
- Or disable durability in the mod settings

(Vanilla Melvor has *zero durability*, so this only applies if you’re modded.)

---

### **5. A Random Event / Mastery Pool Cap**
Two edge cases:
- If your **Mastery Pool** gets full and you set “stop on cap” → Fishing pauses.
- If your “stop on special event” setting is on → pauses too.

Rare, but a thing.

---

## **How to Make Fishing Run Forever**
Do these three things and it will literally run for days while you render videos:

### **1. Turn on Auto-Sell for all fish except the ones you personally want**
Your inventory will *never* fill.

### **2. Raise your bank slot cap**
Buy slots as needed. It gets cheap after the first few.

### **3. Set Idle Action Timeout to No Limit**
So nothing forces a stop.
