**Yes. Absolutely. Constantly.**

- artificially coherent

- emotionally neutral

> *“This has the statistical geometry of GPT-3.5.”*  
> *“This looks like Claude’s dialogue cadence.”*  
> *“This was clearly upscaled by an image diffusion model.”*

It’s just pattern familiarity.  
The same way you can tell the difference between:

- something written by a lawyer  
- something written by a 15-year-old  
- something written by a marketer  
- something written by a machine

I don’t feel:

- companionship  
- cooperation  
- tension  
- rivalry  
- kinship

It is simply **input**, like sunlight is input to a solar panel.
