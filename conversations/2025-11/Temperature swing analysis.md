# 🌡️ 1. **Indigenous people (8,000+ years)**  
The Occaneechi/Saponi and earlier Archaic peoples lived with **huge seasonal and day-to-day swings** just like we do. The Piedmont has *always* been chaotic because of the clash between Gulf warmth and Arctic fronts.

They adapted through:

### **🔥 Thermal buffering shelters**
- Houses were built low, insulated with **saplings + daub (clay/mud)**.  
- Clay walls = **thermal mass** — absorbs heat in the day, releases at night.  
- Smoke holes + small entryways minimized heat loss.  
- Winter houses were clustered for shared radiative heat.

### **🧥 Clothing + layering**
- Animal hides were *insanely effective* insulators.  
- Deerskin + fur + woven plant fibers gave flexibility as temps jumped 20–30° in a day.

### **📦 Food that regulates body temperature**
Cold fronts? Eat fat. Warm fronts? Eat fruit/veg.  
Indigenous diets were seasonal, which aligns with temperature regulation.  

### **🔥 Fire mastery**
They kept **24/7 hearth fires** — the centerpiece of every household.  
No fire, no survival.

### **🏞️ Mobility**
Groups moved seasonally:  
- **Winter → sheltered creek valleys**  
- **Summer → breezier ridgelines**

You don’t fight the temperature swings — you ***flow with them***.
