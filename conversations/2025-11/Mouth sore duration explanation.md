This is the **#1 thing that fits your description**:  
A mucocele forms when you bite your lip and damage a tiny salivary duct. Instead of draining normally, the saliva pools under the skin and creates a **painless or mildly sore, round blister** that:

You should think about seeing a doctor or dentist if:
- It’s been **more than 3–4 weeks**

- It’s extremely painful
- It bleeds easily

These are less likely, but good to keep in mind.
