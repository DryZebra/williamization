# Homemade coffee creamer

## User

I'm trying to make my own coffee creamer in bulk. I've got a gallon of milk, brown sugar, and vanilla extract. What do you think? Could I just combine the ingredients at some ratio and have awesome home made creamer?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Yeah — you *absolutely* can make a great homemade creamer with exactly what you’ve got. And it’ll be way cleaner than store-bought.

Here’s a solid, simple base recipe that works great in bulk:

### Basic Vanilla Brown Sugar Creamer

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups whole milk  
- ½ cup brown sugar  
- 1–2 teaspoons vanilla extract  
- optional: pinch of salt (makes the sweetness taste deeper)

### Method:
1. Pour the milk into a saucepan.
2. Heat on **medium-low**, but don’t boil it.
3. Stir in the brown sugar until fully dissolved.
4. Remove from heat.
5. Let it cool a bit, then stir in the vanilla.
6. Pour into a jar or bottle and refrigerate.

---

### Ratios if you want to scale it

For **every 1 cup of milk**, use:
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ½–1 teaspoon vanilla

So for your **full gallon (16 cups)** of milk:

- 4 cups brown sugar  
- 8–16 teaspoons vanilla (about 2–5 tablespoons)

---

### Important realism things

This will be more like:
✅ Rich, lightly sweetened milk  
✅ Natural flavored creamer  
✅ Way healthier than chemical creamers

It will **not** be as thick as store coffee creamer unless you:
- add heavy cream, or  
- add something like condensed milk (which you aren’t using)

But honestly — it’ll taste warm, real, and good.

---

### Storage

- Keeps **5–7 days** in the fridge  
- Shake before use because the sugar can settle  

---

### Optional upgrade ideas (if you want later)

If you ever want it thicker without weird chemicals:
- Use half milk + half heavy cream
- Or add a splash of condensed milk
