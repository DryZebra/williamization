# **How Law Escaped the Seastone Handcuffs in Wano**

**Law never picked the cuffs himself. He was freed by a traitor inside the Beast Pirates — Basil Hawkins — right before Hawkins confronted him.**

### **1. Law is captured after the Straw Hats split up**
- After the raid plans start falling apart, Law is ambushed and cuffed with seastone.
- He’s brought to the capital and interrogated by Hawkins, who suspects one of the Supernovas betrayed Kaido.

### **2. Hawkins quietly frees him**
This is the part people forget because it’s subtle and happens off-screen:

- Hawkins releases Law from the cuffs *on purpose*.
- He allows Law to kill him because Killer and Kid have already destroyed Hawkins’s chances of survival.
- Hawkins admits during the fight that he switched sides too late and that he *doesn’t hate* the idea of Law trying to stop Kaido.

So when Law suddenly appears free, it’s because his cuffs were **already unlocked by Hawkins** before Law stabbed him with the Kikoku.

Law never used Shambles to escape seastone — that’s impossible while drained.
