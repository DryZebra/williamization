# Bible treat sojourner respectfully

## User

In the Bible where does it say to treat as sojourner with respect?

## Assistant



## Assistant

There are **several** places in the Bible—Old and New Testament—where God explicitly commands His people to treat the *sojourner* (foreigner, immigrant, resident alien) with respect, compassion, and equal justice.

Here are the key passages:

---

## **1. Leviticus 19:33–34 (most direct and famous)**  
**KJV:**  
> *“And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.  
But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself…”*

This is the clearest command:  
- Do not mistreat the sojourner.  
- Treat him as a native-born.  
- Love him as yourself.

---

## **2. Exodus 22:21**  
**KJV:**  
> *“Thou shalt neither vex a stranger, nor oppress him: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.”*

The logic:  
You know what it feels like to be powerless in a land not your own.

---

## **3. Exodus 23:9**  
**KJV:**  
> *“Also thou shalt not oppress a stranger: for ye know the heart of a stranger, seeing ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.”*

God grounds this command in empathy and shared experience.

---

## **4. Deuteronomy 10:18–19**  
**KJV:**  
> *“He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger…  
Love ye therefore the stranger: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.”*

This is unique because:  
- God Himself “loves the stranger.”  
- Therefore, you must too.

---

## **5. Deuteronomy 24:17–22**  
Commands:  
- Do not pervert justice for the sojourner.  
- Leave gleanings (food) for him.

This is material respect—**economic protection**, not just “be nice.”

---

## **6. Deuteronomy 27:19**  
**KJV:**  
> *“Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger…”*

Mistreating foreigners isn’t just wrong—it is placed under a **curse**.

---

## **7. Malachi 3:5**  
God says He will judge those who:  
> *“turn aside the stranger from his right.”*

---

## **8. Zechariah 7:10**  
> *“Oppress not the widow, nor the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor…”*

Foreigners are grouped with society’s most vulnerable.

---

## **9. Hebrews 13:2 (New Testament)**  
> *“Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.”*

While not a legal command like the Torah passages, it carries the same spirit:  
Hospitality to outsiders is holy.

---

## **10. Matthew 25:35**  
Jesus:  
> *“I was a stranger, and ye took me in.”*

Treating the outsider kindly is treating **Christ** kindly.
