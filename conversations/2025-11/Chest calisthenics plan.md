# Chest calisthenics plan

Is there an easy calisthenics for getting a bigger chest?

## Assistant

Yes — there are several easy, equipment-free calisthenics you can use to build a larger, stronger chest. You don’t need weights; what matters is how you use bodyweight and *progressive overload*.  

---

### **1. Foundational Chest Builder: Push-Ups**
**Baseline:** 3 sets of 10–20 reps, 3–5 days a week.  
Once that’s easy, vary the *angle*, *tempo*, or *load*.  
- **Standard Push-Up** – full range of motion, slow on the way down (3 sec).  
- **Wide Push-Up** – hands 2× shoulder width to hit outer chest.  
- **Diamond Push-Up** – hands together under chest for inner pecs + triceps.  
- **Decline Push-Up** – feet on a chair, targets upper chest.  
- **Archer Push-Up (advanced)** – shifts most weight to one arm for overload.  

---

### **2. Time-Under-Tension Routine**
To grow size, slow your reps and control the negative.  
**Example:**  
- 5 slow push-ups (5 sec down, 1 sec up)  
- 10 normal push-ups  
- 15 knee push-ups  
→ Rest 1 min → Repeat 3 rounds  

---

### **3. Volume & Progression Plan**
| Week | Focus | Sets × Reps | Goal |
|------|--------|--------------|------|
| 1–2 | Standard & wide | 3×15 | Build endurance |
| 3–4 | Add decline | 4×12 | Stimulate upper chest |
| 5–6 | Mix in diamonds | 5×10 | Add inner chest mass |
| 7+ | Archer + weighted backpack | 4×8 | Progressive overload |

---

### **4. Support Work**
- **Dips (between chairs or counters)** – 3×8–12 for lower chest.  
- **Plank to Push-Up transitions** – stability and endurance.  
- **Doorway stretch** after each session to keep shoulders mobile.
