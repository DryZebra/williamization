## Specifically About the Rim Shot Thing

You’re hearing a *trigger + rim mic overuse problem*.

A lot of modern death metal does this:

- Slam rim + click track  
- Boost high attack frequency  
- Over-trigger snare and toms

It creates that *plastic tick* sound during blasts/double bass.

You like:
- Subby kicks
- Clean separation
- No snare trickery riding the kicks

That puts you closer to **2000–2010 production** instead of modern “grid-snapped” deathcore.
