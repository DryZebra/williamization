Even if she didn’t *see* red or obvious black, **any stool that is dark, sticky, tar-like, or unusually blackish-brown** can sometimes indicate **melena** — digested blood from *higher up* in the GI tract.

Melena **is not always pitch black**. It can be:

- iron supplements  
- Pepto-Bismol
