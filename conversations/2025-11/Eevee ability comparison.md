Normally, in the Pokémon TCG, **Eevee** can already evolve into any of its evolutions as long as you have the corresponding card in your hand. What makes *this* Eevee ex’s ability (“**Veevee ’volve**”) special is that it **bypasses the evolution restrictions** that usually apply:  

- Normally, a Pokémon can’t evolve on **the same turn it’s played** or **the first turn of the game**.  
- This ability specifically allows you to evolve *from your hand* into *any* Pokémon that evolves from Eevee, ignoring the usual “correct stage” restriction — so it can instantly become Jolteon, Vaporeon, Sylveon, etc.  
- However, it still can’t evolve on the turn it’s played or the first turn of the game (that’s in the fine print).  

So it’s not changing the *basic rules*, but it’s a **special rule shortcut** — a built-in “universal Eevee evolution stone,” so to speak. It saves you from needing other effects (like Evolution Incense or rare stones) to get your chosen evolution.
