### *(The character hidden in the Bible)*

Across all books, 4:21 speaks about **five repeating motifs:**

1. **A call** – someone is summoned, commanded, or addressed.  
   (Ex 4:21, Josh 4:21, Matt 4:21)

2. **A threshold** – someone stands on the brink of action, a journey, or punishment.  
   (Deut 4:21, Neh 4:21, Acts 4:21)

3. **A task or labor** – work that must be done, often hard work, often holy.  
   (Lev 4:21, Neh 4:21, 2 Chr 4:21)

4. **A contradiction** – threat vs promise, violence vs blessing, law vs love.  
   (Judg 4:21, 1 Cor 4:21, Lam 4:21)

5. **A turning** – a moment pivoting from old order to new order.  
   (Luke 4:21, John 4:21, Romans 4:21)

It is *not* a gentle personality.
