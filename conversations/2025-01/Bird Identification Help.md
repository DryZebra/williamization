## Assistant

From the rufous‐tinted tail (distinctly warmer in color than the back) and the bold, rounded spotting on the breast, this appears to be a Hermit Thrush. Unlike other similar brown thrushes, Hermit Thrushes typically show that contrasting rusty tail and a more grayish‐brown back, which is quite noticeable in your pictures.
