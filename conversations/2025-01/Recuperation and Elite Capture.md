# Recuperation and Elite Capture

## User

## Assistant



## Assistant

What you're describing is **"recuperation."** Recuperation is when a radical or oppositional idea is co-opted by the mainstream, often by the ruling class, and repackaged in a way that neutralizes its revolutionary or critical potential. This process allows those in power to appear as if they are addressing the issue while actually maintaining the status quo.

For example, when billionaire black capitalists talk about black oppression, they shift the narrative from class struggle and systemic issues to individual success stories, reinforcing capitalism rather than challenging its role in oppression. This waters down the radical critique and makes the movement more palatable to the establishment.

Another related concept is **"elite capture,"** where discussions of oppression or justice are dominated by privileged individuals from within the marginalized group, shifting the focus toward their experiences rather than the material conditions of the majority.
