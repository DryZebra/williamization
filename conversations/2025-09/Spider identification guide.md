This is a **barn spider** (*Neoscona crucifera*), a type of orb-weaver.  

A few ID points from your photos:  
- Large, round abdomen with a slightly pointed end.  
- Reddish-brown to tan coloration with dense hairs.  
- Distinctive darker triangular/arrow-like markings on the top of the abdomen.  
- Long, spiny legs that are banded in reddish-brown and lighter tones.  
- Building a classic orb-shaped web in the open, which is typical of orb-weavers.  

These spiders are **very common in North Carolina** (and across the eastern and central US). They rebuild their big webs nightly and sit in the center after dark, retreating to a safe spot in the day.  

💡 Good news: they’re **harmless to people**, not aggressive, and help control insect populations.
