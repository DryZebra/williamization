Here’s where you stand, brother:  

The **CBA gives the employer the right to enforce the owner’s rules**, so long as those rules aren’t inconsistent with the agreement. That means Meta (the owner) or the contractor can say “no stickers, no political displays” and the foreman can be ordered to remove them【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L1-L5】【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L94-L102】.  

But the CBA also protects you:  

- **Grievances** – Any dispute has to be raised in writing within **15 days** or it “no longer exists”【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L80-L88】.  
- **Stewards** – The union has the right to appoint a steward on every job, and the steward can enforce the contract without retaliation【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L185-L192】.  
- **Union Discipline** – The union has the right to discipline its own members, not the employer, for breaking union rules【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L177-L180】.  

So, what’s next?  
1. If you or your crew want to fight this, you file a grievance within 15 days.  
2. That grievance will move to the **Labor-Management Committee** if it can’t be solved right away.  
3. If the Committee deadlocks, it can go all the way up to the **Council on Industrial Relations**, and their ruling is final【10†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L68-L76】.  

In plain talk: stickers and political messages aren’t protected under the CBA. But if you think the removal was handled in a way that violates the contract—say harassment, discrimination, or selective enforcement—you have the right to push it up through the grievance process.

Got you. Here’s the **grievance process, step-by-step**, plain and tight:

1) **Tell the steward + write it down now.**  
   You’ve got **15 days** from when it happened to file it *in writing*, or it’s dead. fileciteturn0file2L49-L53

2) **File the written grievance to both sides.**  
   Hand/email it to the **employer’s rep** (PM/HR or GF’s boss) and your **Local 553** rep. The contract says the two sides’ reps try to fix it first. fileciteturn0file2L19-L29

3) **48-hour try-to-fix window.**  
   If the two reps **can’t resolve it in 48 hours**, it **goes to the Labor-Management Committee (LMC)**. fileciteturn0file2L19-L29

4) **Labor-Management Committee meets fast.**  
   LMC is **3 union + 3 management** and can meet **within 48 hours** when noticed. They vote; **majority wins**. fileciteturn0file2L12-L17 fileciteturn0file2L31-L35

5) **If LMC deadlocks → CIR.**  
   If they **fail to agree**, it goes to the **Council on Industrial Relations (CIR)**. **CIR is final and binding.** fileciteturn0file2L37-L40

6) **Status quo while it’s fought.**  
   While it’s in dispute/arbitration, **don’t change conditions** until there’s a deal or ruling. Also, **no strikes/lockouts** over it—use the procedure. fileciteturn0file3L1-L4 fileciteturn0file2L6-L8

7) **Steward protection + access.**  
   Steward has the **right to enforce the CBA** on the clock and **can’t be punished** for doing it; union reps get **job access**. fileciteturn0file6L24-L31 fileciteturn0file6L35-L37

8) **Know the employer’s authority (and the limits).**  
   Management can **enforce owner rules** if **not inconsistent with the CBA** (e.g., a “no stickers/political displays” rule). If it’s **harassing or selectively enforced**, that’s where your grievance can bite. fileciteturn0file3L41-L51 fileciteturn0file14L49-L51

### What to include in your written grievance
- **Who/when/where:** date, time, location, names/witnesses.  
- **What happened:** short, factual.  
- **Contract hooks:**  
  - Management rights cited to **show the limit**: rules must **not be inconsistent** with the CBA. fileciteturn0file3L41-L51  
  - **Harassment-free workplace** principle (if conduct was harassing/intimidating). fileciteturn0file14L49-L51  
  - **Steward/union access** if you were blocked from representation. fileciteturn0file6L24-L31 fileciteturn0file6L35-L37  
- **Remedy you want:** e.g., stop the selective enforcement/harassment, train foremen on policy, remove discipline, post notice.
