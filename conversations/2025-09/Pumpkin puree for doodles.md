# Pumpkin puree for doodles

## User

How much pure pumpkin puree is good to give to a golden doodle?

## Assistant



## Assistant

For dogs like a golden doodle, plain **100% pure pumpkin puree** (not pumpkin pie filling) is generally safe and even helpful for digestion in small amounts. The right amount depends on your doodle’s size:  

- **Small dogs (under 15 lbs):** about 1 teaspoon per meal.  
- **Medium dogs (15–35 lbs):** 1–2 teaspoons per meal.  
- **Large dogs (35–60 lbs):** 1 tablespoon per meal.  
- **Extra-large dogs (60+ lbs):** up to 2–3 tablespoons per meal.  

A golden doodle can range from medium to large depending on breeding, so most fall in the **1 tablespoon to 2 tablespoons per meal** range.  

### Tips
- Start with the lower end (1 tbsp) and see how your doodle reacts.  
- Too much pumpkin can cause loose stools or nutrient imbalance.  
- Make sure it’s just pumpkin puree—no added sugar, spices, or xylitol (toxic to dogs).  
- If you’re using it for digestive issues (constipation or diarrhea), use it for a few days and then taper off once things normalize.
