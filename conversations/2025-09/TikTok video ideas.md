# TikTok video ideas

## Assistant

Since Veo 3 only gives you 8 seconds per clip, the best approach is to think in **micro-parables**—short, striking statements or images that hit like a spark. You can then chain them together as a series (like a playlist) or post them one after another. Here are a few directions we could try:

---

### 1. **The Worker as Christ**
- Visual: Hands gripping a hammer, then cut to bread being broken.
- Line: *“Every worker bears His image. Every nail struck, every loaf broken—through Him.”*

---

### 2. **Communism in Scripture**
- Visual: Wheat fields bending in the wind → people sharing bread at a table.
- Line: *“They had all things in common, and no one among them lacked.” (Acts 4:32)*

---

### 3. **The Ten Commandments as History**
- Visual: Stone tablet → classroom chalkboard → skyscraper.
- Line: *“Every law since is built on this foundation. That’s why they still matter.”*

---

### 4. **Cross vs. Flag**
- Visual: Cross shining behind dark rainbow banners fading.
- Line: *“An identity lasts a lifetime. The Cross lasts eternity.”*

---

### 5. **Invitation**
- Visual: A worker wiping sweat, then looking up toward light.
- Line: *“Don’t be ashamed of your labor. Christ Himself worked with His hands.”*

---

We could script each one tightly for 8 seconds, then test which one hooks best on TikTok.  

Do you want me to draft **storyboard-style scripts** for a set of 3–5 videos (camera cuts, timing, captions), or would you rather start with **one strong concept** and see how it plays in Veo 3?
