## Implications for how different human & chimp genomes really are

Because of these improved data, some long-held claims are being revised:

- The oft‐cited statistic that humans and chimps differ by only ~1% is based on the alignable, simpler portions of genomes, ignoring many structural, repetitive, or divergent regions. citeturn0search2turn0search15turn0search11turn0search7
