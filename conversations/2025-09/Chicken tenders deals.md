# DIY “crunch factory” (fast method)
1. **Marinate** raw tenders 2–24h in buttermilk + hot sauce + 1 tsp salt per lb.  
2. **Dredge** in seasoned flour (2 c flour + 2 tsp salt + 2 tsp paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp pepper).  
3. **Double dip** (back into buttermilk, then flour) for max craggy bits.  
4. **Air fry** 400°F 10–14 min (spritz with oil), or **fry** 350°F ~4–5 min to 165°F.  
Batch and freeze cooked tenders; re-crisp in air fryer.
