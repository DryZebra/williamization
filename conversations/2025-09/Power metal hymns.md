Probably the most explicit. Their whole aesthetic is “metal mass.”

### **Sabaton**  
- **“The Carolean’s Prayer (Karolinens Bön)”** is literally a hymn of Swedish soldiers praying before battle. It was recorded in both Swedish and English, with the structure and solemnity of a sung prayer.  

### **HammerFall**  
- Songs like **“Glory to the Brave”** and **“Templars of Steel”** are written as crusader hymns — slow, reverent, anthem-like, often performed with crowd sing-alongs that feel like hymnals.  

### **Avantasia / Edguy (Tobias Sammet’s projects)**  
- Tobias often writes pieces with explicit liturgical structure. **“Sign of the Cross”** (Avantasia) is a full choir hymn, opening their first metal opera like a church chant.  

### **Virgin Steele**  
- A bit older, but David DeFeis often blended classical/orchestral structures with hymn-like passages, especially in their concept albums (*The House of Atreus*).
