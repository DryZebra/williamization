To Whom It Concerns,  

My name is Ezra Byrd. I am a husband, an electrician, and a Republican candidate for Orange County Commissioner, District 2. In solidarity and faith,  
Ezra Byrd  
Republican Candidate, Orange County Commissioner District 2

# **Draft Labor Endorsement Pitch Letter**

To Whom It Concerns,  

My name is Ezra Byrd. I am an electrician, the president of IBEW Local 553, and a Republican candidate for Orange County Commissioner, District 2. I am writing to you because I believe in the values you defend — the dignity of working people — and because I believe it is time for a Republican to stand openly, without apology, as the candidate of labor.  

I am not a career politician. I am on the job every day. I have stood as a steward and now as president of my Local, fighting to make sure that no matter a worker’s background, every man and woman who puts in a day’s work deserves respect, dignity, and a fair return for their labor.  

I believe, as Lincoln said, that *“Labor is prior to, and independent of, capital. Capital is only the fruit of labor, and could never have existed if labor had not first existed.”* That principle is why I am running. Because too often our work is treated like a disposable commodity — underbid, outsourced, or stripped of its value. Capital has taken its place above labor, and the result has been the hollowing out of our communities.  

Orange County deserves a commissioner who will not bend to corporate pressure or chase headlines, but who will fight to make sure county contracts, county services, and county investments serve the people who actually live and work here. My campaign is about putting labor back where it belongs — at the center of public life.  

I am asking for your endorsement because I want to build a new precedent: that Republicans, too, can and must stand with labor. If we truly believe that all productive work has dignity, then our politics should reflect that truth. Together we can defend wages, strengthen apprenticeship programs, and make sure that the working class of this county has a seat at the table.  

This election is a chance to show that labor’s power is not tied to one party’s fortunes. It belongs to us, the workers. I am ready to carry that banner in Orange County. Stand with me, and we will not just win a seat — we will prove that the voice of labor cannot be ignored.  

In solidarity,  
Ezra Byrd  
President, IBEW Local 553  
Republican Candidate, Orange County Commissioner District 2  

---

This letter:  
- Leads with your **union role and credibility**.  
- Stays focused on **labor, dignity, solidarity, contracts, wages**.  
- Uses Lincoln to anchor your argument.  
- Never mentions family/guns/abortion — keeps them out of play.  
- Closes on a call to endorse you as the **labor candidate**, not the “Republican candidate.”
