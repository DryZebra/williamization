## Resetting / Reprogramming

- To **factory reset** the remote (clearing all codes/learned functions):

## Auto-Code Search (no numbers)

1. **Turn on** the device you want to control (TV, soundbar, etc.).  
2. **Press and hold** the **Setup** button until the LED blinks twice (or stays on).  
3. Press the **Device button** (TV, AUDIO, STREAM, etc.) for the type of device. The LED will stay lit.  
4. Point the remote at the device and **press the Power button** repeatedly (or sometimes **OK/Play** on streamer models).  
   - Each press sends a different code from the library.  
   - When the device turns off (or responds), stop pressing.  
5. **Lock in the code**: Press the **Device button** again to store it. The LED will blink twice.
