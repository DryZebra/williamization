# Thumb pain diagnosis suggestions

## User


I hit my right on my thumb bone almost a month ago and it hasn't stopped giving me this weird sharp pain every once in a while especially if I put pressure on it. Any ideas what's going on under there?

## Assistant



## Assistant

I’m not a doctor, but I can give you some perspective. Since you hit the base of your thumb about a month ago and are still feeling sharp pain when pressing on it, a few possibilities come to mind:

**1. Bone injury (fracture or hairline fracture):**  
Even if you can still move your thumb, small fractures can go unnoticed at first. They don’t always bruise heavily but can cause intermittent sharp pain—especially when pressure is applied.

**2. Ligament or tendon injury (sprain/strain):**  
The thumb joint is supported by ligaments (especially the ulnar collateral ligament at the base). If you overstretched or tore something, it can cause lingering sharp pain, especially with gripping or pressure.

**3. Joint or cartilage injury:**  
A hard impact can bruise the joint surface or damage cartilage, which can make the area sensitive for weeks.

**4. Deep bruise (bone contusion):**  
These take longer to heal than surface bruises and can stay painful for a month or more.

---

### What you should look out for:
- **Persistent swelling, bruising, or deformity** → possible fracture.  
- **Pain when gripping or pinching** → could be ligament/tendon damage.  
- **Instability (feeling like the thumb could "give out")** → often a ligament injury.  
- **Pain not improving after 4+ weeks** → means it’s worth getting checked.

---

### Next steps you might consider:
- **See a doctor (orthopedic/urgent care)**: An X-ray (or MRI if soft tissue suspected) can rule out a fracture or ligament tear.  
- **Support**: A thumb brace/splint can help stabilize while it heals.  
- **Rest & ice** if it flares up.  
- **Avoid forceful gripping/pressing** until you know what’s going on.
