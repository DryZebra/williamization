What you’ve found is the wrapper from a **Lidocaine Patch 5%** (a prescription topical anesthetic).  

Here’s what matters:

### 1. What it is  
- **Lidocaine** is used for pain relief, usually for nerve pain or local irritation.  
- The 5% patch is **prescription-only** in the U.S. (lower-strength creams and gels can be over the counter).  

### 2. Legal implications  
- Simply finding the wrapper in a porta-john is **not illegal** for you.  
- The person who used it may or may not have had a prescription. Misuse would be their issue, not yours.  

### 3. Safety  
- Used patches can still contain residual lidocaine, which could be harmful if handled by kids or pets. That’s why the instructions say to fold and discard carefully.  
- If the actual patch (not just the wrapper) was left around, it should be disposed of safely.  

### 4. Reporting?  
- **Not typically necessary** to report just a discarded wrapper, unless:  
  - You find many of them (suggesting possible drug misuse).  
  - The used patch itself is present and poses a hazard.  
  - You’re in a workplace, school, or public facility where reporting medical waste is required by policy.

- Don’t touch it with bare hands.
