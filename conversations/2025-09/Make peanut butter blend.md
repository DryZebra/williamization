# Make peanut butter blend

## User

How can I make peanut butter from peanuts and a blender?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Here’s a simple way to make peanut butter at home with just peanuts and a blender:  

---

### Ingredients
- 2 cups of roasted peanuts (unsalted is best so you can control flavor)  
- Optional: pinch of salt, 1–2 tsp honey or sugar, 1–2 tsp oil (peanut, canola, or vegetable) for creaminess  

---

### Steps
1. **Roast (if raw):**  
   If you have raw peanuts, roast them in the oven at 350°F (175°C) for 10–15 minutes until golden and fragrant. Let them cool slightly.

2. **Blend peanuts:**  
   Put the peanuts into the blender. Start on low speed, then increase. At first, they’ll turn into a coarse powder, then a clump, and finally a smooth butter as the oils release. This may take 3–6 minutes depending on your blender.

3. **Scrape sides:**  
   Stop occasionally to scrape down the sides so everything blends evenly. Be patient—this is normal.

4. **Adjust texture:**  
   - For **chunky peanut butter:** Remove a few spoonfuls of crushed peanuts early on, then stir them back in at the end.  
   - For **creamy peanut butter:** Keep blending longer, and if it feels too thick, add a small drizzle of oil until it spreads easily.

5. **Flavor (optional):**  
   Blend in a pinch of salt, a little honey or sugar for sweetness, or cinnamon for a twist.

6. **Store:**  
   Transfer to a clean jar. Keep in the fridge up to 3–4 weeks. Natural peanut butter will separate over time—just stir it before using.

---

👉 Tip: If your blender struggles, do smaller batches or pulse in short bursts to avoid overheating.
