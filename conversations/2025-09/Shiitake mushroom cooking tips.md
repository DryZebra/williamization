That’s a **shiitake mushroom** (Lentinula edodes). You can tell by the dark brown, scaly cap and the light-colored gills underneath. These are very common in grocery stores, so if that’s where you got it, you’re safe—it’s not a wild mushroom.

### Best Ways to Cook Shiitake
Shiitakes have a meaty, savory flavor (umami), especially when cooked. A few good methods:

- **Sautéing**: Slice and cook in butter or oil with garlic, soy sauce, or a splash of mirin. They brown nicely and concentrate in flavor.
- **Soups & Broths**: Add fresh or dried shiitakes to miso soup, ramen, or chicken broth for depth.
- **Stir-Fries**: Great with vegetables, noodles, or rice dishes. They soak up sauces well.
- **Roasting**: Toss with oil, salt, and pepper, then roast at ~400°F until crispy on the edges.
- **Grilling**: Marinate caps in soy sauce, sesame oil, and ginger, then grill whole for a smoky flavor.

👉 Important tip: The stems are edible but very tough. Most people remove them and use them for broth or discard them. The caps are the tender, flavorful part.

### Quick Garlic Soy Shiitake
**Ingredients**  
- Shiitake mushrooms (caps, sliced)  
- 1–2 tsp oil (any kind)  
- ½ tsp garlic powder (or more if you like)  
- 1–2 tsp soy sauce  

**Steps**  
1. Heat a pan on medium-high with a little oil.  
2. Add sliced shiitake caps (don’t overcrowd — let them touch the pan).  
3. Cook undisturbed for 2–3 minutes until they brown.  
4. Stir, sprinkle garlic powder, and keep cooking until tender (another 2–3 minutes).  
5. Splash in soy sauce, stir quickly so it soaks in, then remove from heat.  

You’ll end up with savory, garlicky, slightly chewy mushrooms that taste almost meaty.

For shiitake mushrooms, a good slice size depends on how you plan to use them:  

- **For stir-fries or quick sautés** → Slice them about **¼ inch thick** (roughly the thickness of two stacked quarters). This way, they cook evenly, get tender, and soak up the soy sauce.  
- **For soups or broths** → You can go a little thinner, about **⅛ inch**, so they soften quickly and release flavor.  
- **For roasting or grilling** → Keep them a bit thicker, about **⅓ inch**, so they don’t dry out.

### Garlic Soy Shiitake  
**Ingredients**  
- Shiitake mushrooms (sliced about ¼ inch thick)  
- 1–2 tsp oil (olive, vegetable, or whatever you have)  
- 1 tsp minced garlic (from your jar)  
- 1–2 tsp soy sauce  

**Steps**  
1. Heat a pan on medium-high with oil.  
2. Add the sliced shiitake caps in a single layer. Let them sear 2–3 minutes without stirring so they brown.  
3. Stir, then add the minced garlic. Cook about 30 seconds until fragrant.  
4. Splash in soy sauce, stir quickly so it coats the mushrooms, and remove from heat.  

That’s it — you’ll get tender, savory, garlicky mushrooms with a meaty bite.
