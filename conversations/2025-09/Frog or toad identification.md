That’s not a frog but a **toad** — specifically an **American Toad** (*Anaxyrus americanus*).  

Here’s how you can tell:  
- **Dry, bumpy skin** instead of smooth moist frog skin.  
- **Large parotoid glands** (the oval swellings behind the eyes) that produce defensive toxins.  
- **Short legs** compared to frogs, built more for hopping than long jumps.  
- **Brown/tan coloration with dark spots**, which helps them blend in with dirt and leaf litter.  

They’re very common across North Carolina (where you’re at) and are beneficial to have around since they eat tons of insects.
