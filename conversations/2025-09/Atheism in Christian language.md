---

### 1. God hides and reveals through history
- **Proverbs 25:2** — *“It is the glory of God to conceal a thing: but the honour of kings is to search out a matter.”*  
This shows that truth is not always declared, but discovered. From a materialist Christian lens, atheism isn’t rejecting God—it’s taking seriously the honor of searching out what He has hidden in creation.

- **Mark 9:38–41** — When the disciples tried to stop someone casting out demons in Jesus’ name, Jesus said:  
  *“Forbid him not: for there is no man which shall do a miracle in my name, that can lightly speak evil of me. For he that is not against us is on our part. … He shall not lose his reward.”*  
This can show that even those “outside” the fold, working from conscience and truth, are not rejected.

---

### 3. God’s Spirit moves even in those who don’t know Him
- **Romans 2:14–15** — *“For when the Gentiles, which have not the law, do by nature the things contained in the law … these, having not the law, are a law unto themselves: Which shew the work of the law written in their hearts.”*  
Here Paul affirms that people can live rightly without explicit recognition of God’s law, because the truth is etched in their being.

---

### 4. Seeking truth is seeking Christ
- **John 14:6** — *“I am the way, the truth, and the life.”*  
If Christ *is* truth, then every sincere search for truth—even through dialectical and historical materialism—is a search for Him. That lets you say, in Christian language: *“I love Jesus because I love truth. My atheism is not a rejection—it’s an assumption that truth is real, that the universe can be understood, and that history reveals God’s motion.”*

---

### 5. Faith and unbelief are both part of God’s plan
- **Romans 11:32** — *“For God hath concluded them all in unbelief, that he might have mercy upon all.”*
