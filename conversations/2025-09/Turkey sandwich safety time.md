## Assistant

A turkey sandwich is considered **perishable**, so food safety rules apply.  

- The USDA and FDA recommend the **“2-hour rule”**: perishable food (like sandwiches with meat, cheese, or mayo) should not sit out at room temperature (above 40°F / 4°C) for more than 2 hours.  
- If the food is in **direct sun or temperatures above 90°F (32°C)**, the safe window drops to **1 hour**.  

After this, bacteria can grow to unsafe levels, even if the sandwich still looks or smells fine.  

👉 So, if your turkey sandwich has been sitting in the sun:  
- **Max 1 hour** if it’s hot out (≥90°F / 32°C).  
- **Max 2 hours** if it’s cooler (under 90°F / 32°C).
