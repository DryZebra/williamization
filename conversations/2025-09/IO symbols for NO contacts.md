# IO symbols for NO contacts

With IO symbols which correspond to normally open?

## Assistant

In industrial control and electrical drawings, **IO symbols for contacts** are standardized. Here’s a quick guide:

### Normally Open (NO) Symbols
- **Two parallel lines not touching**: `|  |`  
  This is the most common symbol for a NO contact. It means the circuit is open until the device actuates.  
- Sometimes drawn as a **slash or angle breaking between two lines**.  

### Normally Closed (NC) Symbols
- **Two parallel lines with a diagonal slash through them**: `|/|`  
  The slash indicates that the contact is closed in its resting state and will open when actuated.

### Mnemonic to Remember
- **NO = "open gap"** → no line crossing = open until pressed/triggered.  
- **NC = "slash closed"** → the slash shows it’s closed by default, and only opens on actuation.
