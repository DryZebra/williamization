## What to Think About / Requirements

To pull ~200 lbs of trash + the trailer weight + you (if riding), you’ll want something with enough torque, decent load capacity, good hills traction, and strong build. Key parameters:

| Spec | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Motor Power / Torque | You need pulling power, especially if there are slopes or rough terrain. Even modest hills can kill an underpowered drive. |
| Battery & Range | Electric is nice; but hauling extra weight drains battery faster. If you go electric, more Ah or a spare battery helps. |
| Weight Capacity | Between your body, the trailer, the load — make sure the scooter or vehicle supports that. |
| Towing Hitch / Tongue Strength | The point where trailer connects must be strong and properly designed. |
| Stability | Wide wheelbase helps. Three-wheel small scooters might tip more with a load. Larger tires or dual rear wheels help. |
| Off-road vs. smooth path | If the road to the dump is rough, want something rugged. |

Also check legal / regulatory stuff depending on your area (roads, sidewalks, licensing, etc.).

---

## Types of Options

Here are some ideas for what kinds of vehicles might work:

- **Heavy-duty utility tow scooter / tugger** — made for hauling / towing stuff. More expensive but built for this kind of job.  
- **Small ATV / UTV / “yard-buggy”** — if you’re okay with gasoline, something like a small four-wheeler + trailer.  
- **Electric golf cart / utility cart** — these often have decent towing ability, especially off pavement.  
- **Electric mobility scooters** (if modified for towing) — maybe marginal unless very beefy.  
- **DIY solution**: e-bike + trailer, small powered cart, etc., if your loads aren’t always full.
