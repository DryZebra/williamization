# Payment options without card

## User

My girlfriend's debit card expired and she hasn't gotten a new one in the mail yet. She also can't transfer her money into cash app. What can she use? PayPal?

## Assistant



## Assistant

If her debit card expired and she hasn’t gotten a replacement yet, her options depend on what accounts she already has set up:  

### 1. **Direct Bank Access**
- She can still **use her bank account directly** (through online banking or mobile app) for transfers, bill pay, or Zelle if her bank supports it.  
- Many banks let you generate a **virtual debit card** in-app that works online until the physical one arrives.

### 2. **PayPal**
- Yes, PayPal can link directly to a **bank account** (via routing/account number) without needing a debit card.  
- She can transfer money into PayPal, then either spend it online where PayPal is accepted or transfer it out once she gets her new card.  

### 3. **Cash App / Venmo Alternatives**
- **Cash App** usually requires a debit card for instant transfers, but she can still link her **bank account** directly (though transfers take longer).  
- **Venmo** works similarly—she can connect her bank account directly instead of a card.  

### 4. **Stopgaps**
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** sometimes allow linking directly to a bank account, depending on the bank.  
- If she needs cash, she can make an **in-person withdrawal at a bank branch** with her ID.
