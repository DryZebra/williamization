# Rebuilding identity documents

## User

Alright here's the situation. I have experienced a serious accident and I have nothing but my wallet left. Driver's license and cards and stuff but SSN card gone, passport gone, birth certificate gone. What is my very first step to getting my life back together?

## Assistant



## Assistant

The very first step is to **re-establish your core identity documents**. Since you still have your driver’s license and wallet, that’s a huge help—you’ve got a valid piece of photo ID. Here’s the order I’d recommend:  

---

## 1. Replace Your Social Security Card
- Go to the [Social Security Administration (SSA) website](https://www.ssa.gov/ssnumber/) or your local SSA office.  
- You can usually request a replacement card online if you have a driver’s license.  
- If not, you’ll need to fill out **Form SS-5** and provide ID (your license is enough).  

Why first? Your SSN is needed for jobs, banking, taxes, and often for replacing other documents.

---

## 2. Order a New Birth Certificate
- Contact the **vital records office** in the state where you were born.  
- Most states let you request it online, by mail, or in person.  
- They’ll usually ask for a copy of your ID and SSN (or just ID if SSN isn’t available yet).  

This is your foundation document—you’ll often need it for a passport or other replacements.

---

## 3. Apply for a New Passport
- Once you have your birth certificate and SSN card, you can apply at a **passport acceptance facility** (post offices, courthouses, etc.).  
- Use **Form DS-11** (first-time applicants, since your old passport is lost).  
- Bring your driver’s license, birth certificate, passport photo, and SSN.  

This gives you a second form of high-level federal ID.

---

## 4. Secure Your Finances
- Contact your banks and credit card companies to report your lost cards if you haven’t already.  
- Request replacements right away.  
- Consider putting a **fraud alert** or **credit freeze** on your credit reports since your SSN and passport are gone.  

---

## 5. Long-Term Stability Steps
- Make digital and physical copies of all replacements once they arrive.  
- Store originals in a fireproof safe and scans in encrypted cloud storage.  
- If you travel, carry only what’s needed (keep backups safe at home).

Not yet. You’ll need to wait until your **birth certificate** and **SSN card** actually arrive before you can submit a passport application.  

Here’s why:  

- **Form DS-11** (the application for a new passport after reporting one lost) requires you to provide **proof of U.S. citizenship** (usually a certified birth certificate) **and** a valid government-issued photo ID (like your driver’s license).  
- You must also write your **SSN** on the form, and they sometimes ask for proof (the card or a W-2/tax document).  
- Since you reported your passport lost, the old one can’t be used for proof anymore—so the birth certificate is essential.  

---

✅ Fill out **Form DS-11** online and print it (but don’t sign yet—you sign at the acceptance facility).  
✅ Gather:
- Driver’s license (proof of identity).  
- Passport photo (2x2, taken within 6 months).  
- Proof of citizenship (**wait until your birth certificate arrives**).  
- Your new SSN card (once it arrives).  

✅ Locate your nearest **passport acceptance facility** (often a post office or courthouse).  

---
