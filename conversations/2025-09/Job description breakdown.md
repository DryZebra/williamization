## ✅ Why it’s rare

Here are reasons why you might not see many:

- **IBEW’s jurisdiction is usually “inside wire / field work”** — electricians, installers, etc. Office/design/coordination roles often aren’t under union contracts, or if they are, it's less obvious.  
- **Preconstruction coordination can be judged as non-trade work** — sometimes seen as “administrative / engineering support” rather than union trade scope. Unions tend to cover the physical installation/field work more heavily.  
- **Remote work is newer** — many union contracts haven’t caught up to remote flexibility. So remote preconstruction roles may not be under those contracts.
