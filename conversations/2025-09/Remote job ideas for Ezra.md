3. **The Money:** A bank collapse wipes out digits in accounts. Were they ever “real”? Why did they move people?  
4. **The Marriage:** Divorce papers are filed. Was the marriage fake? Or does its weight remain?  
5. **The Law:** Segregation laws overturned. Were they “fake” once gone, or were they always real in their effect?  
6. **The Flag:** A piece of cloth is burned. Why do people fight wars over it?
