“Superfunnels” sound slick, but the logic is the same as those old “downline” scams:

- **Funnels within funnels** just stack the labor. Each person is told, *“don’t worry, you don’t have to build your own, just plug into mine.”*  
- But if you plug into theirs, then your *only* way to make money is to convince someone else to plug into yours.  
- That’s a pyramid in everything but name. The “product” (funnel templates, certifications, access) is just the excuse.  

And you nailed the dialectic:  

- At the **top**, they collect passively, presenting themselves as gurus.  
- At the **bottom**, people are hustling, spending on ads, paying fees, chasing leads, and when they finally realize the margins don’t work, they drop out.  
- The scheme depends on constant *churn*—new hopefuls replacing the disillusioned.  

That’s why they dress it up in new language: “super funnels,” “affiliate clouds,” “lifetime commissions.” Each generation of scam invents shinier words to hide the same exploit.
