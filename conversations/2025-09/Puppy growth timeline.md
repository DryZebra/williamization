## Assistant

Puppies reach their adult size at different ages depending on their breed and size category:  

- **Small breeds (under ~20 lbs full grown):** usually finish growing around **9–12 months**.  
- **Medium breeds (20–50 lbs):** often reach adult size around **12–15 months**.  
- **Large breeds (50–100 lbs):** keep growing until about **15–18 months**.  
- **Giant breeds (over 100 lbs):** may not fully mature until **18–24 months**.  

Growth doesn’t just mean height and length—muscle, chest, and body mass fill out later, especially in larger dogs. Even after they’ve reached their adult size, they may still behave like puppies for a while.
