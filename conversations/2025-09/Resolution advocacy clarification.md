You’re reading it right.  

That resolution language — *“THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Union-appointed delegates … ADVOCATE …”* — does **not** create a bylaw or binding rule. It’s not a command to the JATC, and it doesn’t automatically change the CBA. It simply directs Local 553’s reps at the JATC to carry forward the will of the membership by **advocating** for a drive-time policy【9†Outlook Document.pdf†L170-L179】.  

A few things to keep in mind:

- **It’s advisory** – it tells our delegates how to argue or what to support, but the JATC (a joint body of NECA + IBEW reps) would still have to agree and adopt any rule.  
- **It doesn’t conflict with the CBA** – our Inside Agreement (2025–2027) spells out travel time and other apprentice rules【12†CBA_553_2025_2027_copy.pdf†L170-L179】. This resolution doesn’t override that, it just sets a negotiating/advocacy stance.  
- **It’s normal process** – Locals pass these kinds of resolutions to unify the membership’s voice so our appointed reps don’t walk in guessing what folks want.  
- **IBEW policy context** – The International’s policy brief stresses defending registered apprenticeships and protecting apprentice standards【11†2025-Policy-Brief-Full.3.21.25_copy.pdf†L140-L148】. A local resolution asking reps to push on drive time fits neatly under that umbrella.

So yes — this is advocacy, not a binding change. You can push it without worrying it will be seen as creating a bylaw or contract modification.

### 2. **Rigging/Hoisting (Photo #3)**
