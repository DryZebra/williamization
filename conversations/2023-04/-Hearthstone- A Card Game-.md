# "Hearthstone: A Card Game"

## User

What do you know about the card game Hearthstone?

## Assistant

Hearthstone is a free-to-play digital collectible card game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. It was first released in 2014 for Microsoft Windows and macOS, and has since been made available on iOS and Android devices as well.

In Hearthstone, players use decks of cards featuring characters, creatures, and spells from the Warcraft universe to battle against other players online or against computer-controlled opponents. Players can acquire new cards by purchasing card packs with in-game gold or real money, or by completing various in-game quests and achievements.

The game features several modes of play, including Casual and Ranked matches, as well as several single-player adventures and expansions. In Ranked play, players earn stars for each win and advance through ranks to higher tiers. At the end of each month, players are rewarded with rewards based on the highest rank they achieved.

Hearthstone has a large and active community, with frequent updates and new expansions adding to the game's content. The game has also been featured in several esports tournaments, with professional players competing for large cash prizes.
