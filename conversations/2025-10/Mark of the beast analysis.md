> **καὶ ποιεῖ πάντας... δῶσιν αὐτοῖς χάραγμα ἐπὶ τῆς χειρὸς τῆς δεξιᾶς αὐτῶν ἢ ἐπὶ τὸ μέτωπον αὐτῶν, καὶ ἵνα μή τις δύνηται ἀγοράσαι ἢ πωλῆσαι εἰ μὴ ὁ ἔχων τὸ χάραγμα, τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ θηρίου ἢ τὸν ἀριθμὸν τοῦ ὀνόματος αὐτοῦ.**

### Step 1. Word-by-word Material Translation

- **χάραγμα (charagma)** – not “mark” generically, but *an imprint, engraving, or official stamp*. In the Greek manuscripts it always implies an **authorized symbol** (used for imperial seals or coin dies).  
- **μέτωπον (metōpon)** – literally “between the eyes,” used also in **Revelation 7:3** where God’s servants are “sealed on their foreheads.” The “forehead” is the *visible seat of loyalty* — the public declaration of what one serves.  
- **ἐπὶ τῆς χειρὸς δεξιᾶς (on the right hand)** – denotes *action, work, or participation*; the right hand is the agent of labor and agreement.  

So materially, this describes **symbolic allegiance both in thought (forehead)** and **in deed (right hand)** to the imperial–economic system represented by “the beast.”

---

### Step 2. Socio-Historical Context

Revelation was written during Roman domination. Every merchant transaction involved a **“charagma”**—the emperor’s stamp—on coins, documents, or market tokens. Refusing to use them meant **economic exclusion**: “no one could buy or sell except he who had the mark.”  
Thus, materially, the mark is **participation in imperial economic worship** — loyalty to Caesar’s power expressed through the market.

The **beast** represents state power sanctified as divine; the **image** represents its ideological apparatus (imperial cult). The **mark** represents the *integration of conscience and labor* into that false divinity.

---

### Step 3. Internal Biblical Contrast

Revelation deliberately mirrors **Exodus 13:9** and **Deuteronomy 6:8**, where Israelites are told to bind God’s law *“as a sign on your hand and between your eyes.”*  
John inverts that: just as the faithful are sealed with God’s law, the faithless are sealed with the empire’s. The forehead and hand mark therefore aren’t futuristic microchips — they’re rival liturgies of labor and thought.

---

### Step 4. Materialist Reading of the Chapter’s Motion

**Revelation 13** dramatizes the totalization of empire:
1. Economic compulsion replaces faith with necessity.
2. The system demands *worship* (legitimacy).
3. Those who submit bear its “mark” through work and consent.
4. Those who resist are marginalized or killed.

Materially, this is about **the commodification of life**—the absorption of human labor (hand) and identity (forehead) into a political economy that deifies itself.

---

### Step 5. Materialist Translation (Plain English)

> “And it caused all—the small and the great, the rich and the poor, the free and the enslaved—to receive an *imprint of authority* upon their right hand or upon their forehead,  
> so that no one might trade or labor unless he bore the imprint: the name of power, or the number of its name.”

---

### Dialectical Meaning

In dialectical motion, this “mark” is not prophecy of a future technology but an **eternal symbol of the union of ideology and economy**—the moment when belief and survival become inseparable under a ruling order.  
The “mark” appears whenever people must **profess faith in power to live within it**.  

So materially:  
- **Forehead = ideology** (thought shaped by ruling ideas)  
- **Hand = labor** (material participation in the system)  
- **Mark = social integration into a false totality**

Thus the passage is the *first Marxian image of alienation* in Christian scripture—the worker and thinker branded by empire for permission to exist.
