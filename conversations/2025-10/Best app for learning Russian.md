Most of the *best* Russian-as-a-foreign-language materials are either directly produced in Russia or based on methods developed there. The country treats language teaching as a real science (there’s an entire academic field, **РКИ — “Русский как иностранный”**, “Russian as a Foreign Language”).  

Here’s what actually *works* and is genuinely Russian in origin:  

---

### 🇷🇺 **1. Pushkin Institute (Пушкинский институт) — [pushkininstitute.ru](https://pushkininstitute.ru/?locale=en)**
**This is the official, government-funded Russian language institute.**  
They run a platform called **“Образование на русском”** (Education in Russian), which is basically Russia’s official online classroom for foreigners.  
You’ll find:
- Interactive alphabet training
- Gradual courses (A1–C2)
- Listening + speaking practice
- Certification recognized by universities

It’s free to register, and it’s the *real deal* — this is where many international teachers of Russian actually train.

---

### 🇷🇺 **2. Russian-Made Apps Built from РКИ Methodology**
These aren’t “state apps” per se but are based on Russian academic programs:
- **“Learn Russian with Russian” (by Stepik & Pushkin Institute)** – Hosted on [Stepik.org](https://stepik.org/course/74). It’s simple, free, and uses official grammar sequences from Pushkin.
- **“Полиглот 16 уроков” (Polyglot 16 Lessons)** – A TV-based crash course by Dmitry Petrov, very popular in Russia. You can find the full lessons for free on YouTube; each one is ~25 minutes, conversational, and surprisingly effective.
- **“RuLang”** and **“Russian Cases”** – smaller Android apps built by Russian teachers; useful drills for grammar, especially for case endings.

---

### 🇷🇺 **3. Russkiy Mir Foundation Centers**
If you ever travel or want to video-chat with real teachers, the **Russkiy Mir Foundation** maintains official cultural centers all over the world. They run hybrid courses, distribute Pushkin materials, and occasionally offer **free conversational clubs via Zoom**.
