---

### 🧪 Option 1: Fresh Ginger Syrup (Traditional)

**Ingredients (makes about 1 liter ginger ale):**
- 1 cup **fresh ginger**, peeled and thinly sliced  
- 1 cup **sugar** (white or mix with brown for a deeper flavor)  
- 1 cup **water**  
- 1 medium **lemon or lime** (juice only)  
- Pinch of **salt**  
- Optional: a few drops of **vanilla** or **molasses** for “old-fashioned” style

**Instructions:**
1. **Simmer the syrup:** Combine ginger, sugar, water, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, stirring until sugar dissolves.  
2. **Steep:** Once simmering, lower heat and cook gently for 10–15 minutes. Remove from heat and let it steep another 20–30 minutes for stronger flavor.  
3. **Strain:** Pour through a fine strainer or cheesecloth, pressing out the liquid.  
4. **Add lemon juice** (and vanilla/molasses if desired).  
5. **Chill** the syrup completely before mixing with carbonation.

**To serve:**  
- Add **2–3 tbsp syrup per 8 oz** (250 ml) of SodaStream sparkling water.  
- Stir gently—never carbonate with the syrup in! Only fizz the plain water, *then* mix.

---

### ⚡ Option 2: Instant Extract Version

If you want it faster:
- 1 tsp **ground ginger**  
- 1 tbsp **lemon juice**  
- 1 tbsp **honey** or sugar  
- 1 cup **sparkling water**  

Stir together the ginger, lemon, and sweetener first, then pour into chilled sparkling water. It won’t taste as clean or complex, but it’s a quick ginger-soda fix.

---

### 🧊 Tips
- Store syrup in the fridge up to **2 weeks**.  
- Add a dash of **bitters** or **cinnamon** for a “ginger beer” kick.  
- For a diet version, swap sugar for erythritol or stevia, but boil less to avoid bitterness.

## 🌿 Homemade Sugar-Free Ginger Ale (Fresh Root Version)

### **Ingredients (for about 1 liter batch)**  
- 2–3 inches of **fresh ginger root**, peeled and grated (about 2 tablespoons)  
- 1 liter **cold carbonated water** (carbonated *plain* in your SodaStream)  
- 1 ½ tablespoons **fresh lemon or lime juice**  
- ½ teaspoon **liquid stevia** *or* 2 tablespoons **erythritol / monk-fruit blend** *(adjust to taste)*  
- Pinch of **salt** (rounds out flavor)  
- Optional: ¼ teaspoon **vanilla extract** or a slice of **fresh mint** for a mellow edge  

---

### **Step-by-Step**

1. **Extract the ginger juice:**  
   - Grate your ginger finely and place it in a mug or small bowl.  
   - Pour about ¼ cup of **boiling water** over it and let steep 10 minutes.  
   - Strain through a fine mesh or cheesecloth — that’s your **ginger concentrate**.  

2. **Chill completely:**  
   - You never want to add anything warm to carbonated water; it’ll instantly defizz.  
   - Put the ginger concentrate in the fridge until cold.  

3. **Mix the drink:**  
   - Fill a glass or bottle halfway with **cold SodaStream water**.  
   - Add **½ oz (1 tablespoon)** of your ginger concentrate, lemon juice, sweetener, and a pinch of salt.  
   - Stir gently, then top off with more sparkling water. Adjust to taste.  

---

### **Optional: “Fermented Edge” Version (natural fizz + probiotics)**  
If you want a touch of natural yeast spice like real ginger beer, you can make a **ginger bug starter** once and reuse it:  

1. Combine 2 tbsp grated ginger, 2 tbsp sugar (the sugar gets eaten), and 1 cup water in a jar.  
2. Stir daily and feed a teaspoon of sugar and ginger for 3–4 days until bubbly.  
3. Strain ¼ cup of that liquid into a bottle of non-sweetened ginger brew before carbonation; the yeasts will eat residual sweetener and leave it nearly sugar-free but tangy.  

---

### **Tips**
- Fresh ginger can vary in heat — young roots are milder, older ones hotter.  
- Lemon brightens it; lime makes it sharper.  
- Keep concentrate refrigerated up to **one week**.  
- Always carbonate **plain water first**, then add flavor afterward.

## 🪶 Old-School Sugar-Free Ginger Ale (Frontier Style)

### **You’ll Need**
- 1 hand-sized piece of **fresh young ginger** (like yours)  
- 1 quart (about 1 L) **clean water**  
- 2 tablespoons **raw honey** or **molasses** *(optional: you can skip if you want 0 sugar — it’s mainly for body)*  
- Juice of **1 lemon or ½ lime**  
- A pinch of **salt**  
- Optional flavorings used historically:  
  - 1 clove  
  - a slice of cinnamon stick  
  - a few grains of allspice or black pepper  

---

### **How to Make the Concentrate**

1. **Scrub, don’t peel** your ginger — the skin carries aroma. Slice it thin across the grain.  
2. In a pot, add the ginger, optional spices, and water. Bring to a *gentle simmer* for 10–15 minutes.  
3. Turn off the heat and **let it steep** with the lid on for at least an hour. The longer you steep, the more fire you get.  
4. Strain through a cloth or fine sieve. Add lemon juice and salt while still warm.  
5. (Optional) Stir in honey or molasses *only if you want* a bit of natural sweetness — this is how settlers did it before cane sugar was easy to get.  
6. Chill completely. Store covered in the fridge. It’ll last about a week.  

---

### **To Serve**
- Carbonate a bottle of cold water in your SodaStream.  
- Add **2–3 tablespoons** of the chilled concentrate per cup of fizzy water.  
- Stir gently — don’t shake or carbonate the flavor directly.  

---

### **Flavor Notes**
- *Young ginger* like yours gives floral heat without the harsh burn of old root.  
- The salt and lemon balance the sharpness, making it taste round and crisp even without sugar.  
- A touch of honey or molasses makes it more “root beer” style if you want to share with others used to sweet soda.
