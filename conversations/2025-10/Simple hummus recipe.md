# Simple hummus recipe

Give me a simple hummus recipe I can make with canned chick peas

## Assistant

Here’s a simple, classic **hummus recipe** using canned chickpeas — no fancy tools or ingredients required.  

---

### 🧄 Ingredients
- 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) **chickpeas**, drained and rinsed  
- 2 tbsp **lemon juice** (about half a lemon)  
- 2 tbsp **tahini** (or substitute 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp peanut butter if needed)  
- 1 small **garlic clove**, minced  
- 2 tbsp **olive oil** (plus a drizzle for topping)  
- 2–3 tbsp **water** (to thin)  
- ½ tsp **salt**  
- ½ tsp **ground cumin** *(optional but recommended)*  
- Dash of **paprika** or **chili powder** for garnish  

---

### 🌀 Instructions
1. **Rinse chickpeas** under cold water and drain well.  
2. In a blender or food processor, combine:
   - chickpeas  
   - lemon juice  
   - tahini  
   - garlic  
   - olive oil  
   - salt and cumin  
3. Blend until thick and mostly smooth.  
4. **Add water gradually**, 1 tbsp at a time, until it reaches your preferred creamy texture.  
5. Taste and adjust:
   - More lemon juice for brightness  
   - More salt or cumin for flavor  
   - More olive oil for richness  
6. Serve in a bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with paprika or chili powder.  

---

### 🌿 Optional Upgrades
- Add a **pinch of baking soda** to the chickpeas and briefly microwave or boil to make them softer before blending (extra creamy texture).  
- Stir in **roasted red pepper**, **caramelized onion**, or **smoked paprika** for variation.  
- Top with a bit of **olive oil and chopped parsley** before serving.

7. **Serve** in a bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle paprika if you’ve got it.
