## What “always-on / hands-free / auto-answer” really means (and trade-offs)

Before picking a device, you need to consider a few constraints:

| Feature | Why it matters | Potential downside / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Full-duplex, hands-free** | So your mom can speak and listen without pushing a button (like a phone call) | More prone to feedback, echo, ambient noise interference |
| **Auto-answer / “monitor mode”** | If she can’t or forgets to press anything, the system should pick up automatically | Security / privacy — you may hear ambient sounds unintentionally |
| **Range / signal coverage** | You need coverage from all key locations (indoors, outbuildings, yard) | Walls, trees, interference weaken signal. The “claimed range” is usually in ideal open-line-of-sight conditions. |
| **Power & reliability** | It needs to stay powered and connected, with minimal maintenance | Battery-operated units may run out; WiFi dropouts, interference, or devices may get reset |
| **Ease of use** | For both of you — minimal setup, simple interface | If something gets mis-set (volume, code, channel), it may stop working when you need it most |

Given that your property already has good WiFi coverage, you have more flexibility (you could even use IP-intercoms over WiFi), but you’ll still want a hardware device (not just a phone app) for reliability and to avoid confusing your mom with extra steps.
