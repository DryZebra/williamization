Short answer: no mid-span strap is required.

Per NEC Article 344 (RMC):

- You must secure the RMC **within 3 ft of each conduit body** (344.30(A)).  
- RMC must be **supported at intervals not exceeding 10 ft** (344.30(B)).

So on a 6-ft run between two conduit bodies, put a strap (or other support) near each body (within 3 ft of the body). Because the total span is only 6 ft—well under the 10-ft max—**you don’t need an additional support between the two bodies**. (If framing prevents the 3-ft rule, there’s an allowance up to 5 ft, but that’s an exception.) citeturn0search0turn0search16

Note: Rules about using conduits to support a **conduit body** (314.23(E)) are separate from the raceway-strapping rules above; even if the bodies themselves are supported, you still need the RMC straps within the 3-ft requirement. citeturn0search5
