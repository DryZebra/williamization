## Assistant

There actually *is*, but it’s pretty light-touch — more “flavor” than true survival.  

Here’s how it works:  

### 🍖 Hunger (Core system)
Arthur has **three “Cores”** — Health, Stamina, and Dead Eye.  
- Eating refills those Cores, and different foods help different stats.  
- If you don’t eat for a long time, your Cores deplete faster, meaning your meters (the outer rings) refill slower.  
- Arthur gets visibly thinner when underfed and gains weight if you overeat.  
But — you’ll never die from starvation. It just makes you less efficient.

### 😴 Sleep
You can **sleep at camp or in hotels** to restore your cores and advance time.  
It’s not strictly necessary; you can guzzle coffee and canned beans forever if you want. But sleeping keeps your cores full and your appearance clean (Arthur’s beard, hair, and dirt levels also tie into it).

### 🧼 Other Survival Bits
- **Weather and Clothing:** Cold or hot weather drains your cores faster if you’re not dressed properly.  
- **Weapon Maintenance:** Guns degrade and need oiling.  
- **Horse Bonding:** Your horse can die permanently unless you reload or use horse revivers.

So yeah — *RDR2* has all the *pieces* of a survival system, but they’re soft penalties, not real threats.  
If you want *KCD2*-level realism — hunger that matters, navigation without GPS, slower combat — that’s where the PC modding scene turns *RDR2* into something special.
