# Simple fried rice recipe

## Assistant

Here’s a no-nonsense, restaurant-style fried rice recipe that uses simple ingredients and tastes like what you’d get at a good Chinese takeout spot. The key is heat, simplicity, and a little MSG.

---

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- 2 cups **day-old cooked rice** (cold, preferably jasmine or long grain)  
- 2 tbsp **neutral oil** (peanut, canola, or vegetable)  
- 2 **eggs**, lightly beaten  
- 1/2 cup **frozen peas & carrots mix** (or just peas if you want it really simple)  
- 2–3 tbsp **soy sauce**  
- 1/2 tsp **MSG** (adjust to taste, don’t overdo)  
- 1–2 green onions, chopped (optional but highly recommended)  
- Small dash of **white pepper** (optional, classic restaurant flavor)  

---

## Method
1. **Heat the wok/pan** – Get it very hot before adding oil. Fried rice lives or dies on heat.  
2. **Cook the eggs** – Add a little oil, scramble eggs just until set, then push to the side or remove.  
3. **Fry the rice** – Add more oil if needed, then dump in cold rice. Break it apart and let it fry undisturbed for 20–30 seconds at a time so it gets some toasty spots.  
4. **Season** – Splash soy sauce around the edges of the pan (not straight on the rice, it helps it sizzle), then add MSG and white pepper. Stir to coat.  
5. **Veggies + eggs back in** – Toss in peas/carrots, then return eggs. Stir-fry together.  
6. **Finish** – Toss in green onion at the end for freshness.  

---

## Tips
- **Rice texture matters most.** Use day-old rice from the fridge; fresh rice will be mushy.  
- **MSG + soy sauce = restaurant flavor.** Don’t need oyster sauce, sesame oil, or extras unless you want them.  
- If you want meat, just dice and fry some ham, chicken, or shrimp at the beginning, then continue as written.
