### 🐛 The Evolution Path in Pokémon Red
1. **Weedle** (Lv 1–6):  
   - Starts with **Poison Sting** and **String Shot**.  
   - Learns **nothing else** by level-up.

2. **Kakuna** (Lv 7–9):  
   - Evolves from Weedle at **Lv 7**.  
   - Only knows **Harden** (and whatever it knew before evolving).  
   - So: if your Weedle had *Poison Sting* and *String Shot*, your Kakuna had those plus *Harden.*

3. **Beedrill** (Lv 10):  
   - Evolves from Kakuna at **Lv 10**.  
   - In **Gen 1**, it *should* immediately learn **Fury Attack** upon evolving.  
   - However, **if you evolved Kakuna before level 10 or canceled moves**, it can end up keeping only its inherited moves (*Poison Sting*, *String Shot*, maybe *Harden*) and miss Fury Attack.
