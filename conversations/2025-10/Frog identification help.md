That’s a **Pickerel Frog** (*Lithobates palustris*).  

You can tell by the:
- **Two distinct rows of square/rectangular dark spots** running down its back,  
- **Light tan or bronze base color**,  
- **Bright yellow or orange on the inner thighs** (often visible when it jumps), and  
- **Smooth skin** and **slender body**, unlike the chunkier Leopard Frog.  

They’re native to much of the eastern U.S., including **North Carolina**, and prefer cool, clean water near streams or shaded ponds. They’re mildly toxic to predators — their skin secretions can irritate other animals’ mouths.
