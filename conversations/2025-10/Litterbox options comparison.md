## Assistant

What they’re showing in that ad image is **two different cleaning modes** on the same litter robot:

- **“Cleaning cat poop only”** →  
  This is the regular mode. The drum rotates just far enough to sift out **only the clumped waste** (urine clumps + feces). Clean litter stays inside the globe, so you don’t waste it. The dirty clumps fall into the lined drawer below.

- **“Cleaning all cat litter”** →  
  This is a **full dump/reset mode**. The drum rotates farther and empties *everything* — both clean and dirty litter — into the waste drawer. That way, if you want to do a deep clean, change brands, or just refresh all the litter, you don’t have to scoop it by hand. You pull out the lined bin, throw it away, and start with a fresh fill.  

So in short:  
- **Poop only = daily/normal cleaning cycle.**  
- **All litter = total empty cycle for deep cleaning or a full refresh.**  

That “all litter” option is actually really handy if you like your idea of topping off daily, but want to occasionally do a **hard reset** without scooping anything.
