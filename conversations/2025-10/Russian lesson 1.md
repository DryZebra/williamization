### 🗣️ Lesson 1: Greetings and Introductions

| Russian | Pronunciation | English |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Привет | *pree-VYET* | Hi / Hey |
| Здравствуйте | *ZDRAHST-vooy-tye* | Hello (formal) |
| Как дела? | *kahk dee-LAH?* | How are you? |
| Хорошо, а ты? | *khah-rah-SHO, ah ty?* | Good, and you? |
| Плохо | *PLO-kha* | Bad |
| Нормально | *nar-MAHL-na* | Okay / Fine |
| Меня зовут Эзра | *min-YA za-VOOT Ezra* | My name is Ezra |
| Очень приятно | *OH-chin pree-YAHT-na* | Nice to meet you |

---

### 🧩 Grammar focus: “Меня зовут”
Literally, it means “They call me.”  
- **Меня** = “me” (accusative case)  
- **зовут** = “(they) call”  

So “Меня зовут Эзра” literally translates to “They call me Ezra.”
