## ⚡ Phase 1 – Build Neural Muscle Memory (Weeks 1–2)
Goal: **Stop reading the NEC like a book. Start reading it like a map.**

### 1. Anchor by Articles, Not Pages
You already think in systems—so memorize **Article anchors**:
- **90–110**: Foundation, Definitions, Scope  
- **200–300**: Grounding, Conductors  
- **400–500**: Cords, Receptacles, Motors  
- **600–800**: Lighting, Special Occupancies  
- **900**: Tables and Informational Notes

🧠 Method:
- Each morning, read one Article title and say out loud:
  > “210 is Branch Circuits. 220 is Calculations. 230 is Services.”
- Repeat it *like practicing conduit bends*—not reading.

## 🔩 Phase 2 – Master Code Navigation (Weeks 3–4)
Goal: **Know where everything lives without knowing everything.**

### 1. The “Locate Test”
Every night, have me quiz you:
> “Where would I find the minimum burial depth for UF cable?”

You answer:
> “Table 300.5, top-left corner.”

If you can locate within 10 seconds, you pass.  
If not, you practice until your hand goes to that page like your pouch goes to your strippers.

### 2. Highlight by Logic, Not Color
Color-code by **function**, not topic:
- **Green** → Safety / grounding  
- **Blue** → Sizing / calculations  
- **Red** → Exceptions  
- **Yellow** → Definitions  
You’ll start *seeing* logic trees on the page.
