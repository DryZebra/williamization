- **Estimated tonnage:** ~7.5 million tons total in Southeast Asia (U.S. operations across Vietnam, Laos, and Cambodia).

- **Outcome:** U.S. withdrawal; North Vietnam victory.

#### **3. Korean War (1950–1953)**
