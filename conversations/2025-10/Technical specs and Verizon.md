## ✅ What to look for: guaranteed compatibility criteria

To ensure a flip phone will work on Verizon today, it should meet the following:

1. **4G LTE / VoLTE support** — Since Verizon has shut down CDMA/3G, any phone must support LTE voice (VoLTE).  
2. **Support for Verizon’s LTE bands / network** — The phone needs the right radio hardware to use Verizon’s LTE bands (e.g. band 13, band 4, etc.).  
3. **Be listed or approved by Verizon (or an unlocked phone known to work)** — Verizon often offers “basic phones” on their website; those are guaranteed compatible.  
4. **Unlocked (or locked to Verizon)** — If locked to Verizon, you don’t have to worry about BYOD issues. If unlocked, make sure the unlocked model supports the necessary bands and VoLTE.  
5. **Modern firmware / device profiles** — The phone should have carrier provisioning / firmware that enables Verizon’s services (voice, texts, possibly WiFi calling, etc.).

Phones lacking VoLTE or only supporting older 2G/3G are essentially unusable for voice/text on Verizon now.
