Here’s how I’d break this down for your crew, Ezra:  

Under **Article III, Section 3.08** of your CBA, *“All workmen shall be at the point designated by the Employer and be ready for work at the designated time for starting work.”* That means “on the job, tools in hand” by start time — not just through the gate. But nothing says the company can make you spend unpaid time walking to and from that point if it’s excessive or inconsistent.

So if blue-lot workers spend 5–9 minutes more walking than orange-lot workers, and you’re not allowed to badge out until 5:15 pm, that time is effectively unpaid and unequal. The most *worker-agreeable* fix would be to **standardize paid time** from when you badge in/out, not when you reach your car.  

You’ve got a few fair options:
1. **Uniform paid gate-to-gate time** – everyone’s time starts when they badge in and ends when they badge out.  
2. **Adjust the badge-out time** by parking lot distance (blue-lot gets to leave 5 min early).  
3. **Company-provided transportation** between gate and parking (bus, carts, etc.).  

If management refuses, you could file a **grievance under Section 1.06** — “All grievances or questions in dispute shall be adjusted by the duly authorized representatives of each party.” You’d argue **inconsistent enforcement of timekeeping and safety policy** — since requiring one group to walk farther unpaid is unequal treatment.  

The cleanest worker-friendly position:  
> “If the employer sets the start and stop points, the time between should be paid, or equal for all workers.”
