# Stimulating short activities

## Assistant

Here are some short-burst, dopamine-friendly things that work like TikTok but actually *build* your focus instead of draining it:  

---

### ⚡ Micro-Challenge Loops
These hit the same reward centers as short videos.
- **GeoGuessr** (free) – drops you anywhere on Earth via Google Street View; guess where you are. Each round is 30–90 seconds.  
- **Human Benchmark** – reaction-time and memory mini-games (pattern, aim, sequence).  
- **Duolingo** or **LingQ mini-lessons** – 2-minute bursts of language with sound and instant feedback.  
- **TypingTest / MonkeyType** – surprisingly satisfying if you compete with yourself every few minutes.

---

### 🧠 “Scroll-but-Learn” Feeds
If your brain craves swiping:
- **Reddit r/TodayILearned** or **r/AskHistorians** in “compact” mode → fast info, not sludge.  
- **StackOverflow Hot Questions** or **Wikipedia Random** → click-rabbit-hole satisfaction, each article = one TikTok’s length.  
- **Blinkist / Headway free summaries** → 10-minute “reads” condensed into 1-minute scrolls.

---

### 🎮 Bite-Sized Games
- **20 Minutes Till Dawn**, **Vampire Survivors**, or **Cell to Singularity** – pure dopamine loops in minutes.  
- **Tetris Effect**, **Mini Motorways**, **Downwell**, or **Alto’s Odyssey** – visually rhythmic, short runs, great flow.  
- **Crosswordle / Heardle / Wordle-style clones** – single quick puzzles per day.

---

### 🧩 Physical / Practical Dopamine
Sometimes the hit you want is *embodied*:
- Toss juggling balls for 2 minutes (timed bursts).  
- Do one set of push-ups or kettlebell swings every time you check your phone.  
- Keep a **“micro-goal” jar**: pull a slip like “draw a tree,” “organize one drawer,” “text a friend.” Each finishes in < 5 minutes.

---

### 🧘 Dialed-Back Alternatives
If you’re detoxing from dopamine floods:
- Use **Pomodoro playlists** (5 on / 1 off) where the “off” minute is your mini-scroll time.  
- Try **Stoic** or **Finch** apps – they gamify journaling and reflection, giving you streaks and coins instead of mindless swipes.
