Good question — and great timing to ask it, because when to start trying depends on how your girlfriend’s cycle usually runs.  
Here’s the straightforward breakdown:

---

### 🩸 1. Understand the timing
Ovulation (when the egg is released) happens **about 14 days before the next period starts**, not 14 days after the last one.  
So, if your girlfriend has a **28-day cycle**, she’ll likely ovulate around **day 14** (counting the first day of her period as day 1).  
If she has:
- a **shorter cycle** (e.g. 26 days) → ovulation may be around **day 12**  
- a **longer cycle** (e.g. 32 days) → around **day 18**

---

### 💕 2. The “fertile window”
Sperm can live **up to 5 days** inside the female reproductive tract, while the egg lives **only about 24 hours**.  
That means the **fertile window** is roughly **days 10–15** in a 28-day cycle, with **day 14** as the most fertile.

---

### 🕓 3. When to start trying
Since today was (likely) the last day of her period:
- Start having sex **every day or every other day** beginning **in 3–4 days**, and keep that up for about **a week**.  
- This timing ensures that healthy sperm are already present as she approaches ovulation.

---

### 🧭 4. Optional: tracking ovulation
If you want to be precise:
- Watch for **clear, stretchy cervical mucus** (like egg whites).
- Use **ovulation test strips** around day 10–12.
- Track **basal body temperature** to confirm ovulation has happened (temperature rises slightly after).
