# Dog ate stick advice

## Assistant

It depends on the size of the stick, how much your dog ate, and whether it’s causing problems.  

**Why it can be bad:**
- Sticks can splinter and cut your dog’s mouth, throat, stomach, or intestines.  
- Larger pieces can cause choking or create a blockage in the digestive tract.  
- Sharp fragments could even puncture the gut, which is very dangerous.  

**What to watch for right now:**
- Gagging, choking, or pawing at the mouth.  
- Drooling or difficulty swallowing.  
- Vomiting, diarrhea, or blood in stool.  
- Signs of abdominal pain (restlessness, whining, hunching, refusing food).  
- Lethargy or sudden changes in behavior.  

**What you should do:**
- If your dog is choking or in distress, go to an emergency vet immediately.  
- If it was a small chew and your dog seems fine, keep a close eye for the next 24–48 hours.  
- Do **not** try to make your dog vomit—it could cause more damage on the way back up.  
- Call your vet, tell them what size/type of stick and how much was eaten—they can advise whether your dog needs to be seen.  

👉 Since swallowing wood can be risky, the safest move is to call your vet now and get their recommendation.
