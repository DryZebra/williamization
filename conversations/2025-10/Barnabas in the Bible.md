# Barnabas in the Bible

## User

Who was Barnabas in the bible?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Barnabas was one of the earliest Christian disciples and a major figure in the spread of Christianity after Christ’s resurrection. His name means **“son of encouragement”** (Acts 4:36), and he was originally named **Joseph**, a Levite from Cyprus.  

Here’s a concise summary of his biblical role:  

---

### 🕊️ **Early Church Leader**
- **First Mention:** Acts 4:36–37 — Barnabas sells his land and donates the money to the apostles, symbolizing his generosity and commitment to the new Christian community.
- He becomes known for his kindness, integrity, and leadership in the Jerusalem church.

---

### 🤝 **Mentor to Paul**
- After Saul (later called Paul) converts, most Christians are afraid of him. Barnabas personally **vouches for Saul** before the apostles (Acts 9:26–27), helping integrate him into the church.
- He later **travels with Paul** on missionary journeys, preaching to Jews and Gentiles across Cyprus and Asia Minor (Acts 13–14).

---

### 🌍 **Missionary Work**
- Together with Paul, he helps establish churches in places like **Antioch**, where followers of Jesus were first called *Christians* (Acts 11:26).
- They face persecution but spread the gospel widely and perform miracles (Acts 13:50; 14:8–15).

---

### ⚖️ **Council of Jerusalem**
- Barnabas accompanies Paul to Jerusalem to help settle debates about whether Gentile converts must follow Mosaic Law (Acts 15).  
- He supports inclusion of Gentiles without full adherence to Jewish ritual law.

---

### 💔 **Disagreement with Paul**
- Later, Barnabas and Paul **disagree sharply** over whether to take John Mark (Barnabas’s cousin) on their next journey, after Mark had previously deserted them (Acts 15:36–39).  
- They part ways: Paul goes with Silas; Barnabas goes with Mark to Cyprus.  
  Despite the split, Paul later speaks well of Barnabas (1 Corinthians 9:6) and of Mark (2 Timothy 4:11), implying reconciliation.

---

### 🕯️ **Legacy**
- Barnabas is remembered as a bridge-builder — someone who saw potential in others, gave second chances, and emphasized unity and compassion.  
- Tradition (outside the Bible) says he was **martyred in Cyprus**, continuing his mission until death.
