The story begins with an unexpected turn of events when an ordinary man, driven by a whim, decides to participate in the royal selection. Little does he know that this seemingly impulsive act will plunge him into a labyrinth of secrets and hidden truths.

Fate takes a sharp twist when the protagonist, inadvertently claiming descent from a lineage of "betrayers," finds himself entangled in a web of intrigue. Unbeknownst to him, a clandestine organization believes in the innocence of the "betrayer" and is led by none other than one of the noble houses—the cousins of the "betrayer,” house Hibernal. 

As the story progresses, a compelling red herring emerges. The protagonist begins to entertain the notion that he might be the true descendant of the "betrayer," only to discover the truth—the actual son, who bore a striking resemblance to him, is deceased. This revelation opens up new possibilities, prompting the protagonist and his allies to continue the charade and gain the support of the people in secret.

The narrative deepens as the tale shifts its focus to the female protagonist, a noble battle maiden from the first cousin's household. Her unwavering loyalty and unwavering belief in the protagonist's cause add a layer of complexity to the story. However, the drama intensifies when a loyalist family uncovers the truth about the real son's demise and the protagonist's deception.

This revelation forces the secret organization to emerge from the shadows and declare their allegiance to the protagonist, setting the stage for a tumultuous period in Eldoria's history. The country is thrown into chaos as citizens grapple with their loyalties, torn between royal houses. Suspense, and political maneuvering reach a fever pitch as the story builds toward a climactic showdown.

Amidst the turmoil, a poignant twist emerges. Despite the protagonist's pretense, some citizens genuinely support him, recognizing the good deeds he has done for the people so far. This unexpected turn of events adds a moral dimension to the story, exploring themes of justice, redemption, and the power of compassion.

The narrative unfolds through a series of twists and turns. As the story reaches its climax, alliances shift, secrets are unveiled, and the fate of Eldoria hangs in the balance. The protagonist must confront his past, make difficult choices, and embrace his true identity in order to restore order and bring peace to the realm.

House Perihelion: The house of the betrayer, family mostly killed, people living in the lands ruled by this house are monitored and oppressed. The house now is mostly in shambles after it’s leader allegedly attempted to assassinate the leader of house Leonas and current sitting Queen. This is the region the protagonist is from. This house and the region that it rules over is militaristic, but mostly as a form of martial art and self defense, they are also quite religious, but not nearly as much as their neighbors of house Hibernal. Always preferring diplomacy but never hesitating to defend itself. The people from this region made up a small bulk of the empire’s military. Enough to have a significant influence, but not as large as house Leonas. People from this region are stoic, but extremely strong and kind. They can appear intimidating from a distance, but are generally nice people. 

House Leonas, the house of the sitting queen. 35 years ago, her life was allegedly attempted to be taken by the leader of house Perihelion. After which she utilized her command over the bulk of the armed forces in the country to seize the leader of house Perihelion and have him publicly executed. After which the lands once belonging to the house of Perihelion split between house Hibernal, and house Estival. People from House Leonas are typically very warrior-like, but tactical, even to the point of being scientific about tactics and politics. The noblemen of this house know the advantage given to them by their military might, and aren’t afraid to show it. They’ll do anything to secure their power.  

House Estival, a house very close to house Leonas, the two houses have always gotten along very well for many generations. People from this region typically flaunt their wealth and power in the faces of others, very flashy and pompous. 

House Hibernal, a house very close to House Perihelion. This house is very religious, very reserved and overall kind and caring people. They take the religion of the empire the most seriously of all the houses, home to its most sacred landmarks, churches, and important moments in the religion of the empire. 

House Vernal, an extremely industrious house of smiths, carpenters, and all sorts of skilled craftsmen. The house in the conflict is split between Leonas and Perihelion. While the nobles of the house side with Leonas, the people mostly side with Perihelion either majority through their support for house Hibernal, and some directly support the claims of Perihelion. A small portion of the population supports their nobles. 

House Ernst a noble house of farms. Equestrian and agriculture people, the nobles and the equestrian farmers side with Leonas, while the agricultural farmers side with their similarly stoic and hard working, but fun and jovial cousins in House Perihelion. 

The protagonist is a random commoner from the House of Perihelion, with no royal blood, but culturally connected to the people in the region. He was born shortly after the alleged assassination attempt. Being from a poorer village at the edge of house of Perihelion and Ernst. Every summer, a nearby house would have a nobleman from house Hibernal’s vacation home near the protagonists house.
