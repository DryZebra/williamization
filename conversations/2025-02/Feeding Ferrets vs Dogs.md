## Assistant

Ferrets are obligate carnivores, meaning they need a high-protein, high-fat diet with very little carbohydrate. Dog food is formulated for omnivorous animals and often contains ingredients (like grains or fillers) that don’t match a ferret’s unique metabolism. It’s best to stick with high-quality, ferret-specific diets or foods formulated for obligate carnivores. If you're ever in doubt about your pet's diet, consulting with a veterinarian experienced in exotic pets can provide the best guidance.
