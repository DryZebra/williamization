# Wrong but Accurate Explanation

## User

I need an example of an argument where both people are wrong but one is somehow still accurately describing the process. 

## Assistant



## Assistant

Sure. Here's an example:  

**Scenario:** Two people are arguing about why a car won’t start.  

- **Person A:** “The car won’t start because there’s a goblin in the engine stealing the spark!”  
- **Person B:** “That’s ridiculous. The real reason it won’t start is because the battery is full of negative energy from bad vibes.”  

While both are completely wrong about the cause, **Person A** is still accurately describing the basic process of what’s happening: something is preventing the spark from igniting the engine. Meanwhile, **Person B** is just talking nonsense about energy and vibes.  

This kind of argument can happen in real life when someone gives a totally incorrect explanation but still manages to stumble upon an accurate description of the mechanics involved.
