## Assistant

Below is a sample high‐performance build aimed at 4K gaming (30 fps or better) in titles like Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2. This configuration emphasizes a modern, high‐clock-speed CPU plus a powerful GPU to keep up with 4K’s heavy pixel count—while also future‐proofing your system. Note that prices and availability can change, so consider these as starting points to tailor the build to your budget and preferences.

---

### 1. CPU  
Since Kingdom Come: Deliverance (and its sequel) is known for being quite CPU‐intensive, you’ll want a chip with strong single‑threaded performance as well as enough cores for overall system responsiveness. Two excellent options are:

- **Intel Option:**  
  **Intel Core i7‑13700K**  
  • High clock speeds and strong single‑core performance  
  • Plenty of cores/threads for modern games and multitasking  

- **AMD Option:**  
  **AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D**  
  • The 3D V‑Cache design can offer a noticeable boost in gaming performance  
  • Great balance of cores and high IPC (instructions per cycle)

---

### 2. Motherboard  
Choose a board that fits your CPU choice and supports modern memory and connectivity:

- **For Intel:** A Z790 (or Z690) chipset board supporting DDR5 (or high‑speed DDR4 on select boards) is ideal.
- **For AMD:** A B650 or X670 motherboard is recommended—ensure it supports the latest features you need (PCIe 5.0, USB 3.2, etc.).

---

### 3. Memory  
While 16 GB might work in some current titles, for modern AAA games and future‑proofing at 4K you’ll benefit from more headroom:

- **32 GB (2×16 GB) DDR5** running at around 6000 MHz  
  • DDR5 adds a bit of future‑proofing and faster data rates  
  • Dual‑channel configuration helps overall performance

---

### 4. Graphics Card  
For 4K gaming at high settings, the GPU is the key:

- **Primary Recommendation:**  
  **Nvidia GeForce RTX 4080**  
  • Capable of delivering 4K gaming performance comfortably above 30 fps on demanding titles  
  • Features like DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling) can help maintain smooth frame rates while preserving image quality

- **Alternative:** If you want extra headroom (or plan to run games at ultra settings or higher frame rates), consider the **RTX 4090** or AMD’s high‑end offerings (e.g., RX 7900 XTX). But for your stated goal, the 4080 should suffice.

---

### 5. Storage  
Fast, ample storage reduces load times and keeps your system snappy:

- **Primary Drive:**  
  **1 TB or larger NVMe M.2 SSD** (e.g., Samsung 980 Pro, WD Black SN850)  
  • Quick boot times, game load times, and overall system responsiveness

- **Optional:** A secondary SATA or NVMe drive if you need extra space for media and additional games.

---

### 6. Cooling  
A high‑performance CPU benefits from robust cooling:

- **For Air Cooling:** A high‑end air cooler (e.g., Noctua NH‑D15)  
- **For Liquid Cooling:** A 240–280 mm AIO cooler can offer lower temperatures and quieter operation, especially if you plan to overclock

---

### 7. Power Supply (PSU)  
A quality PSU is critical for system stability, especially with power‑hungry parts:

- **850 W 80+ Gold (or Platinum) Certified PSU**  
  • Reputable brands include Corsair (RM850x), Seasonic, and EVGA  
  • Ensure you have the right connectors for your GPU and headroom for future upgrades

---

### 8. Case and Additional Components  
- **Case:** Choose a mid‑tower or full‑tower with excellent airflow (e.g., Fractal Design Meshify 2, NZXT H710)  
- **Peripherals:** Don’t forget to factor in your monitor (must be a true 4K display), keyboard, mouse, and any other accessories.

---

### Final Thoughts

This build should comfortably run Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 at 4K with at least 30 fps even at high settings. Because game optimization can vary, having a bit of headroom—especially with the RTX 4080—is a wise move. You might even see much higher frame rates if you dial settings down or use features like DLSS, which can upscale performance while maintaining visual fidelity.

Before purchasing, check for:
- The latest BIOS updates for your motherboard,
- Compatibility (especially if you’re opting for DDR5),
- And recent benchmarks (as the game’s final release details might tweak optimal hardware choices).

Feel free to ask if you need further details or adjustments based on your budget and available deals!
