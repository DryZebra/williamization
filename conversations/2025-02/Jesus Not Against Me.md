# Jesus Not Against Me

## User

Somewhere in the Bible Jesus says something like he was not against me in Matthew or something. 

## Assistant



## Assistant

Yes, you're likely thinking of **Matthew 12:30** or **Mark 9:40**.  

1. **Matthew 12:30 (KJV)** – *"He that is not with me is against me; and he that gathereth not with me scattereth abroad."*  
   - Here, Jesus emphasizes that there is no neutrality in spiritual matters—either someone is actively with Him or against Him.  

2. **Mark 9:40 (KJV)** – *"For he that is not against us is on our part."*  
   - In this passage, Jesus tells His disciples not to stop someone casting out demons in His name, explaining that those not opposing them are actually on their side.  

The two verses may seem contradictory at first, but they address different contexts. Matthew 12:30 speaks about being decisively committed to Christ, while Mark 9:40 shows that those not opposing Christ’s work can still be seen as allies.
