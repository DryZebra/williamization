## Assistant

I’m not a veterinarian, but in general, it’s best to be cautious when giving dog treats to ferrets. Ferrets have very specific nutritional needs (high protein, high fat, and minimal carbohydrates) because they are strict carnivores. Many commercial dog treats—especially longer-lasting chews—can contain ingredients (like starches, vegetable matter, or certain binders) that aren’t ideal for a ferret’s digestive system. There can also be a risk that a hard chew could cause intestinal blockages if your ferret tries to swallow pieces of it.

If you really want to offer something like this, it would be wise to check with a vet who’s knowledgeable about ferrets. They might have a better recommendation for a safe, high-protein treat specifically formulated for ferrets.
