## 2. Conspiracies, Revelations, and Hidden Threads

**Ancient History and the “Will of Ohara”:**  
• The arc deepens the mystery of the Void Century. Shaka’s theories—and his mention that “the Will of Ohara still exists”—suggest that the lost history (and the knowledge of ancient civilizations) has been intentionally hidden by the World Government.  
• Vegapunk’s broadcast not only exposes the secret war of 900 years ago (in which Joy Boy played a pivotal role) but also implies that even the legendary Roger Pirates were aware of these truths. This revelation is explosive, as it ties the fabled “D” in Monkey D. Luffy’s name to a long line of opponents of oppressive regimes.

**Government, Immortality, and the True Rulers:**  
• For the first time, the Five Elders and the mysterious Imu are shown wielding almost supernatural powers (including awakened Mythical Zoan abilities and rapid regeneration). Their actions—such as Imu executing Saturn for failure—hint at a hidden hierarchy that has controlled the world for centuries.  
• Conspiracies abound regarding Vegapunk’s “sacrificial” death: many fans suspect that he planned his own murder to ensure his secret broadcast would rock the foundations of the World Government. This, in turn, might have been a deliberate move to force a reordering of global power.

**Hints About the “D” and Joy Boy:**  
• Vegapunk’s revelations tie Joy Boy to the ancient conflict and hint that those bearing the “D.” initial are destined to challenge the world’s oppressive order. There is now speculation that the true history of the Void Century—and even the nature of Devil Fruits themselves (as products of human desire)—will come to light very soon.
