# Self-Perception and Reappraisal

## User

I'm looking for a psychological phenomenon that is basically fake it till you make it, with positive thinking early in the day and reframing 

## Assistant



## Assistant

It sounds like you're looking for **"self-perception theory"** or **"cognitive reappraisal"**, both of which align with "fake it till you make it" and positive reframing.  

- **Self-Perception Theory (Bem, 1972):** This suggests that people infer their own attitudes and emotions by observing their behavior. If you act confident and positive, your mind starts to internalize those traits.  
- **Cognitive Reappraisal:** A key component of cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), this involves changing how you interpret a situation to shift your emotional response. Instead of seeing challenges as threats, you view them as opportunities.  
- **The "As If" Principle:** Similar to self-perception theory, this idea (popularized by William James) argues that acting "as if" you are happy, motivated, or confident can actually lead to those feelings becoming real.

## Assistant

It sounds like you're looking for **"embodied cognition."**  

Embodied cognition is the idea that our physical actions and bodily experiences can shape our thoughts, emotions, and perceptions. Essentially, the way you move, posture yourself, or engage in physical activities can influence your mental state.  

### Examples:  
- **Power Posing** (Amy Cuddy's research): Standing in a confident posture can actually make you feel more powerful.  
- **Facial Feedback Hypothesis:** Smiling, even when you don’t feel happy, can make you feel happier because your brain associates the physical action with the corresponding emotion.  
- **Enclothed Cognition:** Wearing certain clothes (like a lab coat or a uniform) can make you think and act in ways associated with that role.  

This fits well with the idea that physically doing something forces your mind to follow suit. Would this be what you're looking for?
