# Mere Exposure Effect

## User

What's that psychological process called where the more you're exposed to something the more you'll like it?

## Assistant



## Assistant

You're thinking of the **mere exposure effect** (or **familiarity principle**). It's a psychological phenomenon where people tend to develop a preference for things simply because they are exposed to them repeatedly. This effect can apply to various things like music, people, places, and even abstract ideas.
