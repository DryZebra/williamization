## Assistant

Golden Retrievers with an English Cream (also called "British Cream") lineage tend to be more expensive than standard American Golden Retrievers due to their appearance, breeding lineage, and demand.

Here’s a breakdown of typical prices in the U.S.:

- **Standard American Golden Retriever**: $800 – $2,000  
- **English Cream Golden Retriever**: $1,500 – $4,000+  

If the **mother is an English Cream**, and the **father is either English or American**, you’re likely looking at **$1,800 – $3,500** depending on:

- **Pedigree (Champion bloodlines can raise the price)**
- **Health clearances (hip, elbow, heart, and eye checks)**
- **Breeder reputation**
- **Location** (Urban areas or the West Coast often have higher prices)

Some breeders market mixed-lineage Goldens (e.g., American dad, English mom) as “English Cream-style” and may charge a premium based mostly on color.
