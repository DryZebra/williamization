## User

What about when not at a lighting load?

## Assistant

If the switch does not control a **lighting load**, the NEC **does not** require a neutral conductor at the switch location. The requirement in **NEC 404.2(C)** applies **only** to switches controlling lighting loads.  

For other types of loads, such as **motor controls, receptacle controls, or other non-lighting applications**, a neutral **is not explicitly required** at the switch. However, it's still good practice to pull a neutral to switch boxes when possible, especially for future-proofing installations.
