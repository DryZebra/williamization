This would include a stronger theoretical understanding of environmental exploitation as inherently tied to capitalist exploitation, guiding global environmental policy.

   Marxism traditionally advocates international solidarity of the working class. With emerging digital global connectivity, the next great Marxist innovation could be in developing practical tools that support international solidarity, coordination, and revolution in a highly interconnected capitalist world.

5. **Revolutionizing Marxist Education**:  
   Leveraging digital education platforms to propagate Marxist ideology and organize internationally could represent a major practical contribution, reshaping the ways Marxism reaches and mobilizes people globally.

In short, the next major contributions to material Marxism are likely to revolve around **integrating artificial intelligence and ecological sustainability with Marxist economic planning and theory**.
