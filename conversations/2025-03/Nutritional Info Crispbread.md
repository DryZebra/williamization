If an adult male ate only **Swedish-style multigrain crispbread and Sabra hummus** at a maintenance caloric intake (about **2,000 kcal/day**), he would experience both benefits and severe deficiencies over time. Here's what would likely happen:

---

### **1 Week: Initial Adjustment**
- **Macronutrients**: The diet provides carbohydrates (from crispbread), fats (from hummus), and some protein (from both), so he wouldn't experience extreme deficiencies yet.
- **Micronutrients**: Deficiencies in vitamins B12, C, and D would begin developing.
- **Digestion**: High fiber intake (from whole grains and chickpeas) could cause bloating, gas, and diarrhea.
- **Energy Levels**: Initially, he might feel fine, but the lack of variety could cause mild fatigue.

---

### **1 Month: Early Deficiencies**
- **Vitamin Deficiencies**: 
  - **Vitamin C deficiency** begins, leading to mild scurvy symptoms like fatigue and gum inflammation.
  - **Vitamin B12 deficiency** leads to lower energy and mild neurological symptoms like tingling in extremities.
  - **Iron absorption may decrease** due to lack of heme iron sources, potentially leading to mild anemia.
- **Weight Maintenance**: He could sustain his weight, but he might experience muscle loss due to incomplete protein intake.
- **Gut Health**: High fiber content may improve digestion for some but could cause discomfort due to lack of variety in gut bacteria.

---

### **1 Year: Serious Health Issues**
- **Scurvy (Vitamin C Deficiency)**: Gums may start bleeding, wounds heal slowly, skin bruises easily, and severe fatigue develops.
- **Osteoporosis (Vitamin D and Calcium Deficiency)**: The diet lacks calcium and vitamin D, leading to weaker bones, increasing the risk of fractures.
- **Neurological Issues (B12 Deficiency)**: 
  - Nerve damage progresses, leading to memory issues, brain fog, and poor coordination.
  - Potential irreversible neurological damage from prolonged deficiency.
- **Immune System Weakness**: Frequent infections due to low zinc and vitamin C intake.
- **Muscle Loss**: Due to inadequate essential amino acids (hummus and crispbread lack complete protein), muscle atrophy progresses.
- **Emotional/Mental Health Issues**: Mood swings, depression, and fatigue due to lack of omega-3s, essential fats, and key vitamins.

---

### **1 Decade: Extreme Health Decline**
- **Severe Bone Degeneration**: Osteoporosis worsens, leading to fractures and potentially a hunched posture.
- **Severe Neurological Damage**: Permanent cognitive impairment, memory loss, and mobility issues.
- **Severe Immune Suppression**: Frequent and severe infections, potentially leading to early mortality.
- **Heart Problems**: Lack of omega-3s, antioxidants, and essential micronutrients could lead to increased risk of heart disease.
- **Muscle Wasting**: Severe sarcopenia due to a lack of complete protein, leading to frailty.
- **Organ Failure Risk**: Nutrient deficiencies and metabolic imbalances may lead to kidney or liver dysfunction.

---

### **Summary**
A diet of only Swedish-style crispbread and hummus would be **sustainable for a short time** but would become dangerous in the long term. Over a decade, **severe vitamin and mineral deficiencies, muscle wasting, and immune dysfunction** would likely lead to early mortality. While it provides **fiber, some protein, and healthy fats**, it is **critically deficient in essential vitamins (C, D, B12) and complete proteins** required for long-term survival.
