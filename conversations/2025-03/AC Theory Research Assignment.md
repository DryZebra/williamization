### **Assignment Title:** *AC Circuits in the Real World: Research & Reflection*

**Objective:**  
Students will research a real-world application of AC theory (Level 2 concepts) and reflect on how the theory translates into practice. This encourages critical thinking, contextual learning, and independent study.

**Instructions:**

1. **Choose One Topic Below (or propose your own):**
   - How transformers step voltage up/down and why it's essential for power distribution.
   - Power factor in commercial settings—what it is, why it matters, and how it’s corrected.
   - How inductive or capacitive reactance affects HVAC systems, motors, or fluorescent lighting.
   - The function and behavior of RLC circuits in industrial controls.
   - The impact of harmonics in industrial electrical systems.
   - How 3-phase power is generated, transmitted, and used in industry.

2. **Research & Write (1-2 pages typed OR 5-minute video):**
   - Define the key theory from class (include phasors, impedance, resonance, etc.).
   - Show how this applies in a real system (e.g., transformer in a substation, power factor correction in a factory).
   - Include at least one real product, manufacturer, or standard (e.g., Square D transformer, NEMA standards).
   - Explain what would happen if the theory wasn’t properly applied (practical consequences).
   - Reflect: “How does this help me as a future electrician?”

3. **Optional but Encouraged:**
   - Interview a journeyman or contractor about how they deal with this issue in the field.
   - Include a simple hand-drawn diagram or screenshot that illustrates the concept.

4. **Submission Options:**
   - Written report (PDF or Word).
   - Video explanation (uploaded to private YouTube link or submitted via email).
   - Slide presentation with voiceover.

**Grading Criteria:**
- Accuracy of technical concepts (25%)
- Real-world connection and clarity (25%)
- Depth of reflection (25%)
- Neatness, format, and effort (25%)
