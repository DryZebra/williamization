B. B. James is quoting **Matthew 18:6**, a verse where Jesus warns against leading children—or those with childlike faith—into sin. The verse says it would be better for someone to be drowned than to cause such harm.

In this post, he uses the verse in response to the **Center for Baptist Leadership’s** claim that **Andy Stanley's megachurch is teaching families how to "turn their kids transgender."** 

**BB James is implying that doing this (in his view, influencing children toward being transgender) is the kind of action Jesus warned about—leading "little ones" astray—and that it deserves severe condemnation.**

It’s a strong rhetorical use of Scripture to express disapproval, based on a very particular theological and political perspective.
