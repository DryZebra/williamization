---

### **2. Materialist Christian Take: Matter, Motion, and Process Have No Origin**

> “There doesn’t have to be a 'beginning' to existence in the way we think of it. Materialism teaches us that the universe is made of matter in motion. > “The Big Bang isn’t a random explosion from nothing. It’s part of a chain reaction—an entropic and dialectical movement of matter, energy, and potentiality that always existed. > “Entropy isn’t destruction—it’s transformation. The heat death of one system becomes the birth of another. Stars die, and their remnants become us. Entropy is the drumbeat of the Entropic Dance—the rhythm of creation.”

### **4. If They Press on "Where Did the Singularity Come From?"**
