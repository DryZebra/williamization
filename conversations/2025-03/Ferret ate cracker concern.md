## Assistant

A small piece of plain crisp bread cracker is unlikely to harm your ferret, especially if it was just a one-time nibble. Ferrets are obligate carnivores, so their digestive systems aren't designed to handle carbohydrates or grains well—but in small amounts, it's not dangerous.

Here’s what to watch for over the next 24 hours:
- **Lethargy**
- **Vomiting or diarrhea**
- **Loss of appetite**
- **Signs of discomfort or bloating**
