*"Jesus wasn’t a capitalist. He literally said accumulating wealth was worthless. If your treasure is stored in earthly possessions, it is meaningless. True wealth—real power—is in what you create, not what you hoard."*

### **Do Not Worry About Tomorrow (Matthew 6:25-34)**  
✔ Jesus says **don’t worry about money, food, or clothing—just focus on righteousness.**  
✔ **God provides for all creatures—so why hoard wealth?**  

**Communist Take:**  
*"Capitalism thrives on manufactured scarcity. Jesus is literally saying that stockpiling resources out of fear is pointless—there is enough for everyone if we live righteously and collectively. This is the destruction of artificial scarcity."*

## **5. Jesus’ Anti-Rich Rhetoric (Matthew 7:21-23, 19:23-26)**

## **6. The Parable of the Workers in the Vineyard – Communism in Action (Matthew 20:1-16)**  
✔ A landowner hires workers throughout the day.  
✔ At the end of the day, **he pays everyone the same wage.**  
✔ The workers who labored all day **complain—but Jesus says they all agreed to the wage.**  

**Communist Take:**  
*"A person’s worth isn’t determined by the hours they put in—it’s determined by their need. Jesus’ parable obliterates wage labor as a measure of value. All workers deserve a living wage, regardless of when they entered the system."*

## **8. The Parable of the Sower – The Masses vs. the Few (Matthew 13:1-23)**  
✔ **A farmer scatters seed, but only some grow.**
