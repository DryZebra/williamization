You're in luck—Gmail makes this super easy without needing to create a whole new account.

---

### ✅ To create a **Gmail alias (sub-email)**:

Just add a **“+anything”** to your regular Gmail username. Gmail will treat it as a separate address for signup/log-in purposes, but everything still goes to your **main inbox**.

---

#### 📌 Example:

If your Gmail is:  
**`myname@gmail.com`**

You can sign up on TikTok (or any site) with:  
**`myname+vestnik@gmail.com`**

It’ll work as a completely valid and unique email address, but all emails sent to that will still go into **`myname@gmail.com`**.

---

### 🔒 Bonus tip:
You can even filter emails based on the alias:
- In Gmail, search for: `to:myname+vestnik@gmail.com`
- Or create a filter to label/archive/sort emails sent to that alias.
