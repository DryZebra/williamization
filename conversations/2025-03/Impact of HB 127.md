House Bill 127 in North Carolina mainly affects voter registration drives. It requires the State Board of Elections to create a sample voter registration form for organized voter registration efforts. The key changes include:

1. **New Sample Forms** – These forms will provide information on how to register but won't allow individuals to fill in their details.
2. **Registration Requirements for Organizations** – Any group conducting a voter registration drive must register with the county board of elections and provide details like the organization's structure, the number of forms needed, and whether participants are volunteers or paid.
3. **Restrictions on Official Forms** – The official voter registration application form cannot be used for voter registration drives, and violating this rule is a **Class 2 misdemeanor**.
