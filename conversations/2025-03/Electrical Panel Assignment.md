**Assignment:**  
Re-label and verify your home’s electrical panel.  

1. Turn off the main breaker.  
2. Systematically turn on each circuit and verify its load.  
3. Correct any mislabeled breakers.  
4. Ensure all terminations are torqued to spec.  
5. Submit a panel schedule with corrected labels.
