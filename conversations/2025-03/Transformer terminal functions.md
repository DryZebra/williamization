This is a control transformer, and the label shows the primary (line) side marked as H1 and H2, with a center tap labeled in the middle two terminals.

In this case:

- **H1** and **H2** are the main primary terminals for 120V.
- The **middle two terminals** are likely a **center tap** or additional taps for versatility in voltage configuration, often found in control transformers. If this is a 120V-only primary, then:
  - One of the center terminals may be a **neutral** tap for half-voltage configurations (like 60V-0-60V),
  - Or they're simply **jumper points** depending on how the transformer is wound internally.

**To know exactly** what those middle terminals do, you'd need to check the datasheet or model number of the transformer. But most likely in the field:

- You’d apply 120V across **H1 and H2**, and **ignore the middle two** unless you're instructed to use them for something specific (like grounding a center tap or using it for voltage monitoring).  
- If you're replacing a transformer, match connections exactly as they were before.
