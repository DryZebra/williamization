If you're shopping at Family Dollar and need a **cheap, nutritious, no-cook lunch**, here’s a solid option:  

### **High-Protein, Balanced Meal** (~$5–$7 total)  
- **Canned Tuna or Chicken** (~$1.50–$2.50) – Packed with protein, no cooking needed.  
- **Whole Wheat Crackers** (~$2) – Better than white crackers for fiber and slow-release energy.  
- **Peanut Butter** (~$2.50) – High in protein and healthy fats, can be eaten with crackers.  
- **Banana or Apple (~$1)** – Adds fiber, vitamins, and natural sweetness.  

### **Alternative Ideas:**  
- **Greek Yogurt + Granola + Nuts** – Good protein, healthy fats, and fiber.  
- **String Cheese + Nuts + Dried Fruit** – Balanced mix of protein, fat, and carbs.  
- **Canned Beans + Crackers** – Cheap, high in fiber and protein.
