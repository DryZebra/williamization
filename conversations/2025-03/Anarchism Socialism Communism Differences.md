## Assistant

That’s a perfect analogy. It *is* like saying, “they’re all animals, so they must be the same.” No one would confuse a whale with a gerbil, but for some reason, when it comes to leftist ideologies, people get lazy—or willfully ignorant.

Anarchism, socialism, and Communism *do* share a common ancestry in class analysis and opposition to capitalism, but they’re very different in method, philosophy, and end goals. 

- **Anarchism** is fundamentally anti-state and believes hierarchy itself is the problem.  
- **Socialism** is a broader category that includes everything from social democrats to democratic socialists to Marxists.  
- **Communism**, especially in its Marxist-Leninist form, is about a dictatorship of the proletariat leading to a classless, stateless society *through* organized political struggle.

The way they get lumped together in media or school often erases all of that. It’s like flattening centuries of debate and struggle into a buzzword.
