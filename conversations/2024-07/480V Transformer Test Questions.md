2. **Answer 3:** 
   - **Definition:** The turns ratio of a transformer is the ratio of the number of turns in the primary winding to the number of turns in the secondary winding. It determines the voltage ratio between the primary and secondary windings.

   - **Answer 5:** 
     - The secondary voltage will be \( 480V / 4 = 120V \).

3. **Answer 6:** 
   - **Definition:** Transformer taps are adjustable connections on the winding that allow for variations in the turns ratio to compensate for voltage variations and maintain a desired output voltage.
