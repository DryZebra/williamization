- **Timing**: Parrying requires precise timing and is more effective against humanoid enemies with predictable attack patterns.

### 4. Shield Skills:

- **Skill Choices**: Equip a shield with a skill that complements your playstyle, such as a parry skill for counter-attacking or a barricade skill for enhanced blocking.

### 5. Combining Shield Use with Attacks:

- **Prioritize Upgrades**: Focus on upgrading shields you plan to use long-term.

2. **Block and Observe**: Block initial attacks to learn the enemy's attack patterns and timing.
3. **Parry or Counterattack**: When confident, parry predictable attacks or counterattack after blocking.
4. **Manage Stamina**: Keep an eye on your stamina bar, letting it recover between blocks and attacks.
