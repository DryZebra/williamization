Determining whether someone has narcissistic traits or a narcissistic personality disorder (NPD) is complex and should be done by a qualified mental health professional. However, I can provide some general information that might help you reflect on your behavior and feelings.

Narcissistic personality disorder is characterized by:

1. **Grandiosity**: Exaggerated sense of self-importance and a need for admiration.
2. **Lack of Empathy**: Difficulty recognizing or identifying with the feelings and needs of others.
3. **Preoccupation with Fantasies**: Obsessing over unlimited success, power, brilliance, beauty, or ideal love.
4. **Sense of Entitlement**: Expecting special treatment or automatic compliance with one’s expectations.
5. **Interpersonal Exploitative Behavior**: Taking advantage of others to achieve one's own ends.
6. **Envy**: Being envious of others or believing others are envious of them.
7. **Arrogance**: Displaying arrogant, haughty behaviors or attitudes.

Everyone exhibits some narcissistic traits occasionally. However, if these traits are persistent, pervasive, and impact your relationships and daily functioning, it might be worth seeking professional help.
