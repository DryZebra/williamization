#### **Project Information**

**Date:**

---

#### **Pre-Work Inspection**

1. **Obtain Work Permit**
   - Ensure all necessary permits and approvals are obtained before starting the work.

2. **Review Electrical Drawings and Diagrams**
   - Verify the circuit to be worked on.
   - Confirm breaker type and specifications.

3. **Inspect Tools and PPE**
   - Check all tools for proper operation.
   - Ensure Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) is available and in good condition:
     - Insulated gloves
     - Safety glasses
     - Arc flash suit
     - Safety shoes
     - Hard hat

4. **Verify Equipment Condition**
   - Inspect the new breaker for any visible damage.
   - Ensure the replacement breaker matches the specifications of the old breaker.

---

#### **Safety Precautions**

1. **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure**
   - Verify the circuit is de-energized.
   - Lock and tag the breaker to prevent accidental energization.
   - Use a multimeter to confirm the absence of voltage.

2. **Set Up Barriers and Signage**
   - Erect barriers around the work area.
   - Place warning signs to inform others of the ongoing work.

3. **Notify Affected Personnel**
   - Inform all relevant personnel about the breaker swap and the expected duration of the work.

---

#### **Breaker Removal Process**

1. **Disconnect the Load**
   - Ensure all connected equipment is safely shut down.

2. **Remove Breaker Cover**
   - Carefully remove the cover of the breaker panel.

3. **Disconnect Wiring**
   - Label all wires connected to the breaker.
   - Use insulated tools to disconnect the wires.

4. **Remove the Old Breaker**
   - Unscrew and carefully remove the old breaker.

---

#### **Breaker Installation Process**

1. **Install the New Breaker**
   - Place the new breaker in the panel.
   - Secure it with screws or clips as required.

2. **Reconnect Wiring**
   - Connect the wires to the new breaker following the labels.
   - Ensure all connections are tight and secure.

3. **Inspect Installation**
   - Double-check all connections.
   - Ensure the breaker is properly seated and aligned.

4. **Replace Breaker Cover**
   - Reattach the cover of the breaker panel.

---

#### **Post-Installation Check**

1. **Remove Lockout/Tagout**
   - Remove the lock and tag after ensuring all personnel are clear.

2. **Test the Breaker**
   - Energize the circuit and test the breaker.
   - Check for proper operation and load handling.

3. **Final Inspection**
   - Perform a final inspection of the work area.
   - Ensure all tools and materials are removed.
   - Verify that the work area is clean and safe.

4. **Notify Affected Personnel**
   - Inform relevant personnel that the breaker swap is complete and the system is back online.

---

#### **Feedback and Notes**

**Issues Encountered:**  
**Improvements Suggested:**

#### **Pre-Work Inspection**

   - Ensure all necessary permits and approvals are obtained before starting the work. Check with your supervisor if you are unsure about the permits required.

2. **Review Electrical Drawings and Diagrams**
   - Verify the circuit to be worked on by carefully reviewing the electrical drawings and diagrams. Confirm that the breaker type and specifications match the project requirements.

3. **Inspect Tools and PPE**
   - Check all tools for proper operation. Ensure that Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as insulated gloves, safety glasses, arc flash suits, safety shoes, and hard hats are available and in good condition.

   - Inspect the new breaker for any visible damage. Ensure that the replacement breaker matches the specifications of the old breaker.

---

#### **Safety Precautions**

1. **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure**
   - Follow the LOTO procedure to ensure the circuit is de-energized. Lock and tag the breaker to prevent accidental energization. Use a multimeter to confirm the absence of voltage.

2. **Set Up Barriers and Signage**
   - Erect barriers around the work area to ensure safety. Place warning signs to inform others of the ongoing work.

3. **Notify Affected Personnel**
   - Inform all relevant personnel about the breaker swap and the expected duration of the work. Ensure everyone is aware of the safety precautions in place.

---

#### **Breaker Removal Process**

1. **Disconnect the Load**
   - Ensure all connected equipment is safely shut down before proceeding.

2. **Remove Breaker Cover**
   - Carefully remove the cover of the breaker panel using the appropriate tools.

3. **Disconnect Wiring**
   - Label all wires connected to the breaker for easy identification. Use insulated tools to disconnect the wires safely.

4. **Remove the Old Breaker**
   - Unscrew and carefully remove the old breaker from the panel.

---

#### **Breaker Installation Process**

1. **Install the New Breaker**
   - Place the new breaker in the panel. Ensure it is properly aligned and secure it with screws or clips as required.

2. **Reconnect Wiring**
   - Connect the wires to the new breaker following the labels. Ensure all connections are tight and secure.

3. **Inspect Installation**
   - Double-check all connections and ensure the breaker is properly seated and aligned.

4. **Replace Breaker Cover**
   - Reattach the cover of the breaker panel securely.

---

#### **Post-Installation Check**

1. **Remove Lockout/Tagout**
   - Remove the lock and tag after ensuring all personnel are clear of the work area.

2. **Test the Breaker**
   - Energize the circuit and test the breaker. Check for proper operation and load handling.

3. **Final Inspection**
   - Perform a final inspection of the work area. Ensure all tools and materials are removed and the area is clean and safe.

4. **Notify Affected Personnel**
   - Inform relevant personnel that the breaker swap is complete and the system is back online.

---
