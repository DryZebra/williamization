Agenda 47, styled as Agenda47 by Donald Trump's campaign, is a set of proposals that Trump aims to implement if elected as the 47th President of the United States. This comprehensive policy plan includes a variety of measures focusing on increasing the power of the executive branch and addressing several key issues according to Trump's platform.

Some notable proposals under Agenda 47 include:

1. **Construction of "Freedom Cities"**: Trump plans to build new cities on federal land to boost innovation and growth.
2. **Investing in Advanced Technologies**: This includes the development of flying cars and other futuristic technologies.
3. **Baby Bonuses**: Financial incentives to encourage higher birth rates.
4. **Protectionist Trade Policies**: Implementing measures to protect American industries from foreign competition.
5. **Ending Birthright Citizenship**: A controversial plan that may face legal challenges.
6. **Death Penalty for Drug Dealers and Human Traffickers**: Increasing penalties for severe crimes.
7. **Deploying the National Guard to High-Crime Areas**: Aimed at reducing crime in major cities.
