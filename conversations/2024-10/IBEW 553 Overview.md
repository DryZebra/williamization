IBEW Local 553 represents electricians in central and northeastern North Carolina, with its headquarters in Durham. The local was established during the Great Depression in 1934 to ensure that electrical workers in the region had proper representation and fair working conditions. Today, it continues its mission to provide highly skilled, safety-conscious labor to its contracting partners while ensuring its members have access to guaranteed wages, health benefits, and retirement plans【6†source】【8†source】.

This program also offers job placement after graduation, making it a strong option for those seeking a career in the electrical industry【9†source】.
