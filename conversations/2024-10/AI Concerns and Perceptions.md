5. **Loss of Control**: The idea of AI becoming so advanced that it operates beyond human control is a common theme that raises fears about safety and governance.

6. **Privacy Concerns**: AI's ability to process and analyze vast amounts of personal data can lead to invasions of privacy if not properly managed.

7. **Cultural Depictions**: Media and entertainment often portray AI in dystopian scenarios, which can influence public perception and amplify fears.
