## Assistant

Many free image-generating AIs are available, but most have some form of filtering due to legal or ethical concerns. Here are a few options that offer decent creative flexibility, though they may still have certain restrictions:

1. **Craiyon (formerly DALL·E mini)**: A free AI that generates images based on text prompts. It's relatively unrestricted but not as high quality as some paid options.
2. **Artbreeder**: Primarily used for creating character portraits and landscapes by mixing and evolving images. It’s more lenient but not fully unfiltered.
3. **Dream by WOMBO**: Free app offering text-to-image generation with various styles. It’s more accessible but with filters for extreme or inappropriate content.
4. **Deep Dream Generator**: Allows you to create surreal images based on existing images, though with some content restrictions.

Unfiltered AI tools are often restricted due to ethical and legal guidelines, so even the more "free" options tend to have some limitations. Paid options, like MidJourney or DALL-E (pro versions), offer better quality but still maintain content filters.
