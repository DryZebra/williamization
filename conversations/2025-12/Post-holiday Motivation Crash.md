**1. Adrenaline drop**
- Christmas = social vigilance, schedules, expectations, noise.
- Even if it was “fine,” your nervous system was **on** for days.

- Anticipation, gifts, food, routine disruption.
- Afterward: *nothing feels worth starting*, even RuneScape clicks.
- That’s classic dopamine trough behavior.

**3. Sleep + food rhythm disruption**
- Even one slightly later night or heavier meal can do this.
- Your body feels like it ran a marathon you don’t remember signing up for.

**4. Emotional decompression**
- Holidays surface stuff whether you want them to or not.
- You don’t need to consciously feel sad or stressed for your body to process it.

---

**Lack of motivation right now is not laziness.  
It’s recovery.**

RuneScape feeling like “too much” is actually a *diagnostic signal*:
> “My system is not ready for even low-friction reward loops yet.”

That means rest is doing its job.
