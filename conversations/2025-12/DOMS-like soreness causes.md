# DOMS-like soreness causes

## User

Why does it feel like I have DOMS some mornings?

## Assistant



## Assistant

There are **five very normal, very physical reasons** you can wake up with DOMS-like soreness even when you didn’t “work out” the day before. And all of them match your lifestyle, your training pattern, and your day-to-day physical load.

---

# **1. You *are* training—your body just logs it differently**
You’re marathon-training, doing daily miles, push-ups, electrician work (constant ladders, kneeling, carrying weight, overhead strain).  
Your body doesn’t separate it:

- Long days on your feet  
- Steady muscular tension  
- Climbing/descending  
- Carrying tools  
- Stress hormones  

All of this **creates micro-trauma exactly like a workout.**  
You *feel* it the next morning because sleep is when recovery goes full throttle, inflammation rises slightly, and those tissues signal.

---

# **2. Sleep posture = low-grade eccentric load**
If you curl up, twist at the spine, or keep shoulders protracted, your body holds an isometric contraction **for hours**.

Examples:

- Sleeping with one hip higher  
- Shoulder rolled forward under a pillow  
- Low-key neck tension  
- Locked knees or curled toes  
- Back partly twisted

Isometric contraction for hours = DOMS sensation.

---

# **3. Delayed recovery from long-term overuse**
You’ve been stacking:

- Hal Higdon Novice 2 mileage  
- Push-up progression  
- Electrical work (physical labor)  
- Running transition (barefoot form, using new muscles)  
- Weight loss and metabolic shift

This creates a **constant low-level backlog of repair**, so your soreness doesn’t map neatly to yesterday.  
It maps to the *accumulated stress of the week.*

Think of it as your body paying off recovery debt in small installments.

---

# **4. Hydration, electrolytes, and sleep timing**
Even minor fluctuations change how soreness feels the next morning.  
DOMS is partly inflammatory signaling, and things like:

- Slight dehydration  
- Low magnesium/potassium  
- Eating late  
- Sleeping hot  

…all amplify the pain signals.

Given your training and long workdays, **you burn through electrolytes fast**.

---

# **5. Cortisol patterns**
In the morning, cortisol naturally spikes (the *cortisol awakening response*).  
If you were:

- stressed  
- underslept  
- emotionally taxed  
- overtrained  
- physically fatigued  

…the cortisol spike “lights up” tissues that are slightly inflamed from regular use.

Nothing is wrong.  
Your body is just honest with you when you first wake up.

---

# **So is this bad?**

**No.**  
Waking up with mild DOMS-like soreness is common in:

- runners  
- manual laborers  
- people transitioning to barefoot form  
- people doing push-up progression  
- people losing weight  
- people with high emotional load + physical load (you)

It usually means:

- You’re adapting  
- You’re using muscles more evenly  
- You’re clearing accumulated tension  
- You’re within a normal recovery window

If the soreness *goes away* after 5–20 minutes of moving around, it’s **not pathological**—it’s just recovery finishing its job.
