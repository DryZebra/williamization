## Assistant

Short answer: **no, don’t spend 16k on an adamant platebody right now**—especially not as an iron with a thin bank.

Here’s why, in *system* terms, not efficiency-brain terms.

---

## 1. What are you actually buying with an addy platebody?

Compared to an **adamant chainbody** or even a **mithril platebody**, the upgrade is *tiny*:

- **Defense difference**: very small
- **Damage reduction**: barely noticeable at your combat level
- **Kill speed / survivability**: almost unchanged
- **Unlocks**: none

You already have:
- Addy helm
- Addy legs
- Addy kiteshield (which *is* a meaningful upgrade)

So your **core defensive value is already there**. The platebody is the *least impactful* addy slot.

Spending **most of your bank** for a marginal +def bump is a bad trade.

---

## 2. Wilderness zombie pirates are a trap for where you are

Yes, zombie pirates drop addy plates.

But:
- They’re in the **Wilderness**
- Guarded by **level 123 chaos druids**
- Risk of PKers
- You’ll need food, prayer, attention, and luck

That’s not “frontier man gameplay,” that’s **overreaching the system**.  
RuneScape *will* punish that.

If you lose gear or supplies, you’re set back harder than the platebody ever helped you.

---

## 3. The ironman truth: you’re about to outgrow adamant anyway

This is the key insight.

At ~40s combat stats, the *real* armor progression isn’t:
> Addy plate → full addy → chill

It’s actually:
- **Rune** (from quests, shops, drops)
- **Proselyte** (huge prayer value)
- **Barrows** (later, but inevitable)

You will **replace that addy platebody surprisingly fast**, long before it “pays itself back” in usefulness.

So spending 16k now is like buying premium tires for a truck you’re about to sell.

---

## 4. What you should do instead (better uses of that 16k)

Here are **objectively stronger investments** for *your exact position*:

### ✅ Stick with:
- Mithril platebody **or**
- Adamant chainbody (if you can get one)

You lose almost nothing.

### ✅ Spend money on:
- **Food buffer** (so you can take risks safely)
- **Teleport runes** (this is huge for irons)
- **Quest requirements** that unlock *real* power
- **Skill unlocks** (crafting, magic, prayer, agility)

Those *multiply* your account instead of slightly padding one stat.

---

## 5. The correct ironman instinct (and you already have it)

You said:
> “Most of my bank”

That alone tells you the answer.

If a single piece of mid-tier gear empties your bank, **it’s not time for that gear yet**.

Ironman progression isn’t about having the best thing *available*.
It’s about having the best thing that **fits the system you can sustain**.

Right now:
- Your system supports **mith + partial addy**
- It does *not* support luxury purchases or wildy gambles

And that’s perfectly fine.

---

## Final verdict

**Do not buy the adamant platebody.**
- Keep your bank
- Keep your momentum
- Let the platebody come naturally later, or skip it entirely
