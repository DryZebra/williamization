## 5. Keeping dust from murdering your next phone

No matter what you buy, dust is your real enemy. Here’s what I’d actually do for *any* phone you pick:

1. **Port plugs / dust caps**
   - Tiny silicone USB-C port plugs are cheap. If you charge wirelessly most of the time (Moto G Power 5G can), you can leave a plug in almost permanently.

2. **Wireless charging = save the port**
   - If you choose a phone with wireless charging (like Moto G Power 5G 2024), use:
     - A **cheap Qi pad** at home / bedside  
     - A **wireless charging mount** in the truck  
   - That way, the USB-C port only gets used in emergencies.

3. **Rugged case with port flaps**
   - For job-site use, grab a case with **sealed flaps over USB-C and headphone jack** (OtterBox Defender, Supcase, Poetic, etc. – there are always knockoff versions for Motos and A-series Samsungs).
   - This is a *huge* reduction in dust infiltration into ports and mics.

4. **Holster instead of pocket**
   - Dust + lint in jeans pockets is brutal on ports. A belt holster (yeah, boomer style, but practical) keeps the phone out of the worst grime and impact zones.

5. **Compressed air / toothpick day**
   - Once a week:  
     - Gently scrape the port with a wooden toothpick (not metal).  
     - Blast mics/speakers with canned air.  
   - This alone extends mic/speaker lifespan a ton.
