## Which is worse to train to 99?

### 🔮 Magic — **Easier overall**
Magic looks intimidating on paper, but in practice it has **multiple passive, profitable, or utility-driven paths** that make 99 very manageable over the life of an Ironman.

Why Magic is easier long-term:
- **Teleport XP is free XP** you were going to get anyway
- **High Alch** converts junk into value while training
- **Enchanting** bolts/jewelry stacks XP with crafting goals
- **Burst/Barrage Slayer** turns combat training into insane XP later
- **Mage Arena / God spells / utility casting** all give progress-based XP
- Endgame runes become self-sustaining through GotR, RC, drops, and shops

Magic XP sneaks up on you. Many irons “accidentally” end up with 90+ Magic just by playing correctly.

Magic is *front-loaded annoying*, but **back-loaded trivial**.

---

### 🏹 Ranged — **The real long-term pain**
Ranged is deceptively rough on Ironmen, especially if you’re not doing GE-style buy-and-burn.

Why Ranged is worse:
- **Ammo production bottleneck**
  - Feathers, darts, bolts, tips, bars, scales
- **Gear progression is slow**
  - No easy equivalent of “just cast better spells”
- **Chinchompas are late and limited**
- **Slayer Ranged XP is slower than Magic**
- **Bossing expects high Ranged**, but doesn’t always *train* it efficiently
- You don’t get passive Ranged XP from utility the way Magic does

Most irons hit late game thinking:
> “Why is my Ranged still 87 when everything else is 95+?”

That’s normal.

---

## Lamps: what actually pays off

### ✅ Use combat lamps on **Ranged**
Especially:
- Early–mid game lamps
- Achievement diary lamps
- Any “free combat XP” sources

Each lamp saves:
- Ammo creation time
- Supply drain
- Future frustration when bosses assume 90+ Ranged

### ❌ Don’t waste lamps on Magic
Magic:
- Will level itself
- Has intentional XP sinks that *benefit you*
- Is tied to progression systems you *want* to do anyway

---

That same design philosophy applies here.

Magic is like Fishing:
- Multiple routes

- Systems reward non-EHP play

Ranged is like old-school power fishing:
- Linear
- Resource hungry
- Punishing if ignored too long

So you hedge against that future wall now.

## Final recommendation (Ironman-correct)
