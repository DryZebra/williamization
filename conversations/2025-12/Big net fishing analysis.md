## Short answer (the blunt truth)

**Big net fishing is almost entirely dead content for modern play.**  
For an Ironman who thinks in systems, **skipping it is usually correct**.

You are not missing a hidden payoff loop.

---

## Why it *feels* broken (because it is)

You’re comparing:

- **Small net**
  - Shrimp: 10 xp
  - Anchovies: 40 xp
  - Clean inventory
  - Fast turnover

vs.

- **Big net**
  - Mackerel: 20 xp (worse than anchovies)
  - Cod/Bass: fine but not amazing
  - Inventory flooded with:
    - Seaweed (1 xp)
    - Boots/gloves (1 xp)
    - Oysters (1 xp)
    - Junk
  - Bream **not catchable**
  - Slower XP per action
  - More friction

Your instinct is right: **the opportunity cost is awful**.

---

## What big net fishing was *originally* for

Big net fishing made sense in **early RuneScape** because:

1. **Food scarcity mattered**
   - Bass was good food once
   - Lobsters weren’t trivial yet

2. **XP rates were low everywhere**
   - 20 xp vs 40 xp wasn’t a dealbreaker

3. **“Junk” wasn’t junk**
   - Seaweed mattered before sand was trivial
   - Oysters/pearls were novelty content
   - Collection-style thinking didn’t exist

4. **AFK wasn’t solved**
   - Big net was “do something while chatting”

That ecosystem is gone.

---

## Why it doesn’t fit modern Ironman systems

Modern OSRS systems reward **one of three things**:

1. **High XP per action**
2. **Critical resource loops**
3. **Unique unlocks**

Big net does **none** of these well.

### 1. XP?
Bad.

Anchovies beat it.
Barb fishing annihilates it.
Tempoross humiliates it.

### 2. Resources?
Seaweed is:
- Easier to gather later
- Stackable
- Faster via farming / underwater
- Not worth early friction

Boots/gloves are:
- Almost useless
- Only relevant for Foundry much later
- Not worth the fishing time

### 3. Unlocks?
None.

No diary gate.
No unique.
No permanent perk.
No equipment.
No minigame synergy.

You do big net **because you already want pearls**, not because big net is good fishing.

## Why your “skip it” instinct is correct

For an Ironman building systems:

- **Fishing progression is solved**:
  - Shrimp/anchovies → trout/salmon → lobster → swordfish → shark
- **XP efficiency is solved**:
  - Barb fishing + drop
  - Tempoross
- **Cooking solves itself**
- **Seaweed is better later**
- **Coins are trivial**
- **Feathers are cheap**

Big net adds **friction without leverage**.

That’s the cardinal sin in OSRS design.
