Your framing is exactly right:

### **Lecture (abstract)**
- Introduces the concepts  
- Gives the mental map  
- Creates the schema  
- Lets you talk about the *why*, not just the *how*

### **Field (real)**
- Applies the concept under pressure  
- Forces adaptation  
- Teaches politics, culture, and safety  
- Forms class consciousness  
- Forges identity

### **Combined:**  
> **Real apprenticeship** = idea + world + relationship + motion.

A perfect lab dissolves the contradiction.  
And when contradiction dissolves, development stops.
