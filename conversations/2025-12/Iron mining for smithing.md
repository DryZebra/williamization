## What you’re trying to unlock (and why 30 is the real breakpoint)
- You can *start* Sleeping Giants / Giant’s Foundry with **15 Smithing**, but… citeturn4search10turn0search23  
- At **30 Smithing**, you unlock **steel** (and iron/steel) alloys at Giant’s Foundry. That matters because MLM gives you **coal**, and coal + iron = steel, which becomes your first “MLM → Foundry” loop that actually feeds itself. citeturn2search6

So: **yes, aim for 30 Smithing** (not just “minimum to enter”).

## Can you “just do MLM gold” instead of iron?
No, not for this specific problem.
- **Gold bars require 40 Smithing** to smelt at a furnace. citeturn2search4  
- MLM also doesn’t even start giving gold ore until **40 Mining**. citeturn2search22  

So MLM gold can’t replace your **20 → 30 Smithing** bridge.

## The exact calculation: 20 → 30 Smithing with iron scimitars (no ring)
XP milestones:
- Level 20 XP: **4,470**
- Level 30 XP: **13,363**
- XP needed: **13,363 − 4,470 = 8,893** citeturn3view0

Each iron scimitar “cycle” looks like this:
- Smelt **2 iron ores** into **2 iron bars**  
  - Each successful smelt gives **12.5 Smithing XP** citeturn4search1  
  - So smelting 2 successful bars = **25 XP**
- Smith **1 iron scimitar** (uses 2 bars) for **50 XP** citeturn0search25  
- Total per completed scimitar = **25 + 50 = 75 XP**

Scimitars required:
- **8,893 / 75 = 118.57… → 119 scimitars**

Bars required:
- 119 scimitars × 2 bars each = **238 iron bars**

### Now the painful part: ore required without ring of forging
Iron smelting is **50% success**, and a failure deletes the ore with **0 XP**. citeturn2search24turn4search1

So to *expect* 238 bars:
- Expected ores = **238 / 0.5 = 476 iron ore**

✅ **Answer (expected / “math exact”): Mine and bank 476 iron ore.**

### What I’d actually tell a Hardcore to do
Because you can go dry (no upper bound), you want a buffer so you don’t have to run back later:

If you end up with extra bars, great—those become your first steel batch once MLM starts feeding you coal.

## Should you go higher than 30 before “parking at MLM”?
Mining at MLM doesn’t care about Smithing, so you can “park” there whenever. But for your **Foundry loop quality**, 30 is the first meaningful breakpoint because it enables **steel** at Foundry. citeturn2search6

If you want a *slightly smoother* early Foundry experience, pushing to the next milestone **after 30** is more about convenience than necessity. The big milestones for Foundry alloys are:  
- **30 Smithing:** Steel + Iron/Steel  
- **50 Smithing:** Steel/Mithril  
- **70 Smithing:** Mithril/Adamantite  
- **85 Smithing:** Mithril/Runite or Adamant/Runite citeturn2search6  

But you don’t need to pre-plan those right now; your immediate bottleneck is getting a steel-capable Foundry loop online.

Since you’re already **20 Smithing**, completing it would shove you **past 30** with almost no mining—meaning your “Varrock East iron power-mine” step basically disappears.
