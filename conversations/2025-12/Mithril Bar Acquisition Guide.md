## Assistant

Short answer: **don’t grind 50 Smithing for one mithril bar.** That’s a trap for an iron at your stage.

Here’s the clean breakdown, time-wise and sanity-wise.

---

## Best use of your time (ranked)

### **1. Burthorpe Warriors – do this**
This is honestly why they exist.

- **Combat:** Perfect for your stats (40ish melee / 37 range)
- **Drops:**  
  - **Mithril bars**  
  - **Mithril ore**  
- **Speed:** You’ll usually get what you need in **10–30 minutes**
- **Bonus:** No annoying mechanics, no dead drops, no wilderness, no keys

If you just need **one bar**, this is the most *ironman-correct* answer.

> Kill them until you either get the bar directly or an ore you can smelt.

---

### **2. Buy mithril ore → smelt it (if unlocked)**
If you’ve unlocked access:

- **Blast Furnace ore shop** sells mithril ore
- Buy **1 mithril ore + coal**
- Smelt it normally (no BF required)

This is slightly less “pure” but still completely valid and faster than grinding Smithing levels.

---

### **3. Skeletal Fremennik – acceptable but slower**
You already clocked the issue:

- Drops are okay
- Kill speed is slower
- More effort per roll

Fine if you’re already there, **not optimal** if you’re starting from scratch.

---

### **4. Chaos Dwarves – skip**
You’re right to avoid them.

- Muddy key nonsense
- Mostly junk
- Bad time/value ratio for a single bar

---

### **5. Grinding 47 → 50 Smithing – absolutely not**
Let’s be blunt:

- Hundreds of iron + coal
- Several hours minimum
- You gain **nothing meaningful** except access to one bar you’ll never care about again

That grind makes sense **only** if:
- You already planned to push Smithing anyway  
- Or you’re feeding Giant’s Foundry / Blast Furnace long-term  

For a quest gate? No.

---

## Recommendation (clear answer)
**Go kill Burthorpe Warriors until you get a mithril bar or ore.**  
That’s the best use of your time by a wide margin.
