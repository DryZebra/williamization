## Assistant

Yep — you’re **not crazy** for thinking *Excalibur* can feel *pretty decent* early on as an Ironman weapon in **Old School RuneScape (OSRS)**, and other players do talk about it — **but with a few important caveats**.

### 🗡️ What *Excalibur* Actually Is
- It’s a **reward from the *Merlin’s Crystal* quest**, requiring **20 Attack** to wield.  
- Stat-wise it’s basically like an **Adamant longsword** with *slightly worse strength bonus* (so decent early damage for its level).  
- It also has a **special attack, *Sanctuary***, that temporarily boosts your **Defence** — useful for survivability on low-gear Ironmen. citeturn0search7

### 👍 Why Some Ironmen Like It Early
- **Free to obtain** (quest reward) and **re-obtainable** if lost, so you don’t have to rely on Grand Exchange/trading. citeturn0search7  
- **Better stats than bronze/iron/steel weapons** you’d otherwise use around **20 Attack**, so it **feels like an upgrade** for early-ish combat.  
- The **Defence buff** from *Sanctuary* is actually *handy* for staying alive against early monsters when you have low armor.

So yes — in those early low-level Ironman days, a lot of players treat it as a **solid early-game choice** simply because it’s an **upgrade over rusty gear** and helps survivability.

### ⚠️ Why Most Players Don’t Treat It as *BiS*
Most Ironman gear guides and community discussions don’t list Excalibur as a “must-grind future weapon” for melee progression. Instead players talk about moving toward better options faster:  
- **Scimitars / faster weapons** (higher DPS) like Rune Scimitar once you can access them. citeturn0search2  
- Quest and task rewards that give **stronger weapons earlier** with better stats/speeds.  
- Later-game weapons that notably outclass Excalibur (like Dragonscape gear, Godswords, etc.).

A subreddit thread mentions the *enhanced Excalibur* (the upgrade later obtainable) being used for its **healing effect** — showing that **the community does value the weapon’s utility at certain points**, just not as a long-term best weapon. citeturn0search1

### 🧠 TL;DR
**Yes, other players agree:**  
- *Excalibur* feels *good early* for Ironmen because it’s **free and better than other options at the ~20 Attack range**, and its **special attack helps survival**.  
- **No, it’s not generally considered a competitive weapon long-term** — most players soon switch to faster and stronger weapons as soon as they can get them.  

If you’re enjoying it in your Ironman run **early on**, that’s a totally valid and common experience!
