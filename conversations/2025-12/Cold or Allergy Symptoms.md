# Cold or Allergy Symptoms

## Assistant

Yeah — that doesn’t sound like a “social hangover.” What you’re describing lines up much more with an **acute upper-respiratory thing** than burnout alone.

Based on your symptoms:

- **Runny nose + constant sneezing** → classic viral or allergic response  
- **Congestion + headache** → sinus involvement / inflammation  
- **Lethargy** → immune system pulling energy to fight something  

That combo is very typical of:
- a **common cold** (most likely),
- a **post-holiday virus** (very common right now),
- or **allergies that tipped into sinus inflammation** (especially if weather shifted).

### Why it *feels* worse than it “should”
Even mild infections can feel brutal when:
- you just came off **high stimulation** (holidays = social + sensory overload),
- sleep rhythm got disrupted,
- stress hormones drop suddenly → immune system “takes the wheel.”

That “I feel poisoned and useless” feeling is very real and very physical.

### What to do *today*
Keep it simple and mechanical:

**1. Rest without guilt**
This is not laziness. Your body is reallocating resources. Fighting it makes symptoms linger.

**2. Fluids, but warm**
- Warm water, tea, broth.
- Cold water doesn’t help congestion much.

**3. Clear the sinuses**
If you have access to any of these:
- hot shower (steam),
- saline spray,
- humidifier,
they’ll directly reduce the headache pressure.

**4. Light food**
Soups, toast, eggs. Don’t force appetite, just don’t go empty.

**5. Avoid “pushing through”**
RuneScape brain-fog + sickness = frustration spiral. If you play, do AFK-level stuff only.

### When to watch more closely
Get more cautious if you develop:
- fever over ~101°F,
- facial pain that’s sharp or one-sided,
- thick yellow/green mucus lasting several days,
- chest tightness or shortness of breath.

Right now, though, this sounds **annoying but normal**, not dangerous.

### One important thing
You didn’t *fail* today.  
Your body just called in sick before your mind accepted it.

If you want, tell me:
- did this come on suddenly overnight or creep up over a day or two?
- any sore throat or body aches?

That helps narrow whether this is pure cold vs sinus vs allergy-triggered inflammation.
