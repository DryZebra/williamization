## 🧠 Core Loop: Grind vs. Action Progression

### **OSRS: Skill & Grind-Driven RPG**
- **Progression comes from repetitive tasks** in skills like Fishing, Woodcutting, Combat, Prayer, etc.

- Many players enjoy it because it’s *systematic*, predictable, and deep in optimization/grind strategy.

**Grind example:** Chop logs → get XP → make money → train next skill.

## 🎮 Gameplay Feel: Action vs Systems

### **OSRS**  
- Mostly menu + click interactions  
- Slow pacing  
- Very tactical in **resource management, quests, and macro planning**

### **Wilds**
- Fast-paced, **skill-based combat**
- Dodging and timing matter in real time  
- Each monster fight feels like an *event*, not a checkbox task  
- You’re learning **patterns**, not grinding arbitrary walls

So while both have loops that *could* be called “grinding,” **Wilds’ grind is more about mastering mechanics and upgrading gear to *tackle specific challenges*—not just stacking numbers.**  
(That’s closer to how an MMO progression feels, but still fundamentally action-oriented.)
