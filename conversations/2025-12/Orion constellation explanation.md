## Assistant

That’s **Orion**.

You can tell by the classic pattern:
- **Two bright stars at the top** (the shoulders),
- **Three stars in a straight line across the middle** (Orion’s Belt),
- **One very bright star at the bottom** (the foot, often identified as Rigel).

It’s one of the easiest constellations to recognize because that belt line is so distinctive, and many star charts (including game or low-resolution ones like this) exaggerate those features to make it obvious.

If this came from a game or puzzle (OSRS Observatory vibes), Orion is almost always one of the “gimme” constellations for exactly that reason.
