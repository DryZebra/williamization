## Assistant

**Short answer:**  
Pearl bolts are a **niche, mostly-dead content item**. Their only real use case is **early-to-mid game Ironmen fighting Water-typed NPCs**, and even there they’re usually worse than easier alternatives.

---

## What Pearl Bolts actually do
- **Require:** 41 Ranged, crossbow
- **Special effect:** Extra damage against **water-based creatures**
- **How you get them:** Pearl bolt tips from **Oysters** (Fishing Trawler / underwater areas), then fletched onto bolts

On paper, they’re the *water-slayer bolt*.

---

## Intended targets (and why it doesn’t matter much)
Pearl bolts are supposed to be good against:
- **Dagannoths**
- **Waterfiends**
- **Kraken tentacles**
- **Sea trolls**
- Miscellaneous aquatic NPCs

In practice:
- These enemies are **either weak already**, or
- You’ll be using **better ammo by the time you fight them seriously**

---

## Why Pearl bolts are basically obsolete

### 1. **Broad bolts outclass them**
- **Broad bolts** are cheap, strong, and universal
- No niche condition
- Available very early for Ironmen

If broad bolts exist, Pearl bolts lose almost all relevance.

---

### 2. **Bone bolts beat them early**
For low-level Ironmen:
- Bone bolts + Dorgeshuun crossbow
- Easier to get
- Comparable or better DPS for early combat

---

### 3. **By the time they “should” matter, you’re past them**
- Dagannoths → you’re soon on slayer with better gear
- Waterfiends → you’ll want **better Ranged or Magic**
- Kraken → **Magic is king**, not Ranged

---

### 4. **The acquisition cost is annoying**
- Fishing Trawler / underwater RNG
- Fletching time
- For a payoff that is… barely noticeable

Ironman logic always asks:
> “Does this save me time or resources later?”

Pearl bolts fail that test.

---

## The *only* real use case (and it’s still weak)
**Early Ironman, low gear, killing Dagannoths with a crossbow**, *before* unlocking broad bolts.

Even then:
- You’ll replace them quickly
- You don’t need them
- They’re more of a curiosity than a solution
