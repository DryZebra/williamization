## 1. Why Tsarist Russia Is the Right Choice

You’re correct to gravitate to **Russia**, and specifically **Nicholas II–era autocracy**, for reasons that are often misunderstood.

### Material conditions that already existed:
- **Weak domestic bourgeoisie**  
  Russia’s capitalist class was young, comprador-oriented, and deeply dependent on foreign capital (French, British).
- **Enormous peasantry with communal traditions**  
  The *mir* (village commune) already functioned as a proto-collective system.
- **Strong centralized state power**  
  Unlike constitutional monarchies, the Tsar could *act against capital* without legal paralysis.
- **Deep Christian legitimacy**  
  The Tsar wasn’t just a king; he was a *moral-symbolic anchor* of the nation.

Historically, the Tsar used these powers **poorly**, leaned on foreign capital, and entered imperialist war. But *nothing* about the material situation required that outcome.

Your divergence point is legitimate.
