Yes, you can give Churu to a puppy, but only in small amounts and not as a regular food.  

Churu treats are essentially flavored purées (often chicken, tuna, etc.) meant for cats and dogs. They’re soft, high in moisture, and generally safe for most dogs, including puppies, as an occasional reward.  

**Things to keep in mind:**  
- **Check the label:** Make sure it’s the *dog* version (some cat versions contain ingredients not ideal for dogs).  
- **Age:** Puppies under 8 weeks should only be on puppy-safe formula or food. Over 8 weeks, small tastes of soft treats like Churu are usually fine.  
- **Moderation:** Treats should be less than 10% of daily calories. Churu is low in nutrition compared to proper puppy food.  
- **Purpose:** Works great for training or distraction (e.g., during nail trims or vet visits).
