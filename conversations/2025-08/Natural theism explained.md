# Natural theism explained

## User

What is a natural theist?

## Assistant



## Assistant

A **natural theist** is someone who believes in God (or a divine being) based primarily on *reason, experience, and observation of the natural world* rather than on revelation, scripture, or organized religion.  

It’s closely related to **natural theology** and **deism**, though not identical:

- **Natural theology** is the tradition of arguing for God’s existence and attributes through logic and observation (e.g., the design of the universe, moral order, causality).
- **Deism** often goes further, holding that God exists but does not intervene in the world beyond creation.
- **Natural theism** is broader—it simply means the belief in God justified by natural reason, not necessarily excluding the possibility of revelation or miracles.

So, a natural theist might say: *“I believe in God because the universe shows design, order, and moral structure—not because of scripture or church authority.”*
