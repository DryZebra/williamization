*Queer as Folk* was a groundbreaking TV drama about the lives of gay men and women. There are actually two versions:  

### 🇬🇧 The Original (UK, 1999)  
- Created by **Russell T Davies** (who later revived *Doctor Who*).  
- Ran for 2 short seasons.  
- Set in Manchester, focused mainly on three gay men navigating relationships, work, and family.  
- Known for being one of the first TV shows to portray gay life frankly and unapologetically.  

### 🇺🇸 The Remake (US/Canada, 2000–2005)  
- A longer, more soap-opera style series.  
- Ran for **5 seasons** on Showtime.  
- Set in Pittsburgh (but filmed in Toronto).  
- Expanded cast of characters: friends, partners, lesbians raising kids, people dealing with HIV, and workplace discrimination.  
- The initials **QAF** usually refer to this version when written on tapes from the early 2000s.  

It was pretty controversial back then because it showed things on TV that mainstream audiences weren’t used to seeing — but also really important for representation.
