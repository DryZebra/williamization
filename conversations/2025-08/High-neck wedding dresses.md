Designers often describe them with terms like:  
- **High-neck gown** – a general category for dresses that cover the collarbone and neck.  
- **Mock-neck wedding dress** – similar to a turtleneck but without the fold-over.  
- **Turtleneck wedding dress** – specifically when it has that structured, full-coverage neckline.
