- Signup: minimal, sometimes just email.  
- Spam rating: ⚠️ Medium–High. Used for throwaway Craigslist/romance scams.  

---

### 4. **Sideline / TextPlus / FreeTone**
- Freemium (free tier w/ ads, better features paid).  
- Minimal signup.  
- Spam rating: ⚠️ Medium. Abused, but less than TextNow.  

---

## 🚩 Why Spammers Love TextNow
- It’s free.  
- It’s disposable (abandon and make a new one in minutes).  
- It looks like a “real” US mobile number in carrier lookups.  
- No strong ID verification.  

---
