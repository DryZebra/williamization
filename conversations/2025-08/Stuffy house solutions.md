You’re probably both right in a way.  

A house can feel *stuffy* for a few reasons:  
- **Odors trapped inside** – dog pee definitely releases ammonia and other compounds that linger and can make the air feel heavy or sharp.  
- **Poor air circulation** – if windows haven’t been opened much or the HVAC filter is old, stale air builds up.  
- **Humidity** – higher humidity traps smells and makes the air feel heavier.  

Since you already suspect the puppy pee, that’s a big factor. Even small accidents soak into carpet, wood, or baseboards and continue releasing odors. A guest (like your girlfriend) will notice it more strongly than you, because you may have gone a bit nose-blind to it.  

**Quick fixes you can try tonight:**  
1. **Enzyme cleaner on pee spots** – regular cleaners just mask odor; enzyme sprays break down the urine compounds.  
2. **Open windows for cross-ventilation** – even 15 minutes helps refresh the air.  
3. **Change or clean HVAC filters** – dusty filters hold onto smell and reduce circulation.  
4. **Baking soda bowls or sprinkle on carpet** – absorbs odor while you work.
