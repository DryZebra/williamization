# Mail Lien Application

## User


Where do I mail this?

## Assistant



## Assistant

You should mail the **Lien Recording Application** to the **North Carolina Division of Motor Vehicles (NC DMV)**, **not** to the lienholder (like the bank).

### Here's where to send it:

**NC Division of Motor Vehicles**  
**Vehicle Titles/Liens**  
**3148 Mail Service Center**  
**Raleigh, NC 27697-3148**

---

### What to include:
- This completed **MVR-6** form  
- The **original certificate of title** (unless it's held by a prior lienholder)  
- Any required **fees** (check the current DMV fee schedule or call to confirm if needed)  

You may want to use **certified mail** or a service with **tracking** to ensure delivery.

Let me know if you need help confirming the current lien recording fee or checking the title status.

The price of a **VHS to digital converter** depends on the type and quality you're looking for:

### 🔹 **Budget Options ($20–$50)**
These typically use a USB capture card and RCA (red, white, yellow) cables:
- **Examples**: 
  - ClearClick Video to Digital Converter 2.0 (~$40–$50)
  - UCEC USB 2.0 Video Capture Card (~$20–$30)
- **Pros**: Cheap, plug-and-play
- **Cons**: May require separate VCR, lower quality, more setup effort

### 🔹 **Mid-Range All-in-One Devices ($80–$150)**
These record directly from VHS to SD card or USB, no computer required:
- **Examples**:
  - DIGITNOW Video Capture Converter (~$80–$100)
  - ClearClick HD Video Capture Box (~$120–$150)
- **Pros**: Easier to use, often better video quality
- **Cons**: Still need a working VCR to plug into

### 🔹 **Professional Solutions ($200+)**
Used by archivists or studios for higher quality:
- **Examples**: Elgato Video Capture (used with high-end VCR), or services that digitize tapes for you
- **Pros**: Better quality, software support
- **Cons**: Expensive, sometimes overkill for home use

---

If you already have a working **VCR**, a simple USB converter (~$25–$40) is usually enough for personal use. Let me know what setup you have, and I can recommend one.
