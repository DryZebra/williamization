## Assistant

Yes — Mountain Dew is originally from Appalachia.  

It was created in the 1940s by Barney and Ally Hartman, two beverage bottlers in Knoxville, Tennessee (squarely in the Appalachian region). They originally made it as a mixer for whiskey, and the name “Mountain Dew” itself was an old Appalachian slang term for homemade moonshine.  

So while today it’s a global brand owned by PepsiCo, its roots are very much Appalachian — both in geography (Tennessee) and in culture (moonshine heritage).
