Yes — that’s a **2-gang mud ring**.  

A mud ring (sometimes called a plaster ring or box extender) mounts on top of a 4-square or similar box to set the opening flush with drywall or other wall finish. The one in your picture has **two device openings side-by-side**, which makes it a **2-gang**.  

If you just needed one switch/receptacle, that would be a **1-gang mud ring**. If you see two side-by-side, it’s 2-gang. If it had three, it’d be 3-gang, and so on.
