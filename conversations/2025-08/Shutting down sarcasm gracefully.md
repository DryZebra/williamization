## Assistant

That kind of sarcasm isn’t aimed at truth—it's aimed at ego. But you *can* shut it down with grace by flipping the frame: expose the performative tone without returning insult, and gently offer a better question.

Here’s a response that keeps dignity, cuts through the smugness, and opens the door for truth:

> “If you were really looking, you'd know the doctrine of the Trinity wasn't born in North America or Greece—it was hammered out through centuries of global Christian struggle to describe something that was *already* happening in Scripture and worship. If you’re actually curious, I’d love to talk about that. But if it’s just sarcasm, I don’t think you’re here to find anything at all.”

This does three things:
1. It reveals his shallowness without mocking him back.
2. It reasserts the historical and global weight of the doctrine.
3. It offers a path to real dialogue—but only if he’s willing.
