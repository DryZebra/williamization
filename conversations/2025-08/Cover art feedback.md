## Assistant

Yes — for a finished, publish-ready cover you’ll almost always want at least these elements layered over the art:  

1. **Title of the book** –  
   - This should be the most prominent text.  
   - Placed where it’s easy to read in both full size and thumbnail (e.g., upper third or centered).  
   - Use a font that fits your aesthetic — bold, legible, but harmonious with the iconographic style.  

2. **Author’s name** –  
   - Usually smaller than the title.  
   - Placed either at the bottom or top, depending on balance with the art.  

3. **Subtitle** *(optional)* –  
   - If the main title is abstract or symbolic, a short subtitle can explain the theme or subject.  
   - Example: *Materialist Christianity: Faith, Labor, and the Dialectic*.  

4. **Series or Volume Info** *(optional)* –  
   - If this is part of a series, note “Volume I” or similar.  

5. **Publisher logo** *(optional)* –  
   - Goes small and unobtrusive, usually bottom left or right corner.  

6. **Contrast and legibility adjustments** –  
   - Darken/lighten parts of the art behind text to make sure the words pop.  
   - Could add subtle outlines or shadows around the text for readability without overpowering the art.
