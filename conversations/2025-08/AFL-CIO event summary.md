1. ARTICLE IV - MEMBERSHIP AND DELEGATES 
 
SECTION 1.  Membership in the North Carolina State AFL-CIO 
shall consist exclusively of such of the following organizations 
within the geographical limits of North Carolina as shall conform 
to the membership requirements of this organization:  (1) local 
unions directly affiliated to the AFL-CIO and local unions of 
national and international unions and organizing committees 
affiliated with the AFL-CIO;  (2) Central Labor Councils/Area 
Labor Federations chartered by and in good standing with the 
AFL-CIO;  (3) local, district and state councils chartered by an 
international union or by a trade or industrial department of the 
AFL-CIO; (4) to the extent that participation is authorized by the 
AFL-CIO Executive Council, subordinate bodies of the Alliance for 
Retired Americans shall be permitted to affiliate; (5) State 
chapters of AFL-CIO constituency groups, as defined in the Rules 
Governing AFL-CIO State Central Bodies, that are chartered by a 
national AFL-CIO constituency group.  If a constituency group has 
one or more chapters in the state, but no statewide chapter, a 
local chapter designated by the national constituency group may 
be admitted as an affiliate; and (6) local unions with solidarity 
charters authorized by the Executive Council of the AFL-CIO, for 
so long as the charter remains in effect. 
 
Chapters may be created if the state federation and Central 
Labor Council/ Area Labor Federation determine that an 
organizational presence is needed in a specific geographic area 
within its jurisdiction. Chapters shall have no right of 
representation or involvement in the affairs of the state 
federation or Central Labor Council/Area Labor Federation.  
Chapters may be authorized to enact by-laws consistent with the 
state federation’s Constitution; elect a chapter President and 
Secretary-Treasurer; hold meetings of delegates from local 
unions within the chapter’s geographic jurisdiction and make 
recommendations to the state federation or Central Labor 
Council/Area Labor Federation for their consideration.  Chapters 
may not assess a per capita or membership fee.  In accordance 
with the endorsement procedure of the state federation the 
chapter may endorse candidates running for local office within 
the chapter’s geographic jurisdiction and may screen, interview 
and recommend to the state federation or Central Labor Council/ 
Area Labor Federation candidates for state and federal legislative 
office but such recommendation is not binding on this state 
federation. 
 
SECTION 2.  Affiliated local unions in good standing shall be 
entitled to representation and votes in conventions of the North 
Carolina State AFL-CIO.  Such representation shall be based upon 
the average number of members on whom per capita dues have 
been paid for the twelve-month period ending June 30 of the 
year in which the convention is held.  Local unions affiliated for 

less than twelve (12) months shall have their average 
membership proportionately reduced by dividing the per capita 
dues paid for the months affiliated by twelve (12). 
 
The schedule of delegate representation shall be as follows: 
100 members or less: 2 delegates 
101 to 300 members: 3 delegates 
301 to 500 members: 4 delegates 
501 to 700 members: 5 delegates 
701 to 1,000 members: 6 delegates 
 
Local unions having more than 1,000 members shall be entitled 
to one (1) additional delegate for each additional five hundred 
(500) members or major fraction thereof. 
 
Local unions which are not in good standing ( three (3) months or 
more in arrears in the payment of per capita dues at convention 
time), shall not be entitled to any delegates or votes at the 
convention. 
SECTION 3.  Except on roll call votes, each delegate shall be 
entitled to one (1) vote.  Voting shall be by voice or division of 
the house (show of hands or standing vote) unless a roll call vote 
is properly demanded by the required number of delegates as 
provided in Section 8 of this Article.  No delegate shall be 
permitted to cast the proxy of another delegate. 
 
SECTION 4.  On roll call votes, each local union shall be entitled 
to a number of votes equal to the average membership of the 
local union as determined in the manner set forth in Section 2 of 
this Article. 
 
SECTION 5.  Each central body or other subordinate bodies 
referred to in Section 1 (2) and (3) of this Article, in good 
standing, shall be entitled to the maximum number of 3 
delegates and 3 votes.  Subordinate bodies of the Alliance for 
Retired Americans as referred to in Section 1 (4) of this Article, in 
good standing, shall be entitled to one (1) delegate and one (1) 
vote.  Each constituency group, as referred to in Section 1 (5) of 
this Article, shall be entitled to one (1) delegate and one (1) vote. 
 
SECTION 6.  The votes of an organization shall be apportioned 
equally among the registered and present delegates from that 
organization and any votes left over shall be discarded.  Each 
delegate present shall be entitled to cast only his/her/their 
assigned number of votes; however, the delegation from an 
organization may agree that one of its delegates may be 
permitted to cast the total vote of the delegation.  If any 
delegate shall challenge the correctness of the votes so cast, the 
individual delegates of that local shall be polled.  In no case shall 
fractional votes be allowed. 
 
SECTION 7.  The apportionment of votes of each organization 
shall be included in the report of the credentials committee and 
shall not be changed except upon further report of the 
credentials committee.

SECTION 8.  Upon the request of thirty percent (30%) of the 
delegates present at the convention and voting on the questions, 
including election of officers, the chairperson shall order a roll 
call vote. 
 
SECTION 9.  All delegates to the convention must be members in 
good standing of a local union affiliated with the North Carolina 
State AFL-CIO, unless the delegate is a district, state, national or 
international union representative regularly servicing such 
affiliated local union, or unless the delegate was selected to 
represent local bodies chartered by the trade and industrial 
departments of the AFL-CIO, or state or local chapters of AFL-CIO 
constituency groups within the geographic limits of the council 
that are chartered by a national AFL-CIO constituency group.  No 
delegate shall be seated to represent more than one (1) 
organization. 
 
SECTION 10.  Each affiliated organization may designate one 
alternate delegate for each regular delegate to which it is 
entitled.  The alternate delegate shall become a regular delegate 
representing his/her/their organization, in case a regular 
delegate is unable to serve, by registering with the credentials 
committee as a regular delegate.  
 
SECTION 11.  The President of the North Carolina State AFL-CIO, 
if elected as a delegate from an affiliated organization, shall have 
the number of votes as determined for all such delegates. 
 
SECTION 12.  No person will be eligible to serve as a delegate 
who holds a salaried position, or any other position of 
administrative or executive authority in a union or any 
subordinate branch of a union which has been suspended, 
disaffiliated, or expelled from the AFL-CIO. 
 
SECTION 13.  The North Carolina State AFL-CIO shall not reject 
any credentials presented by a duly elected or appointed 
delegate who is eligible to be a delegate under the provisions of 
this Article. 
 
SECTION 14.  No delegate shall be seated except upon 
presentation of a proper credential from an affiliated 
organization in good standing, which credential shall be 
examined and attested to by the Credential's Committee as to 
the eligibility of the delegate and the standing of the 
organization sending the delegate. 
 
 
2. ARTICLE V - CONVENTIONS 
 
SECTION 1.  Regular conventions of the North Carolina State AFL-
CIO shall be held annually in August, September, or October at a 
time and place designated by the Executive Board.  The 
President shall issue the call for the regular convention not later 
than sixty (60) days preceding the convention. 
 
SECTION 2.  Special conventions may be called by the President 
with the concurrence of two-thirds (2/3) of the Executive Board, 
or they may be called by the Executive Board on its own motion, 

two-thirds (2/3) of the Board concurring.  The President shall call 
a special convention upon written request of one-fourth (1/4) of 
the affiliated local unions representing at least one-third (1/3) of 
the membership on whom per capita dues are paid to the North 
Carolina State AFL-CIO setting forth the purpose or purposes for 
which they wish the convention called.  The Executive Board 
shall fix the time and place of a special convention and shall 
cause at least thirty (30) days’ notice to be given all affiliated 
organizations.  The President shall issue the call that shall state 
the particular purpose or purposes of the special convention and 
no other business shall be transacted. 
 
Representation at special conventions shall be the same as for 
regular conventions and shall be calculated as set forth in Article 
IV, Section 2, except that the last day of the third (3rd) month 
prior to the month in which the special convention is to be held 
shall be substituted for June 30th where it appears in said 
Section. 
 
SECTION 3.  Blank credentials in duplicate shall be sent out to 
each affiliated organization with the Convention Call.  One copy 
of the credential shall be returned to the President with the 
name and address of the delegate duly certified thereon, at least 
fifteen (15) days prior to the opening of the convention if the 
delegate is to be considered for appointment to a convention 
committee.  The other copy of the credential shall be presented 
to the Credentials Committee by the delegate at the time of 
registration; in the absence of credentials on the official form, 
the Credentials Committee may accept duly certified credentials 
which to it appears to be valid. This section shall be read to allow 
the President to furnish blank credentials electronically and 
permit delegates to return such credentials electronically. 
SECTION 4.  Each delegate’s credentials shall be accompanied by 
a registration fee. Such fee  shall be set forth by the North 
Carolina State AFL-CIO Executive Board to cover convention 
expenses. 
 
SECTION 5.  The President shall appoint a corps of Sergeants-At-
Arms to assist in maintaining order and decorum at the 
convention.  The President shall have the authority to appoint 
such committees as may be needed to expedite the work of the 
convention.  Resolutions shall be referred by the President to the 
appropriate committee of the Executive Board according to 
subject matter, unless otherwise ordered by the convention. 
 
SECTION 6.  Resolutions for consideration of the convention may 
be submitted by an affiliated organization, by a duly certified 
delegate, or by the Executive Board and shall be sent to the 
President at least twenty-one (21) days prior to the date for 
convening the convention.  No other resolutions shall be in order 
for consideration by the convention unless accepted by consent 
of two-thirds (2/3) of the delegates present and voting. 
 
SECTION 7.  Sectarian religious questions shall not be proposed, 
considered, acted on, or discussed in meetings of the North 
Carolina State AFL-CIO.

3. ARTICLE VI - THE PRESIDENT, THE EXECUTIVE BOARD, AND 

HIS/HER/THEIR DUTIES 
 
SECTION 1.  The President and the elected Vice-Presidents shall 
constitute the Executive Board of the North Carolina State AFL-
CIO.  The convention credential report will be used to determine 
the number of dues paying members of each international.  The 
Vice-Presidents shall be elected to seats on the Board as follows: 
 
(a) Any international union with local affiliates paying monthly 
per capita dues to the North Carolina State AFL-CIO on 6000 or 
more members shall be entitled to three seats on the Board.  A 
caucus of the delegates from each such international union shall 
nominate three of its members to the full convention for 
election. 
 
(b) Any international union with local affiliates paying monthly 
per capita dues to the North Carolina State AFL-CIO on 1500 or 
more members shall be entitled to two seats on the Board.  A 
caucus of the delegates from each such international union shall 
nominate two of its members to the full convention for election. 
 
(c) Any international union with local affiliates paying monthly 
per capita dues to the North Carolina State AFL-CIO on 1000 or 
more members shall be entitled to one seat on the Board.  A 
caucus of delegates from each such international union shall 
nominate one of its members to the full convention for election. 
 
(d) Those international unions with local affiliates paying 
monthly per capita dues to the North Carolina State AFL-CIO on 
less than 1000 members shall together be entitled to four seats 
on the Board.  A caucus of delegates from those international 
unions shall nominate four of its members, no two from the 
same international union, to the full convention for election. The 
Executive Board will follow the process for filling board vacancies 
and vote within 30 days of passage of this amendment to fill the 
additional seat until the next regularly scheduled election. 
 
(e) The Central Labor Councils/Area Labor Federations, the 
Building Trades Council, and constituency groups shall each be 
entitled to one seat on the Board.  A caucus of the Presidents of 
each Central Labor Council/Area Labor Federation shall nominate 
one representative to the full convention for election; a caucus 
of all constituency group chapter presidents shall nominate one 
representative to the full convention for election; and a caucus 
of Building Trade Council delegates shall nominate one 
representative to the full convention. 
 
(f) Any member may run for the Board, provided that 
he/she/they is a member of and is nominated by a delegate from 
the international union in 1.a.-1.c, one of the International 
Unions in 1.d., or one of the groups in 1.e. that he/she/they 
seeks to represent.  No more than one member of the same 
international union shall hold a seat described in 1.d. 

 
(g) The North Carolina State AFL-CIO will undertake all 
reasonable efforts to ensure diversity of representation at every 
level.
