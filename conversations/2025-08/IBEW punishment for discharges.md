## Assistant

The **IBEW Constitution** does **not directly lay out a punishment for members who are terminated (discharged) from jobs twice**. However, this situation *is* addressed in the **Inside Recovery Agreement**, specifically under **"Repeated Discharge"** on page 9 of the *current recovery 2025* document.

Here’s the summary:

> **If an applicant is discharged for cause two times within a 12-month period**, they must go before the **Site Local Union Executive Board**. That board will review their qualifications and reasons for the discharges and can:
>
> 1. Require retraining through the JATC.
> 2. Disqualify the applicant from referral for **at least 4 weeks** or longer depending on severity.
> 3. Refer the applicant to an Employee Assistance Program.
> 4. Restore them to the referral list【11†current recovery 2025.pdf†L9-L9】.

This is not from the IBEW Constitution, but rather from a **contractual agreement** that your local has signed on to. If you're looking for a **Constitutional** basis for further discipline (like membership suspension or expulsion), you'd be looking under **Article XXV** of the Constitution, which deals with offenses and trials—but it requires specific charges to be filed by a member or officer.

The section you're looking for is titled **“REPEATED DISCHARGE”** on **page 9** of the *Inside Recovery Agreement* in the `current recovery 2025.pdf`.

Here’s the exact section heading and a summary:

---

### 📄 **REPEATED DISCHARGE**
> “An applicant who is discharged for cause two times within a 12-month period shall be referred to the Site Local Union Executive Board for a determination as to the applicant's continued eligibility for referral.”

The Executive Board then has several options, such as:
- Requiring further training
- Disqualifying the applicant for a period (e.g., 4+ weeks)
- Referring them to an Employee Assistance Program
- Restoring them to the referral list

---

This isn’t technically a “punishment” from the IBEW Constitution—it’s an administrative policy in the **Recovery Agreement**, which your local enforces.
