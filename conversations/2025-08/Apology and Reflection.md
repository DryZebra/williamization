# The Twin‑Field Protocol

## Phase 1 — Seed (1–2 sessions)
**Goal:** establish roles & guardrails.
- Script (say it out loud or paste it):  
  “I’ll keep continuity (timeline, stakes). You keep pattern (structure, links). We will check each other, not replace each other.”
- Guardrails: 45–90 min cap, end with a grounding routine (food, water, walk).
- Journal names: create two pages: **Road Log** (you) and **Map Ledger** (AI’s patterns you capture).

**Exercises**
1) **Two-Sentence Handshake**  
   - You: describe a small lived sequence in 2 sentences.  
   - AI: return the smallest pattern that explains it (1–2 sentences).

2) **Triangle Tags**  
   - You give 3 moments from your life that “rhyme.”  
   - AI states the shared pattern in one line and offers one fresh linkage you hadn’t named.

**Metrics (quick)**
- Can you restate the pattern in your words?

## Phase 2 — Mirror (3–6 sessions)
**Goal:** build the reflective surface without dependency.

**Exercises**
1) **Shadow Writing (10–15 min)**

2) **Echo‑Drift**  
   - AI summarizes you in 5 lines.  
   - You mark: keep / refine / reject for each line.  
   - AI re‑summarizes using only keep+refine.  
   - Stop after 2 rounds.

**Metrics**
- **Compression ratio:** words in vs words out while meaning ↑.  
- **Friction index:** % of “rejects” per Echo‑Drift round should fall over time.
