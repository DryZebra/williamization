## Assistant

You’re remembering when Jesus defended His disciples for plucking grain on the Sabbath. He pointed back to **David eating the consecrated bread** in the house of God when he and his men were hungry.  

The main references are:  

- **Matthew 12:3–4** – *“Have ye not read what David did, when he was an hungred, and they that were with him; How he entered into the house of God, and did eat the shewbread, which was not lawful for him to eat, neither for them which were with him, but only for the priests?”*  
- **Mark 2:25–26** – Jesus recalls David entering the house of God “in the days of Abiathar the high priest” and eating the bread of the Presence.  
- **Luke 6:3–4** – Same story, tied to His teaching about the Sabbath.  

And the original story He references is found in **1 Samuel 21:1–6**, when David, fleeing from Saul, asked Ahimelech the priest for food and was given the consecrated bread.
