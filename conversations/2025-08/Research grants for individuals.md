# Research grants for individuals

## User

Can I get research grants as an individual to keep studying Christianity full time? Traveling to churches around North Carolina, interviewing and trying to talk to them about Materialist Christianity?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Yes, **you *can* get research grants as an individual**, but it's harder without institutional affiliation. That said, **there are creative and realistic paths you can pursue**, especially since your project has:

- A **regional focus** (churches in North Carolina)  
- A **philosophical and sociological angle** (Materialist Christianity)  
- A **documentary and public education component** (interviews, travel, sharing findings)

Here’s how to approach this:

---

## 🧭 1. **What Kind of Grant Fits Your Work?**

You are doing *independent field research + public engagement*—this overlaps with grants for:

- **Independent scholars** or self-directed researchers  
- **Artistic and humanities projects** (oral history, documentary, philosophy of religion)  
- **Public theology or interfaith dialogue**  
- **Sociology or anthropology of religion**  
- **Creative nonfiction / writing fellowships**

---

## 🏛️ 2. **Potential Funding Sources**

### 🔹 **Local and Regional Opportunities**
- **North Carolina Humanities Council** – They offer **Road Scholars** and **Community Engagement Grants**  
  > https://nchumanities.org/programs/grants/
  - Especially strong if you turn this into a “conversation project” where you present back to communities.

- **Duke University’s Kenan Institute for Ethics** or the Divinity School (even without being a student)
  - Reach out and offer a pitch as an *independent collaborator*. These places fund *faith in practice* explorations.

- **Appalachian College Association / Blue Ridge Heritage Grants**
  - Since your girlfriend is Appalachian-rooted and you’re working in-region, your framing might qualify.

---

### 🔹 **National/General Sources**
- **The Louisville Institute**  
  > https://louisville-institute.org/  
  - Offers **Grants for Individuals** (Pastoral Study Projects, Theological Exploration Grants)
  - They *do* fund independent Christians working on theology “in practice.”

- **The Pollination Project**  
  - Funds individual changemakers doing community or social transformation work.

- **Pulitzer Center** (for documentary/interview work—could apply if you want to publish stories or videos)

- **Sustainable Arts Foundation**  
  - Funds creators with families—if your work overlaps with creative nonfiction or public writing.

- **GoFundMe or Patreon**  
  - If institutional grants are slow, consider combining small donations to fund travel/interviews.

---

## 📚 3. **What You’ll Need to Prepare**

Even as an individual:
- A **project proposal** (1–3 pages)  
- **Detailed budget** (travel, food, equipment, etc.)  
- Some **samples of your work** (your book, a short video, or research summary)  
- **Clear purpose**: What will you *do* with the interviews? A book? Website? Public archive?

You can also create a **fiscal sponsor** relationship—i.e., partner with a nonprofit that agrees to “host” your grant so you can qualify for bigger funds. This is common for independent researchers.
