## Assistant

There are no widely known or traditional hymns *specifically* titled after Ezra and Nehemiah—but their themes have deeply influenced many hymns, especially those about:

- **Rebuilding**
- **Return from exile**
- **God's faithfulness to a remnant**
- **Revival through Scripture**
- **Covenant renewal**

### Here's what you could sing that *evokes* Ezra and Nehemiah:

#### 1. **"Guide Me, O Thou Great Jehovah"**
> Think of Nehemiah’s long journey to rebuild Jerusalem under God's hand. This hymn is about trusting God while walking an uncertain, holy path.

#### 2. **"Come, Thou Fount of Every Blessing"**
> A hymn about remembering the Lord's help, just like Ezra reading the law to the people and calling them to return.

#### 3. **"Great Is Thy Faithfulness"**
> Perfect for the post-exile mood of both books—God had been faithful even when Israel had not.

#### 4. **"The Church’s One Foundation"**
> It echoes the rebuilding of the temple and walls: a people re-founded on the Word.

#### 5. **"Revive Us Again"**
> Ezra and Nehemiah both led revivals—this hymn captures the spirit of national and spiritual renewal.
