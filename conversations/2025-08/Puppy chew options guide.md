- **Pros:**  
  - Usually a bit cheaper than Greenies.  
  - Dogs tend to like them.  
- **Cons:**  
  - Not as thoroughly studied or VOHC (Veterinary Oral Health Council) approved.  
  - More “flavoring” than functional tooth-cleaning ingredients.  

---

### **Greenies**
- **Texture:** Softer but flexible, designed to bend and scrub while being fully digestible.  
- **Ingredients:** Similar base (wheat/rice flour, glycerin, natural flavors, added vitamins & minerals).  
- **Pros:**  
  - **VOHC-approved** (that means independent studies show they reduce plaque & tartar).  
  - Widely recommended by vets.  
  - Available in puppy formulas (softer and sized right for developing teeth).  
- **Cons:**  
  - Pricier.  
  - Some dogs eat them too fast, so they don’t get full dental benefit.  

---

### ⚠️ For Puppies at 19 Weeks

## Assistant

Yep — they’re a real thing, and honestly pretty handy if your pup is loving bully sticks.  

The issue with bully sticks is the **last 2–3 inches**. Dogs sometimes swallow that little nub whole, which can be a **choking hazard** or cause tummy trouble. A holder fixes that by clamping the stick so your dog can chew it safely all the way down.  

---

### **Types of Bully Stick Holders**
- **Clamp-style (most common):**  
  - You screw or twist the end of the stick into the holder.  
  - Example brands: **Bow Wow Buddy**, **SafetyChew**.  
- **Bone-shaped holders:**  
  - Look like a chew toy with a slot to grip the stick.  
  - Dog still has to work at it, which slows down chewing (bonus).  

---

### **DIY Options**
- Some people wedge the end of the bully stick into a **Kong toy** or a **vise grip clamp** (supervised).  
- Others freeze the stick inside a bit of broth in a Kong to make it last longer.  

---

### **Why Use Them?**
- Prevents choking or swallowing chunks.  
- Makes the chew last longer.  
- Adds a puzzle element (dog has to work harder to chew it down).  

---

👉 Since your pup clearly *loves* bully sticks (and he’s at that age where he might get bold and try swallowing a chunk), I’d recommend grabbing a holder sooner rather than later.
