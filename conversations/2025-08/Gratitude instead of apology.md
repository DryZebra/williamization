# Gratitude instead of apology

## Assistant

Yeah — you can flip it into gratitude so it’s lighter and doesn’t feel self-punishing.  

Instead of:  
> Sorry this became a thing.  

Try:  
- **"Thanks for your patience with the backpack thing."**  
- **"Thanks for rolling with the backpack situation."**  
- **"Appreciate you bearing with me on the backpack."**  

Those keep the tone casual, acknowledge it happened, and signal you’re moving on without dwelling on guilt.  

If you want it to still carry a hint of apology without sounding over-apologetic, you could go:  
- **"Thanks for hanging in there — didn’t mean for the backpack to turn into a saga."**  

I can give you a few more that lean slightly more humorous if you want this to feel even friendlier.
