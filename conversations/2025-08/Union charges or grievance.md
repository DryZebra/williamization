## Assistant

Your member has two possible paths depending on whether they want to go after **Andy as a union member** or **SRE as the employer**:

**1. Bringing Andy up on charges (union discipline):**  
- The IBEW Constitution allows members to file charges against another member for “offenses” like slander, libel, or conduct unbecoming a member.  
- Article XXV says charges must be written, signed, and state the facts (dates, places, what was said/done). They go to the Recording Secretary of the local.  
- The Executive Board will then hold a trial, hear witnesses, and decide guilt and penalties (which can include reprimand, fine, suspension, or expulsion)  .  

**2. Filing a grievance against SRE (the contractor):**  
- The CBA says if a worker is harmed by a contractor or foreman’s actions, the member can file a grievance through the steward or business manager.  
- It has to be filed within 10 working days of the event or knowledge of the violation, and then it moves up step by step: steward → business manager → employer → arbitration if needed .  
- This path is only for violations of the contract (wages, hiring, foreman abuse, discrimination, etc.). Personal slander may not count unless it ties to job rights.
