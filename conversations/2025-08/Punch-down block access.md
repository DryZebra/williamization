## Assistant

What you’ve got in the picture is a **110 punch-down block** (sometimes used in patch panels or wall fields).  

There isn’t usually a “front-side only” quick connect option for those. Standard practice is:  

- **Yes, you punch from the top/back side** of the block, right into the slot where the Vs are cut. That’s what the punch-down tool is for.  
- The little cover/strain relief piece that sometimes snaps over the front isn’t a termination point—it’s just to hold or dress the cables.  
- If you want easier access (for example, working only from the room side), you’d normally use **keystone jacks** or a **patch panel** instead. Those are designed to be terminated straight on the face side.  

So for this kind of block:  
👉 You’ll have to strip, seat, and punch the wires from the back/top side. There’s no alternate access method built into it.
