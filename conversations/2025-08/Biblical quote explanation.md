You’re thinking of **Proverbs 25:2**. In the King James Version it reads:  

> *“It is the glory of God to conceal a thing: but the honour of kings is to search out a matter.”*  

Other translations render “thing” as “matter” or “mystery,” but the meaning is the same: God’s greatness is shown in the hiddenness of truth, and a ruler’s greatness is shown in diligently uncovering and understanding it.
