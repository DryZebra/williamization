# Vaping withdrawal support

## User

My girlfriend is quitting vaping. What withdrawal symptoms should we expect. And how can I help her as her knight in shining armor?

## Assistant



## Assistant

That's wonderful that she's quitting! Vaping withdrawal can bring about several symptoms as the body adjusts. Here are some common ones to expect:

1. **Nicotine cravings** – She might experience strong urges to vape, especially in the first few days or weeks.
2. **Irritability or mood swings** – Nicotine affects dopamine levels, so without it, she might feel more easily frustrated or down.
3. **Anxiety or stress** – A feeling of heightened anxiety is common as the body adjusts.
4. **Difficulty concentrating** – The lack of nicotine can make it harder for her to focus.
5. **Sleep disturbances** – She might have trouble falling or staying asleep as her body adjusts to the absence of nicotine.
6. **Increased appetite or weight gain** – Sometimes, the body replaces the craving for nicotine with food.
7. **Physical symptoms** – Headaches, fatigue, dizziness, and even coughing (as her lungs begin to heal) can happen.

### How you can help her:
1. **Be supportive and patient** – Offer emotional support, and be understanding when she’s irritable or anxious. Remind her of the positive change she’s making for her health.
2. **Help distract her** – Keep her busy with fun activities to keep her mind off the cravings. Go for walks, play games, or have a cozy movie night.
3. **Encourage healthy habits** – Help her replace the vaping habit with healthier alternatives, like drinking water, chewing gum, or doing deep breathing exercises.
4. **Create a calm environment** – Make sure she feels safe to vent her frustration or stress without feeling judged. Calm, understanding, and a listening ear will be powerful.
5. **Celebrate small victories** – Whether it’s an hour, a day, or a week without vaping, make sure to celebrate those milestones together.

If she ever feels overwhelmed, remind her how strong she is and that you’re there for her. She’s making a huge commitment, and having you by her side will make all the difference.
