### 🧠 Concept 2: Power Factor (How Efficiently Power Is Used)

**Rewritten Question (Simplified Numbers):**

> 🔌 *A circuit uses 120 kW of real power, but the total power flowing from the utility is 150 kVA. What is the power factor of this circuit?*

**Answer Choices:**
a) 70%  
b) 80% ✅  
c) 90%  
d) 100%

---

**Whiteboard Prompt:**
1. Re-draw the **Power Triangle** from last time:
   - Base = True Power = 120 kW  
   - Hypotenuse = Apparent Power = 150 kVA  
   - Label the angle θ  
2. Write the formula:  
   \[
   \text{Power Factor} = \frac{\text{True Power}}{\text{Apparent Power}} = \frac{120}{150} = 0.8 \text{ or } 80\%
   \]

---

**Explanation for Teaching:**
- “Power Factor tells us **how efficiently** the power is being used.”
- “If the power factor is 1 (or 100%), it means **everything** from the power company is being used usefully.”
- “If the power factor is 0.8 (80%), then **only 80% of what you're buying is actually doing work**—the rest is just reactive.”

---

**Quick Analogy to Explain:**
"Imagine buying a bucket of water (kVA), but only 80% of it makes it to the plant to cool it down (kW). The rest splashes around uselessly due to turbulence (kVAR). That’s your power factor—how much actually helps."
