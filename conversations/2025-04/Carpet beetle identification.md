## Assistant

Thanks for the clear photos — that little bug appears to be a **carpet beetle larva** or a **related small beetle**, like a varied carpet beetle (*Anthrenus verbasci*). These beetles are tiny (usually 2–3 mm), have rounded backs, and often have a mottled or shiny brown pattern like the one in your photos.

**Key ID features:**
- Oval, dome-shaped body
- Brown or mottled coloration
- Short legs and antennae
- Often found indoors, especially near fabrics or light sources

### Are they harmful?
- **To people?** No, they don't bite or sting.
- **To property?** Yes — the larvae (not adults) can damage natural fibers: wool, leather, feathers, even pet hair.

### What to do:
- Check for signs of larvae in clothing, rugs, or furniture.
- Vacuum thoroughly, especially around baseboards and under furniture.
- Wash infested fabrics in hot water.
- Consider pest control if the problem persists.
