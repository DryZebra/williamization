The world we live in is not governed by matter alone. Our daily choices, our fears, our loyalties, our beliefs—most of them are shaped by things we cannot touch. A stop sign has no power in itself. Marx understood this boundary. He spoke of the **superstructure**—laws, morality, religion, politics—as the reflection of the material base. He showed how ideas do not descend from the heavens. They are ruled by stories.

This is not postmodernism. This is not idealism. This is not to say that reality is subjective. It is to say that **reality has levels**. That some things are *real by consequence,* not by composition.

These are what I call **sub-objects**.

A sub-object is something that does not exist independently of recognition, but once recognized, becomes real through its ability to shape behavior. It is not fake. It is not imaginary. It is not metaphysical. It is *relational*. Its reality depends on people, but once accepted, it behaves like an object. It creates consequences. It moves the world.

Marriage is a sub-object. A nation is a sub-object. A brand is a sub-object. A sin is a sub-object. Even “me” is a sub-object in the minds of others.
