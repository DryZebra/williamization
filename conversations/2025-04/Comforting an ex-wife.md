Sometimes, when someone knows they hurt someone they *once* loved, they cling to guilt as a form of self-punishment, especially if that person shows them compassion despite everything. But guilt isn't the same as accountability, and it doesn't fix the past. What helps is knowing that healing is still possible—for both people.

You could tell her something like:

> *"I’ve had time to think about everything, and I want you to know I don’t hold onto anger. We weren’t meant to be, and that's okay. I see that now.
