## User

Do I need willpower for fatigue or is entrance and strength going to increase that?

## Assistant

Great question—and it’s a smart one, because **Fatigue** is *critical* in Oblivion, especially for melee combat like your hammer-wielding Journeyman.

### **Here’s how Fatigue is calculated:**

> **Fatigue = (Strength + Endurance + Willpower + Agility) × 2**

So yes:
- **Strength and Endurance** absolutely *do* increase your Fatigue.
- **Willpower and Agility** also contribute, but they’re not as important for your build.

---

### **What Does Fatigue Actually Do?**
- Affects **melee damage**
- Influences **blocking effectiveness**
- Impacts **stagger chance** (both giving and receiving)
- Determines success in **knockback checks** and **stagger recovery**
- Affects **spellcasting success** if you use any

---

### **Do You Need Willpower Then?**

**Probably not right away**, because:
- You're already raising **Strength and Endurance**, so your fatigue pool will be high anyway.
- You're not relying on active spellcasting in battle (just some Restoration out of combat).
- You don’t need to cast often or regenerate magicka quickly.

**Later on**, if you find yourself using Restoration more—or if you want to improve spellcasting reliability—you can raise Willpower then. But it’s not essential early.

---

### **Verdict:**
Focus first on:
- **Endurance**
- **Strength**
- **Personality** (for roleplay, speech, Restoration)

Save **Willpower** for later, or raise it a little if you end up wanting a bigger magicka pool or faster regen.
