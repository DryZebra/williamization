## 🥪 No-Fridge, No-Cook Lunch Ideas

### 1. **Peanut Butter & Banana Wraps**
- **Ingredients**: Whole wheat tortilla, peanut butter, banana
- **Preparation**: Spread peanut butter on the tortilla, place the banana, roll it up, and slice into bite-sized pieces.
- **Why it works**: Provides a good balance of protein, healthy fats, and carbohydrates. It's also budget-friendly and doesn't require refrigeration.

### 2. **Tuna Salad with Crackers**
- **Ingredients**: Canned tuna (preferably in pouches), whole grain crackers, optional: mustard or relish packets
- **Preparation**: Mix tuna with mustard or relish if desired, and scoop onto crackers when ready to eat.
- **Why it works**: Tuna is a great source of lean protein and omega-3 fatty acids. Using pouches eliminates the need for a can opener and reduces mess.

### 3. **Chickpea Salad**
- **Ingredients**: Canned chickpeas, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper, optional: diced bell peppers or cucumbers
- **Preparation**: Rinse chickpeas and mix with olive oil, lemon juice, and seasonings. Add diced vegetables if desired.
- **Why it works**: Chickpeas are high in protein and fiber. This salad can be made in advance and stays fresh without refrigeration for several hours.

### 4. **Nut Butter and Rice Cakes**
- **Ingredients**: Rice cakes, almond or peanut butter
- **Preparation**: Spread nut butter on rice cakes.
- **Why it works**: This combination offers a satisfying crunch with healthy fats and complex carbohydrates. It's also lightweight and easy to pack.

### 5. **Trail Mix and Dried Fruit**
- **Ingredients**: Mixed nuts, dried fruits like raisins or apricots, optional: dark chocolate chips
- **Preparation**: Combine ingredients in a resealable bag.
- **Why it works**: Trail mix provides a mix of protein, healthy fats, and natural sugars for energy. It's non-perishable and requires no preparation.
