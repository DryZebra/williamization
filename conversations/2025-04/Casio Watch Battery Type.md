Yeah, good question — **alternatives are very limited** for a CR1620 because of its size (16mm wide, 2.0mm thick), but here’s the real situation:

- **CR1616** is sometimes *forced* in if you're careful — it’s the same width (16mm) but thinner (1.6mm instead of 2.0mm).  
  - It **might** make slightly worse contact and could have a shorter lifespan.
  - Sometimes you can **bend the contact tab** *very slightly* to hold it tight, but **only if you're careful** and don't crack the contact.
  
- **CR1632** and **CR1625** are **too thick** — they won't fit without damaging the case or breaking the seals.

So **if you absolutely can't find a CR1620**:
- A **CR1616** *can work in a pinch* but it’s not ideal.
- It's better to **order a CR1620** if possible (even if you have to wait a few days).
