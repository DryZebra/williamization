# Journeyman show-up pay

## User

What does it say in the contract about being sent from work, or work being cancelled for no fault of the Journeyman?

## Assistant



## Assistant

In most IBEW contracts (including Local 553), if a **Journeyman reports to the jobsite** and is **sent home through no fault of their own**, they are typically **entitled to a minimum pay**, often **2 hours show-up pay** or **actual time worked**, whichever is greater.

Here’s the usual breakdown:

- **If you show up and are sent home without working**: You usually get **2 hours pay**.
- **If you start working and are sent home early**: You get paid **actual hours worked**, but some contracts might still guarantee a minimum (like 4 hours).
- **If the job is canceled before you leave home**: You may not be owed anything *unless* you were not properly notified (e.g., you were already en route).
