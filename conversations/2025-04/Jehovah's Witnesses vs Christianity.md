## Assistant

You're right to notice that Jehovah’s Witnesses are often treated differently by mainstream Christian denominations. The main reasons come down to **doctrinal differences**, particularly about the **nature of Jesus**, the **Trinity**, and their **organizational structure**. Here’s a breakdown:

---

### **1. Nature of Jesus & the Trinity**

- **Jehovah’s Witnesses reject the Trinity**. They believe that **God (Jehovah)** is one singular being, not three persons. Jesus is seen as **God’s first creation**, a distinct being **inferior to God**, similar to the **Logos** in some early Christian thinking.
  
- They believe that Jesus **is the Archangel Michael**, who came to earth as a man, died, and was resurrected as a spirit being.

- This view aligns **somewhat** with Islam in that both deny Jesus is **God**—but unlike Islam, Jehovah's Witnesses do believe Jesus is the **Messiah**, **God's Son**, and **died for humanity's sins**.

---

### **2. The Holy Spirit**

- They believe the **Holy Spirit** is not a person but rather **God’s active force** (like His power in action), not a distinct divine being.

---

### **3. Authority & Scripture**

- Jehovah’s Witnesses use their own translation of the Bible, the **New World Translation**, which includes wording that reflects their theology (e.g., “the Word was *a* god” in John 1:1).
  
- They place strong authority in the **Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society**, their governing body, which sets doctrine. Critics say this leads to **centralized control** and a discouragement of personal interpretation.

---

### **4. Christian Community Tension**

- **Mainstream Christians** (Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant) may differ on many things, but they mostly affirm the **Nicene Creed**: the Trinity, the divinity of Christ, and the resurrection of the body.

- Jehovah's Witnesses reject all of that—so most Christians view them as **non-Christian** or even **heretical** in the historical sense.

---

### **5. Evangelism & Exclusivity**

- They believe they are the **only true Christians** and all other religions (including other forms of Christianity) are part of “Babylon the Great,” a false religious system soon to be destroyed.

- This exclusivity and their aggressive evangelism can alienate them from others.

---

So yes, you’re right in comparing them somewhat to Latter-day Saints—Jehovah’s Witnesses are **Christian in inspiration**, but their theology diverges **so radically** that many Christians see them as **outside the faith**. Not for lack of sincerity, but because of **deep doctrinal differences on who Jesus is**.
