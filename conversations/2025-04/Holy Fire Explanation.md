### **1. What Is the Holy Fire “Made Of”?**

Physically, it's just **ordinary fire**, produced by combustion. There's **no scientific evidence** that the flame is chemically different or miraculous. What is claimed to be miraculous is the **manner of its appearance**, not the flame itself.

The flame is believed by some Orthodox Christians to appear spontaneously in the tomb, after which it’s used to light candles. But in practice, the Greek Orthodox Patriarch enters the tomb **alone**, and the actual method of ignition is **not verified**—leading many to believe it is **secretly lit by conventional means**.

---

### **2. Why Doesn’t It Burn People (At First)?**

There are a few reasons why people seem to touch it without being burned, especially in the **first few minutes**:

#### **A. Low-Temperature Flames from a Candle**
Freshly lit **candle flames**, especially large beeswax candles, burn at **relatively low temperatures**—about 1000°C at the hottest point, but much cooler at the tip. The **outer edge of the flame** and especially the **blue base** is hottest. People may be touching **just the faint outer edges**.

#### **B. Moisture and Oil**
Some believers prepare by **moistening their hands or beards**, or using **olive oil**, which provides a short protective barrier. Fire-walkers use similar tricks—**brief contact with flame won’t instantly burn** if conditions are right.

#### **C. Thermal Inertia and Brief Contact**
Short exposure (less than a second) to low-temperature flames is often survivable without injury, especially if you’re not panicking or pressing into it. Many also use **long taper candles** to mediate the heat.

#### **D. Symbolic Performance**
Some argue that people are **encouraged to perform** displays of devotion and bravery to demonstrate faith. This includes waving the flame around and briefly touching it to themselves in a **controlled and practiced way**.

### **Conclusion**
The Holy Fire is not chemically unique—it's a symbolic, religious tradition, not a scientific anomaly. The reason people don’t burn themselves is largely **physics and ritual**: careful technique, short exposure, and crowd psychology.
