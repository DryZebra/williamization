# Ferret Slow Blinking Meaning

## Assistant

Ferrets slow blink for similar reasons to cats—it’s a sign of **relaxation, trust, and comfort**. When a ferret gives you a slow blink, especially during cuddling or quiet time, it likely means:

- **They feel safe around you.**  
- **They’re content and sleepy.**  
- **They’re relaxed but still aware of you.**

It’s a non-verbal way of showing that they’re not stressed or threatened. If your ferret slow blinks while making eye contact, it’s probably their version of a little “I’m happy with you” moment. You can even try slow blinking back—some ferrets will respond with another blink or a gentle snuggle.
