## Assistant

Alright, you're going the **fruitarian-light** route: one big, fresh produce meal a day, backed by multivitamins. Lazy, minimalist, and aiming to drop weight without your bones turning to dust. Here's a practical, *don't-die* recommendation for two people.

### Daily Meal Plan (For 2 People – 1 Shared Meal)

**Core Principle:**  
- Keep blood sugar stable  
- Avoid only-sugar fruits  
- Include *bulk*, *fat*, and *fiber*  
- Hydrate through fruit + drink water  
- One “meal” can be a big plate/salad-style bowl to split

---

### **Daily Produce List (2 People):**

- **2 bananas** (potassium, slow-release carbs)  
- **1 avocado** (healthy fat, satiety, fat-soluble vitamin absorption)  
- **1 cucumber** (hydration + volume)  
- **1 bell pepper** (vitamin C, crunch)  
- **2 carrots** (fiber, vitamin A)  
- **2 handfuls spinach or kale** (iron, folate, roughage)  
- **1 apple or pear each** (fiber + sugar control)  
- **1 handful blueberries or strawberries** (antioxidants)  
- **1 lemon** (squeeze over salad for flavor & vitamin C boost)  

**Optional (rotate daily):**  
- 1 kiwi or orange (C + enzymes)  
- Small handful of nuts/seeds (1–2 tbsp total: sunflower, walnut, or pumpkin)  
- 1 boiled egg each (if you're not *strictly* fruit/veg and want more satiety)

---

### **Instructions:**

1. **Evening Prep (takes 10 minutes max):**  
   - Chop everything and mix into a giant salad bowl or platter  
   - Avocado and lemon last to avoid browning  
   - Salt (tiny pinch) + lemon or vinegar for taste  
   - Add nuts/seeds on top for fat if no avocado  
   - Boil eggs while you cut if using them

2. **Eat cold, eat together, eat all of it**  
   One meal a day: get full on it.

---

### **Multivitamin Covers You For:**
- B12  
- D3  
- Zinc  
- Selenium  
- Iodine  
- Vitamin K2 (if your multi has it)

---

### Weekly Tip:
- **Change colors daily** — red bell pepper one day, yellow the next. Green leaf, red leaf, romaine.  
- If digestion gets too “quick” — add a bit of starch (like ½ sweet potato or boiled egg) once every few days.

---

**Real Talk Reminder:**  
You're not going to die eating only fruit and vegetables for a few weeks with a multivitamin, **but if you start feeling foggy, weak, or freezing all the time**, you’re dipping too far into deficit. Add a small protein or fat source now and then.

Stay disciplined. Stay hydrated. You're not rabbits—you’re lazy lions in lean season.
