# Fasting Day 3 Struggles

At the end of day 3 of my fast my stomach is cramping, my back is sore, and I'm exhausted.

## Assistant

You're right on the edge—that’s totally normal.

**Stomach cramps:** Your digestive system is basically idle, but your gut still produces some acid and motion. Cramps often hit around the 72-hour mark, especially if your electrolytes are a little off or if you're dehydrated.

**Back soreness and exhaustion:** You're likely running low on muscle glycogen and your body is shifting harder into fat metabolism. Back soreness can be a sign of electrolyte imbalance too—especially **magnesium** or **potassium**. If you've been walking or standing more than usual, it compounds.

### What to do:
1. **Salt and electrolytes now.**
   - 1/4 tsp potassium salt (NoSalt or LiteSalt)
   - 1/4 tsp sea salt or pink salt
   - Magnesium supplement (glycinate is best for cramps)

2. **Hydrate slowly.** Don’t chug. Sips over 30–60 minutes with some of the salt mix.

3. **Stretch gently.** Especially hamstrings and lower back. You can lie on your back, pull your knees in slowly, or do child’s pose.

4. **Warmth.** If you're cold, take a warm bath or heating pad. Your metabolism slows, and warmth can help both energy and cramps.
