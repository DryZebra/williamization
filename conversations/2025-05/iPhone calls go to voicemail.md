# iPhone calls go to voicemail

## User

My coworkersiPhone doesn't ring the first time someone calls it just goes straight to voicemail.

## Assistant

If your coworker’s iPhone sends calls straight to voicemail without ringing, here are the most common reasons and fixes:

### 1. **Focus Mode / Do Not Disturb is on**
- Go to **Settings > Focus**.
- Check if **Do Not Disturb**, **Sleep**, or any other Focus mode is active.
- Turn it off or adjust the allowed calls settings.

### 2. **Silence Unknown Callers**
- Go to **Settings > Phone > Silence Unknown Callers**.
- If this is on, calls from numbers not in contacts, Siri suggestions, or recent calls will go straight to voicemail.

### 3. **Airplane Mode or No Signal**
- Swipe down from the top-right to check for **Airplane mode**.
- Also check **signal strength** — no service = no ringing.

### 4. **Call Forwarding**
- Go to **Settings > Phone > Call Forwarding**.
- Make sure it’s off unless intentionally forwarding.

### 5. **Carrier Settings or Voicemail Glitch**
- Try restarting the phone.
- Check for a **Carrier Settings Update** in **Settings > General > About**.
- Call the carrier if the issue persists — it could be on their end.
