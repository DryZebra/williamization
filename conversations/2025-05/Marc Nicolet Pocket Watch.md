### **How to Store Your Grandfather’s Pocket Watch Properly**

**1. Avoid Plastic Bags:**  
Plastic traps moisture and can accelerate tarnishing or even rust, especially with old oils inside the movement. Ziplocks can create a microclimate of slow decay over years.

**2. Use a Breathable, Padded Container:**  
- A **small watch or jewelry box** with a **soft lining** (felt, suede, or cotton) is ideal.  
- If possible, use a box with **anti-tarnish cloth** (often used for silverware) or tuck a **piece of acid-free tissue** inside.

**3. Add Silica Gel (Optional but Helpful):**  
Throw in a **silica gel pack** (like the kind from electronics packaging) to absorb moisture—just make sure it’s not in direct contact with the watch.

**4. Store Face-Up in a Stable Spot:**  
Keep it lying flat, face up, and **away from magnets** (like speakers), **extreme heat or cold**, or direct sunlight.

**5. Wind Occasionally (If You Feel Confident):**  
Winding it once a month keeps the internal lubricants distributed. But if you're unsure or it's never been serviced, **don’t overwind**.
