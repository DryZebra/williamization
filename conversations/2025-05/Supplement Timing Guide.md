# Supplement Timing Guide

## Assistant

Great setup — you’ve got all the core minerals and vitamins covered. Here’s a simple breakdown of when to take each for best absorption and minimal side effects:

---

**1. Multivitamin**  
**Best time:** **Morning with food**  
- Most multivitamins contain B vitamins and possibly iron, which can cause nausea on an empty stomach and may interfere with sleep if taken late.  
- Taking it with food improves absorption and reduces stomach upset.

**2. Iodized Sea Salt**  
**Best time:** **Use throughout the day in food**  
- No need to “take” it at a specific time. Just ensure you’re getting a reasonable amount daily (1/2 tsp = about 1,150mg sodium and full daily iodine).  
- If you’re fasting or on low-sodium, you can mix a pinch in warm water (like sole water) and sip it slowly in the morning.

**3. Potassium Salt (e.g. NoSalt)**  
**Best time:** **Split between morning and evening, small doses**  
- Too much at once can irritate the stomach or cause heart palpitations.  
- Add 1/8 to 1/4 tsp to meals or water 1–2x/day. Works well combined with sea salt.

**4. Magnesium (Glycinate or Citrate)**  
**Best time:** **Evening**  
- Magnesium helps relax muscles and calm the nervous system, so it's best at night.  
- If you’re taking glycinate, it’s especially calming; citrate may help with digestion or constipation.

---

**Summary schedule:**

| Time      | Supplement                      | Notes                           |
|-----------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Morning   | Multivitamin + breakfast        | Absorption + energy boost       |
| Anytime   | Sea salt in food or drink       | Use naturally                   |
| Morning   | Potassium (small dose)          | Start replenishing              |
| Evening   | Magnesium + optional potassium  | Relaxation + balance electrolytes |

**1. Multivitamin**  
**Take:** **Evening (with your electrolyte dose)**

**2. Iodized Sea Salt**  
**Take:** **Morning and evening**  
- Add **1/4 tsp** to a glass of warm water (or split into two 1/8 tsp doses).  
- Sip slowly — this will help with dizziness, fatigue, and sodium loss.

**3. Potassium Salt (NoSalt)**  
**Take:** **Split — once in the morning, once in the evening**  
- **1/8 tsp at a time**, mixed with your sea salt drink or separately.  
- Don't exceed 1/4 tsp per dose unless you're sweating heavily or cramping.  
- Sip over time to avoid stomach upset or heart palpitations.

**4. Magnesium (Glycinate/Citrate)**  
**Take:** **Evening, 1–2 hours before sleep**  
- Helps prevent muscle cramps, headaches, and helps with sleep.  
- Magnesium glycinate is easier on the stomach during fasting than citrate.

---

### **Example Day (Fasting)**

|--------|------------|--------------------------------------|

| 9pm    | Magnesium + more water | Helps sleep/cramps |

---

**Important Tips:**
- **Always mix salts in water** and drink gradually — never dry or in capsules during fasting.
- **Watch for symptoms**: dizziness = more sodium; fatigue or muscle weakness = more potassium/magnesium.
- **Avoid iron-containing multis** if you get nausea — iron is roughest without food.
