**Meal: Ground Beef and Sweet Potato Hash with Eggs and Garlic Yogurt**

**Shopping List (all from a regular grocery store):**
- 1.5 lb ground beef (80/20 or leaner)
- 2 large sweet potatoes
- 1 yellow onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 4–6 eggs
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- Olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional: smoked paprika or cumin

---

**Prep Instructions (done in one session, ~30–40 min):**

1. **Peel and dice** the sweet potatoes into small cubes. Toss in olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 400°F for ~25 minutes until soft and browning.
2. While that roasts, **dice the onion and mince the garlic.**
3. In a large skillet, **brown the ground beef** with onion and half the garlic until fully cooked. Season with salt, pepper, and (optional) paprika or cumin.
4. When the sweet potatoes are done, **toss them into the skillet** and mix thoroughly. Let it cook a few more minutes so the flavors soak in.
5. In a separate pan or the same skillet cleared, **fry or poach the eggs** to your liking.
6. In a small bowl, mix **Greek yogurt with the remaining garlic**, a bit of olive oil, and salt—this is your sauce.

---

**Serve:**
- Pile the hash into bowls.
- Top with 1–2 eggs each.
- Drizzle or dollop the garlic yogurt on top.

---

**Meal: Chicken and Mahatma Rice Burritos**

**Shopping List (for 2 people, 1 meal):**
- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (or breasts if preferred)
- 1 cup Mahatma white rice
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 large flour tortillas
- Olive oil
- Salt, pepper, chili powder (or paprika if you want it milder)

**Optional Add-ons (if budget/availability allows):**
- 1 avocado
- 1 lime
- Cilantro or shredded cheese

---

**Evening Prep (cook once, 30 min):**

1. **Cook the rice**: 
   - 1 cup Mahatma rice to 2 cups water, a pinch of salt. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cover 15 minutes.
   - Once done, fluff and let it sit uncovered.

2. **Cook the chicken**:
   - Season with salt, pepper, chili powder. 
   - Sear in olive oil over medium heat ~5 min per side until cooked through. Let it rest, then chop or shred.

3. **Sauté onion and garlic**:
   - Dice the onion, mince garlic. Cook in the same pan as the chicken until soft and golden.

4. **Build the mix**:
   - Combine chopped chicken, cooked rice, and sautéed onion/garlic in a large bowl. Mix well.

5. **Assemble**:
   - Spoon the mix into tortillas. Add avocado, squeeze of lime, or cheese if you’ve got it. Roll tight.

---

**Storage:**
- Wrap in foil or parchment. Easy to eat cold or reheat in a pan or microwave.
