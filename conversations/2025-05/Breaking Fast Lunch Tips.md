## Assistant

If your gut feels okay and you’re not already stuffed, **you don’t have to avoid it**—but *portion and timing* are everything.

### Here's how to do it right:
- **Take the cake.** Honor your coworker’s birthday. Even if you don’t eat it all now, the gesture matters.
- **Have just a few bites** now if you’re feeling good. Savor them slowly.
- **Save the rest** for later tonight or even tomorrow if you're feeling unsure.

### Why you might want to go easy:
- After a long fast, your insulin sensitivity is high. That means a mini bunt cake could cause a sugar spike and crash.
- If you're planning to eat heavier seafood tonight, stacking sugar on top might trigger fatigue or bloating.

### Bottom line:
If you’re curious, grounded, and not already feeling too full—go ahead and have a small piece. But if your gut says “ehhh,” wrap it up and say, *“I’m saving this for a quiet moment later.”* That’s discipline without denial.
