**GROCERY LIST (Walmart – Whole Foods Only):**

**Proteins:**
- 1 pack thick-cut bacon (1 lb)
- 1 pack chicken thighs (2–3 lbs, bone-in/skin-on is cheapest)
- 1 block sharp cheddar cheese (8 oz)

**Vegetables:**
- 1 large yellow onion  
- 2 green bell peppers  
- 2 tomatoes  
- 1 small bag spinach (5–8 oz)  
- 1 head of broccoli  
- 1 zucchini  

**Carbs/Staples:**
- 1 bag red potatoes (3–5 lbs)  
- 1 loaf real sourdough or whole wheat bread  
- Olive oil  
- Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika  

---

**TONIGHT’S COOKUP:**

**1. OMELETTE (Dinner Tonight + Breakfast Tomorrow):**  
- Chop onion, bell pepper, tomato, spinach.  
- Cook half the bacon, chop it up.  
- Sauté all veggies in bacon fat with some olive oil if needed.  
- Scramble 10–12 eggs, add grated cheddar, pour over veggies, fold with bacon.  
- Slice and store leftovers for breakfast tomorrow.

**2. ROASTED CHICKEN + VEGGIES (Tomorrow's Lunch + Dinner):**  
- Cut potatoes, zucchini, broccoli. Toss with oil, salt, paprika.  
- Lay out chicken thighs on baking sheet, season heavy (salt, pepper, garlic).  
- Roast at 400°F for 45 minutes. Store in containers for lunch and dinner.

**3. Toast Slices (Side or Snack):**  
- Slice and toast sourdough. Serve with dinner or eat cold with eggs.

---

- **Breakfast:** Leftover omelette + toast

---

**SCRAMBLED EGGS WITH SAUSAGE, POTATO, TOMATO & CHEESE**

**Ingredients:**
- 8–10 eggs  
- 1/2 lb breakfast sausage  
- 1 large russet potato  
- 1 ripe tomato  
- 1/2 onion  
- 1/2 bell pepper  
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar  
- Salt, black pepper, garlic powder  
- Oil (olive or any)

---

**Instructions (cook all at once tonight):**

1. **Prep the potato:**  
   - Dice it small (like ½-inch cubes). Toss in a hot oiled pan with a pinch of salt and cook until browned and soft (10–12 min).

2. **Add sausage:**  
   - Crumble it into the pan with the potatoes. Let it brown and mix into the crispy edges.

3. **Add veggies:**  
   - Dice onion and bell pepper, throw 'em in. Cook until soft (5 min).  
   - Dice the tomato and toss it in last—let it break down a little and add moisture.

4. **Eggs in:**  
   - Beat your eggs in a bowl with salt, pepper, garlic powder. Pour over the whole skillet.  
   - Stir constantly over medium heat. Let it set just a little, then fold gently.

5. **Cheese last:**  
   - Once eggs are barely done, kill the heat and stir in cheese. Let it melt in the residual heat.

6. **Eat half now, save half for breakfast.**

---

**Why it works:**  
The potato adds chew, sausage brings fat, tomato keeps it juicy, and the cheese glues it all together. It’s rich without being greasy. **Feels like a meal, not a side dish.**

---

**Pair it with:**  
- A slice of toast if you need carbs.  
- A little hot sauce or avocado if you want to fancy it up tomorrow.
