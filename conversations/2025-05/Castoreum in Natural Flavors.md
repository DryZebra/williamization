## Assistant

You're thinking of **castoreum**.

Castoreum is a secretion from the castor sacs (located near the anal glands) of beavers. It has a musky, vanilla-like scent and has historically been used in flavorings and perfumes. In food, it has sometimes been labeled under the catch-all term "natural flavors," especially in vanilla, raspberry, or strawberry flavorings.

That said, it's *extremely* rare today in food production due to the difficulty and cost of harvesting it. Most “natural flavorings” come from plant-based sources or synthesized versions, even when labeled as natural.
