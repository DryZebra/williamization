# Grocery List for Iftar

I need groceries for tomorrow. Breaking fast at 12pm for 2 people, include dinner and lunch only.

## Assistant

Alright. You're feeding two people, breaking fast at noon, and only need **lunch and dinner**. Here's your **single grocery list** for today—simple, powerful, and real. No fluff.

---

**DAILY GROCERY LIST (2 PEOPLE – LUNCH & DINNER ONLY):**

**Proteins:**
- 1.5 lb chicken thighs (bone-in or boneless, your call)
- 4 large eggs

**Vegetables:**
- 1 bunch kale or collard greens
- 2 large carrots
- 1 yellow onion
- 1 head garlic

**Fats & Staples:**
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Red pepper flakes (optional, for flavor kick)

**Filler/Bulk:**
- 2 medium sweet potatoes
- 1/2 cup dry lentils (brown or green)

---

**PREP (Cook Once Tonight for Both Meals):**

**Step 1 – Roast Chicken & Veg:**
- Chop carrots, onion, and sweet potatoes into chunks.
- Toss with oil, salt, pepper.
- Lay chicken thighs on top, skin-side up (if applicable).
- Roast everything at 400°F for ~40 minutes or until chicken hits temp and sweet potatoes are soft.
- Save half the roasted chicken + veg for lunch.

**Step 2 – Greens & Lentils:**
- Boil lentils in salted water ~20 min till tender, drain.
- In a pan: oil, garlic, red pepper flakes, then chopped kale. Wilt it down. Add lentils. Season.
- Crack in 4 eggs, cover, and let steam-cook until set.
- This is your dinner: garlicky greens, protein-rich lentils, and eggs over top.

---

**TOMORROW’S MEALS:**

**Lunch (Post-Fast):**  
Roast chicken, sweet potatoes, and carrots reheated together.

**Dinner:**  
Lentil-garlic greens and eggs. Warm it in a pan or microwave.

---
