# Conduit Body Support NEC

## User

Where in the NEC does it say conduit bodies need to be supported on both sides within three feet?

## Assistant



## Assistant

The NEC (National Electrical Code) does **not** explicitly state that **conduit bodies** must be supported **on both sides within three feet**. However, this requirement is often derived from how conduit bodies are treated in relation to **conduit support rules**.

Here's how it breaks down:

---

### NEC 2023 — **Article 344.30(A)** (Rigid Metal Conduit) (Similar rules in Articles 342, 350, 352, 358, etc.):

> **"Supports shall be installed so that the raceway is securely fastened in place and shall be supported within 3 ft (900 mm) of each outlet box, junction box, device box, cabinet, conduit body, or other conduit termination."**

---

### Important Interpretation:
- A **conduit body** (such as an LB, T, or C) is considered a **termination point or junction** in the conduit run.
- The conduit **must be supported within 3 feet of each side** of a conduit body — **not because the conduit body needs support**, but because the **raceway must be supported** within 3 feet **of its termination** at that body.
- If the conduit body has entries on both sides, that means you need **a support within 3 feet on both sides** of it.

---

### Reference Example:
This interpretation is often confirmed in the **NEC Handbook commentary**, which states:

> *"Conduit bodies are considered terminations, and therefore raceways entering or leaving them must be supported within 3 feet of the connection."*
