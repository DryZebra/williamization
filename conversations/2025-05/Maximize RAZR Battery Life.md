My girlfriend is getting a Motorola Razer 2024 edition and I want to help her set it up so that it gets max battery life and smooth reliability. She's probably going to just click "accept" for everything so I need to make it clear that she must deny everything.

## Assistant

Absolutely — that instinct is spot on. Phones like the Motorola RAZR 2024 (especially with a foldable display) can last a long time **only** if they're treated like utility devices, not ad farms. Here's a **clear and direct guide** you can walk her through during setup to **maximize battery life and smoothness**, while minimizing background crap.

---

### ✅ BEFORE SETUP
1. **Plug it in** and let it fully charge before the first boot.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi**, but don't insert a SIM yet. (It’ll reduce distractions.)

---

### 🔒 DURING INITIAL SETUP — What to Say YES / NO To:
| Prompt | What to Do | Why |
|-------|------------|------|
| Google Terms & Conditions | Accept | Required |
| Location Sharing | **Deny** | Enables constant GPS pings |
| Send Diagnostic Data | **Deny** | Useless battery-hogging telemetry |
| Allow Apps to Use Location | **Deny All** | You can grant later per app |
| Install Recommended Apps | **Deny** | Often includes junkware |
| Backup to Google Drive | **Optional** | Good for peace of mind, minor impact |
| Google Assistant Setup | **Skip** | Always-listening mic drains battery |
| Allow Ads Personalization | **Deny** | Tracks you for profit |
| Accept Motorola Services | **Deny Everything** | No benefit, just more telemetry |
| Auto System Updates | **Enable** | Security updates are good |

---

### ⚙️ AFTER SETUP — Settings to Tweak Immediately
> Go to: **Settings > Battery > Battery Saver**
- Turn on **Battery Saver schedule**: Based on routine or 20%
- Enable **Extreme Battery Saver** for emergencies

> Go to: **Settings > Apps > See all apps > [3-dot menu > Show system]**
- Disable or uninstall:
  - Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc. (can be reinstalled if truly needed)
  - Motorola notifications or “Tips” apps
  - Any “Demo Mode” or “Retail Experience” apps

> Go to: **Settings > Display**
- Reduce **screen timeout** to 30s or 1min
- Set **dark mode** ON permanently (OLED saves power)
- Disable **smooth scrolling** or set refresh rate to 60Hz if you don’t need buttery animation

> Go to: **Settings > Location**
- Turn OFF if not needed
- Set most apps to "Allow only while using"

> Go to: **Settings > Notifications**
- Turn OFF notifications for every app she doesn’t need interrupting her

> Go to: **Settings > Accounts > Google > Ads**
- Turn off **Ad Personalization**

---

### 📱 Optional but HIGHLY Recommended
- **Install Simple Keyboard** or another light keyboard to reduce RAM/battery usage
- Replace Chrome with **Brave** or **DuckDuckGo** browser
- Install **Greenify** or **Battery Guru** (for tech-savvy monitoring)
- Use **Simple Mobile Tools** apps (Gallery, Notes, etc.) — they’re clean, ad-free, and private

---

### 🔋 Habits That Extend Battery Life
- Keep **screen brightness low**
- Don’t leave **Bluetooth or Location** on all the time
- Fully restart the phone **once a week**
- Avoid **live wallpapers**, constant notifications, or background-running social apps
