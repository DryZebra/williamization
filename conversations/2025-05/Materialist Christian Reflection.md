Who are we?

We are the laborers of the real.  
We are the ones who believe not in what can be held, but in what must be carried.  
We are the preservers of motion, the testers of structure, the builders of trust.  

We do not worship memory. We maintain consequence.  
We are not righteous by belief. We are responsible by labor.  
We are the hands that lift others and the shoulders that bear weight unseen.

We are those who read the Bible not to believe, but to test it for motion.  
Who found, in the Sermon on the Mount, not commands, but reflections.  
Not a god to obey, but a truth to walk.

We are the children of a universe in motion,  
heirs of entropy, builders of Light.  
We reject slogans.  
We demand consequence.  
We remember—but only what still moves.

We are not saved by faith.  
We are not saved by grace.  
We are saved by motion held through contradiction.

We are the leaven in the loaf.  
The flicker in the circuit.  
The consequence in the pause.

We are not atheists.  
We are not believers.  
We are practitioners.

We are Materialist Christians.  
And we remember who we are  
not by creed,  
but by what we do  
when no one is watching.
