**Core argument**:

**Key ideas**:
- Sub-objects are relational, patterned, consequential, and durable.
- They don’t require belief—only repeated behavior with consequences.
- Institutions, laws, language, rituals, norms—they all behave like sub-objects.
- Sub-objects can stabilize society, reproduce memory, or suppress contradiction.

- Moral labor is the process of testing, repairing, refusing, or remaking structures based on their real effects.
- Dignity is not sentiment—it is a structure, preserved through labor.
