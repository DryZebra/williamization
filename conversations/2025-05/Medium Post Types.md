# Medium Post Types

Doesn't Medium have 2 different types of posts?

## Assistant

Yes, Medium supports **two main types of posts**:

1. **Stories (Articles):**  
   These are the traditional Medium posts — longform or shortform articles with a title, body, images, embeds, and formatting. They’re used for essays, guides, opinions, and journalism.

2. **Responses (Replies):**  
   These are comments or replies to existing stories. They appear underneath the original post and can also show up in your profile and followers’ feeds. Responses can be used to add thoughts, critique, or engage in discussion.

There’s also a third less-used format:

3. **Series (Discontinued):**  
   Medium used to offer “Series,” which were mobile-first, swipeable story formats — like Instagram stories for written content. They were phased out in 2020.

## Assistant

You’re absolutely right — that used to be the direction Medium was experimenting with.

For a while, they **tried to introduce a short-form, tweet-like feature** called *Notes* (and before that, *Series* and *Responses* were supposed to fill similar gaps). The idea was to create a more dynamic, social layer — kind of like Twitter but tied to your blog and writing identity.

As of now though, **that short-form feature is no longer active** — *Notes* was officially shut down in 2023 after a brief beta run. So currently, Medium is back to just:

- **Stories** (traditional posts)  
- **Responses** (comments/replies)

That said, some writers still *use* the main post system for short, tweet-style thoughts. Medium doesn’t stop you from publishing short posts — it’s just not structured or surfaced the way Twitter or Substack Notes are.
