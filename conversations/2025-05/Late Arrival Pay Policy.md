## Assistant

Based on the **Inside Recovery Agreement** and standard practices in the IBEW/NECA framework, here’s what you need to know:

### 1. **Gang Box Start Time Enforcement**
Your contractor’s policy of making workers sit out until 7:30 for being a minute late is **not mentioned or authorized** anywhere in the Recovery Agreement. There is a clause that says:

> “All workmen shall be at the point designated by the Employer and be ready for work at the designated time for starting work.”【11†current recovery 2025.pdf】

This means workers are expected to be *ready* to work at start time—but it doesn't authorize punishment beyond normal disciplinary channels. There's **no clause** allowing withholding pay or imposing unpaid sit-outs as punishment.

### 2. **Time and Location Clarification**
The contract **does not define whether the start time is at the gate or the gang box**, but in practice, if badging through a gate and walking to the gang box is required, many stewards argue that should be factored in. If your contractor expects workers *at* the gang box by 7:00, then your arrival at the gate should allow enough time to meet that.

But a **30-minute sit** for being **1 minute late** is clearly **not proportionate**, nor is it supported by contract language.

### 3. **Contractor Rules vs. Agreement**
The agreement allows employers to set rules, but they **cannot conflict** with the terms of the agreement. Forcing a worker to sit for 30 minutes unpaid may violate the CBA’s intent to provide fair compensation for actual work time and may be a **grievable issue**.

> “All grievances shall be received and handled first by the Site Local Union... in writing within 15 working days of its occurrence...”【11:4†current recovery 2025.pdf】

### 4. **What You Should Do**
- **Document the policy.** Get a witness or write down who, when, and how the rule was applied.
- **Report to your job steward or business agent** immediately. This sounds like a strong candidate for a grievance.
- You have **15 working days** to file a grievance officially.
