## Assistant

The setting shown in your image is the **diopter adjustment** on your binoculars. It’s used to fine-tune focus **for differences between your eyes**.

### How to use it:
1. **Close your right eye** and use the **center focus wheel** to focus on an object with your left eye.
2. **Then close your left eye**, open your right eye, and turn this **diopter ring** (the one in your picture, usually on the right eyepiece) until the object is in sharp focus for your right eye.
3. Once done, you only need to use the center focus wheel for general focusing during normal use.

The `+` and `-` symbols indicate adjustments for farsightedness and nearsightedness. The `0` is the neutral middle point.
