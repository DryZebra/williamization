# Apple Watch Android Compatibility

## User

Can I use an apple watch with an android phone?

## Assistant



## Assistant

No, you **cannot use an Apple Watch with an Android phone** in any official or supported way.

Apple Watch requires an **iPhone** for initial setup and ongoing functionality. Even if you borrow an iPhone to set it up, features like messaging, notifications, and health tracking won't fully work unless the watch remains connected to an iPhone.

If you're using an Android phone, here are some **alternatives** to Apple Watch that work well:

- **Samsung Galaxy Watch series** – best overall for Android, especially with Samsung phones.
- **Pixel Watch** – great for Pixel and other Android phones.
- **Garmin** – excellent for fitness tracking.
- **Fitbit** – good for health and wellness, though owned by Google now.
