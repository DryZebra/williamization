A **Proverbs 31 woman** refers to the idealized woman described in **Proverbs 31:10–31**, often called the *"Virtuous Woman"* or *"Wife of Noble Character."* This passage is a poetic acrostic in Hebrew, where each verse begins with a successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet. It's a tribute, not a prescription—a celebration of a strong, wise, capable woman from a time when such qualities were rarely praised.

Here’s a summary of her key qualities:

- **Trustworthy and dependable** – Her husband trusts her fully (v. 11).
- **Hardworking** – She works with willing hands, rising early, and managing her home and business (vv. 13–19).
- **Entrepreneurial** – She considers a field and buys it, plants a vineyard, and trades profitably (vv. 16, 18, 24).
- **Compassionate** – She opens her hands to the poor and needy (v. 20).
- **Prepared and organized** – Her household is clothed and prepared for all seasons (vv. 21–22).
- **Wise and kind** – She speaks with wisdom, and her kindness is consistent (v. 26).
- **Honored by her family** – Her children and husband praise her (v. 28).
- **God-fearing** – Above all, she is praised for fearing the Lord (v. 30).

**Key Verse (Proverbs 31:30):**  
*"Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain, but a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised."*

In context, this passage wasn’t originally written *to* women but to men—King Lemuel’s mother advising her son what kind of woman is truly worthy of praise. Today, many Christian women strive toward these traits not as a checklist, but as a portrait of strength, dignity, and spiritual depth.
