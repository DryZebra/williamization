# Gigabit Ethernet Switch Function

## User

What's a gigabit Ethernet switch for?

## Assistant



## Assistant

A **gigabit Ethernet switch** is a network device that connects multiple devices (like computers, printers, game consoles, smart TVs, etc.) on a local area network (LAN) and allows them to communicate with each other at speeds up to **1 gigabit per second (Gbps)**.

Here’s what it’s for:

1. **Expands wired network connections**: Lets you plug in more devices using Ethernet cables when your router doesn’t have enough ports.

2. **Faster local data transfer**: Enables high-speed sharing of files between devices on your network (e.g., from your PC to your NAS or server).

3. **Low-latency, stable connections**: Ideal for gaming, streaming, or workstations where Wi-Fi might be unreliable or too slow.

4. **Acts as a central traffic director**: Each device can talk to others without slowing the whole network down, since it manages traffic intelligently.

If your home or office has more than a few devices that need reliable, high-speed wired internet, a gigabit switch is essential.
