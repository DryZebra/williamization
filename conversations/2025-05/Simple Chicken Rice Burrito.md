## Assistant

That’s a solid workingman’s burrito right there. Here's how I’d do it, stripped to the bone but loaded with flavor and calories that stick to your ribs.

**Ingredients (makes 4 big burritos):**
- 1 lb chicken thighs or breasts, chopped
- 1 cup Mahatma yellow rice (dry)
- 2 cups water
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack cheese
- 4 large flour tortillas
- Sour cream (optional, for topping)
- Spices: salt, black pepper, cumin, chili powder, paprika, oil

**Evening Prep (cook once, eat all day):**

1. **Pan fry** chopped chicken in oil with salt, pepper, cumin, chili powder, and paprika. Get some color on it.
2. **Add onion and garlic** to the same pan, sauté till soft and browned.
3. **Pour in 2 cups water** and 1 cup Mahatma yellow rice. Stir it up, bring to a simmer, cover, and let it cook till rice is tender (about 20 minutes).
4. **Once done**, stir in shredded pepper jack until melted throughout.
5. Let it cool slightly, then spoon into 4 tortillas. Wrap tight. Portion out.

**Meals:**
- **Breakfast**: Eat cold or microwave one burrito with a little sour cream.
- **Lunch**: Room temp or warmed, it holds well in foil.
- **Dinner**: Toast it in a dry pan for a crispy finish, add sour cream if you got it.

**Optional grit:** Add a diced jalapeño while frying if you want heat. Add canned beans if you need more bulk. But this version? It gets the job done with zero waste and max flavor.
