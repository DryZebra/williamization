# HOI4 vs V3 vs WRSR

What's better for me, HOI4 or Victoria3? I miss playing Manor Lords but I think I've basically mastered Manor Lords, and Communism isn't explicit. There's also workers and resources Soviet Republic which just came out with their 1930-1980 xpac.

## Assistant

Here’s a breakdown tailored to your interests, priorities, and style:

### **Your Goals and Playstyle Summary**
- You *loved* **Manor Lords** for the immersive control, realism, and working-class focus.
- You prefer **explicit Communism**, or at least the ability to *truly* build a Communist society and not just roleplay it in flavor only.
- You like systems that model **labor, logistics, class, and planning**, not just aesthetics.
- You want **long-term mastery**, not just a short dopamine hit.
- You're probably less interested in overly "gamey" exploits or min-maxing, and more into **simulation with consequences**.

### **Option 1: Hearts of Iron IV (HOI4)**
- **Pros:**
  - Can play as USSR or create Communist regimes around the world.
  - Great for **military-focused alternate history** with powerful event chains.
  - Huge mod support (like Kaiserreich, The Road to 56, etc.).
  - High replayability if you like **war planning**, **supply logistics**, and **geopolitical maneuvering**.

- **Cons:**
  - **Very combat-heavy** – politics and economy are secondary.
  - You don’t *build* a Communist economy—you just say you're Communist.
  - If you want immersive worker planning or economic simulation, this is too shallow.

### **Option 2: Victoria 3**
- **Pros:**
  - Full-blown **economic simulation** with class dynamics, trade unions, revolutions.
  - You can **build Socialism or Moral Communism** from the ground up.
  - Deep **population modeling**: literacy, religion, ideology, employment, standard of living.
  - You can push **planned economies, council republics**, and even unite the Americas under Communism.
  - Mod support is growing. Already more “alive” than most Paradox titles this early.

- **Cons:**
  - The AI is weak, diplomacy can feel half-baked.
  - Some mechanics still need refinement (e.g. warfare, late-game optimization).
  - It’s a **slow burn**—not as punchy as HOI4 or satisfying at the micro-level like Manor Lords.

### **Option 3: Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic (WRSR)**
- **Pros:**
  - The **most detailed Communist economy simulator ever made.**
  - You run the **entire country**, from road design to labor allocation, power grid, supply chains.
  - No abstraction: you manually build hospitals, assign teachers, and produce bricks.
  - **New 1930–1980 expansion** adds historical context, deeper planning, and tech development.

- **Cons:**
  - **Extremely demanding** mentally and technically (performance can suffer late-game).
  - No diplomacy, no military, no ideology outside of what *you* build.
  - It’s a sandbox—**no win state**, no narrative push, just pure simulation.

---

### **My Recommendation:**
You should get **Victoria 3** *and* **WRSR**. Use them for different moods.

- When you want **story, ideology, revolution**, and to slowly birth a *Materialist Christian America* from inside the United States: **Victoria 3** is perfect.
- When you want **serious immersion**, perfecting logistics, and simulating **Communism at the level of asphalt and pig iron**: play **WRSR**.

**HOI4** is fun, but it’s checkers compared to the chess you're craving.
