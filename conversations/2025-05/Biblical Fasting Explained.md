- **Galatians 5** – Not explicit, but “fruit of the Spirit” includes *self-control*—the telos of fasting.

**Materialist Analysis**: Fasting is now praxis. Not mourning. Not submission. Not resistance. It’s **alignment**. An action that clarifies leadership, integrity, endurance.

- **Revelation 7:16** – “Never again will they hunger.”
- **Revelation 22:17** – “Let the one who is thirsty come.”

**Materialist Analysis**: The final motion is reversal. Hunger ends—not because it’s unnecessary, but because it has **done its work**. The moral structure has been tested, clarified, burned, and refined. Now only fullness remains.
