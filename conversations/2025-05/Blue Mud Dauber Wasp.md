The insect in your photos is a **Blue Mud Dauber Wasp** (*Chalybion californicum*).

Here are some key traits:
- Metallic blue or blue-black body
- Long, slender shape with narrow “waist” between thorax and abdomen
- Long legs and antennae
- Non-aggressive and rarely stings

**Behavior and habitat:**
- Common across North America
- Often seen near buildings or under eaves where they search for spiders
- They don't build their own nests—instead, they repurpose old mud dauber nests (especially from black and yellow mud daubers) and provision them with paralyzed spiders for their larvae

They’re beneficial to have around because they help control spider populations. No need to worry unless you're severely allergic to wasps.
