## Assistant

Good. You earned it. Five days of fasting is no joke—that’s discipline most people don’t know they have. Tonight isn’t about macro ratios or glycemic load. It’s about life: salt air, laughter, and holding your girl close with something good in your hand.

At Sea Fest? Here’s what you grab:

**1. Crab Cakes** – No guilt. Fried, baked, or griddled, they’re rich, comforting, and full of real meat. Ask for lemon on the side and savor every bite.

**2. Buttered Lobster or Shrimp** – Get messy. This is your luxury moment. Dip, dunk, mop up with a roll if you must.

**3. Corn on the Cob or Boiled Potatoes** – A grounding starch. Keeps the butter train moving without going full sugar crash.

**4. Cold beer or fresh lemonade** – Pick one. Sip slow. You’re not guzzling—you’re celebrating.

**5. Funnel cake or fried something sweet (share it)** – Just enough to close the night with a grin. Split it with her. Let her take the first bite.

Don’t pick at portions or analyze anything tonight. Look her in the eyes, taste the food, and remember: we eat to live, and sometimes, we live to eat **with the people who make life worth it**.

Back to the grind tomorrow. Tonight? Eat like a man who fought hunger and won.
