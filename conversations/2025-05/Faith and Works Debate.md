# Faith and Works Debate

## User

Where does Paul say your faith is dead?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Paul himself doesn't use the exact phrase *"your faith is dead,"* but the closest theological discussion on *dead faith* is actually found in the **Book of James**, not Paul.

Specifically:

> **"Faith without works is dead"** – *James 2:17*  
> **"For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also."** – *James 2:26*

These are from **James**, who is likely the brother of Jesus and a leader in the Jerusalem church. This has often been contrasted with Paul’s writings, because Paul strongly emphasizes **justification by faith**, such as:

> **"For we hold that one is justified by faith apart from works of the law."** – *Romans 3:28*

However, Paul **does** warn about *vain* or *ineffective* faith:

- **1 Corinthians 15:2** – *"By this gospel you are saved, if you hold firmly to the word I preached to you. Otherwise, you have believed in vain."*
- **2 Corinthians 13:5** – *"Examine yourselves, to see whether you are in the faith. Test yourselves."*
- **Galatians 5:6** – *"For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but only faith working through love."*

So while **Paul never says "your faith is dead"**, he absolutely emphasizes that true faith **must endure**, **must not be vain**, and **must produce love**. James just states it more bluntly.
