### Here's what makes them exceptional:

- **Highly potent venom**: Black widow venom isn’t just powerful against vertebrates—it’s *lethal* to other arthropods. They can take down prey far larger than themselves with a single bite and quick envenomation.

- **Documented spider-killers**: Studies and spider forums both report *Latrodectus* species killing:
  - Orb-weavers (*Araneus*, *Neoscona*)
  - Jumping spiders (*Phidippus*)
  - Even other widows (cannibalism isn't off the table)
