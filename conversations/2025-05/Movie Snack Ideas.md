# Movie Snack Ideas

## User

I'm going to hang out with a friend and watch a movie. I don't want pizza, what food should I offer to pick up and bring?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Great question—here are some crowd-pleasing, non-pizza options that travel well and are easy to eat during a movie:

---

**1. Tacos or Burritos (Mexican Takeout):**  
- Easy to customize for picky eaters  
- You can grab a mix of meat, vegetarian, and spicy options  
- Chips and guac/salsa travel great

**2. Sushi Platters or Poke Bowls:**  
- Clean, fresh, and upscale feel  
- You can eat quietly and cleanly during a movie  
- Bonus: chopsticks = less grease on fingers

**3. Chicken Tenders or Wings (with sauces on the side):**  
- Portable and fun  
- Go boneless if you want less mess  
- Fries or tots as a side

**4. Mediterranean Box (Gyros, Falafel, Hummus, Pita):**  
- Feels light but filling  
- Shareable, and you can mix and match bites  
- Bonus: Usually comes with pickled veggies and salad

**5. Banh Mi or Asian Fusion Sandwiches:**  
- Unique but familiar  
- Easy to split or eat whole  
- Usually comes with crisp veggies and flavorful sauces

**6. Indian Street Food (Samosas, Naan wraps, Chaat):**  
- Finger-friendly or fork-friendly depending on the dish  
- Great for variety and spice  

**7. Fancy Sandwiches or Wraps:**  
- Think roasted veggies, turkey + brie, club wraps, etc.  
- Cut them in halves or quarters for sharing
