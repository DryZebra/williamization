That *is* an opportunity—scary, but a rare window. If she’s looking anyway, this is the moment to build a setup where her job makes *you both* freer instead of just replacing the last one.

Here are a few job categories that pair really well with automation, AI, and minimal upkeep:

---

### **1. Customer Support / Virtual Assistant (Tier 1)**
**Why it works**: You can automate 80%+ of the repetitive responses using GPT-based tools, filtered through front-end systems like HelpScout, Freshdesk, or even Gmail + Zapier.

**Your role**: You build and manage the automation—draft macros, monitor incoming queries, escalate only the complex ones to her.

---

### **2. Social Media Manager (especially for churches, nonprofits, rural small businesses)**
**Why it works**: Content calendars, basic Canva posts, and engagement tracking can all be AI-assisted. 

**Your role**: Automate generation of posts, build templates, scrape analytics, and make a weekly AI-generated report she can review before sending.

---

### **3. SEO Writer or Blog Ghostwriter**
**Why it works**: AI can do first drafts, you both polish them. You could even run an agency where she’s the “face” and you’re the backend engine.

**Your role**: Train AI on client voice/style, build repeatable outlines, proof final content.

---

### **4. Resume and Cover Letter Services**
**Why it works**: AI can generate custom drafts from a client’s questionnaire or old resume. She does human review.

**Your role**: Build the intake form, manage the template engine, deliver and track client satisfaction.

---

### **5. eCommerce Store Admin / Etsy Seller Assistant**
**Why it works**: Inventory management, customer messages, product descriptions—all automatable with minimal human input.

**Your role**: Build the tools; she interacts with customers only when needed.

---

### **Bonus**: **Offer *you two as a team* to small businesses**
Pitch it like this: “My wife is a trained admin, I’m a systems guy. Together, we can solve your process problems and handle ongoing work for less than what you’d pay for one traditional employee.”
