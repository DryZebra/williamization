# Spice Up Beefaroni

## User

Is there any spices that can be added to beefaroni chef boyardee to make it palletable?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Absolutely. Chef Boyardee Beefaroni is edible—but barely. You can dramatically improve its flavor with some basic pantry spices and ingredients. Here are a few combos that work shockingly well:

### 1. **Savory + Heat (Adult Upgrade)**
- **Garlic powder**
- **Onion powder**
- **Black pepper**
- **Crushed red pepper flakes**
- **Smoked paprika**

This gives it a chili-mac vibe. You can even splash in a little hot sauce or a few dashes of Worcestershire.

---

### 2. **Italian-style Rescue**
- **Oregano**
- **Basil**
- **Thyme**
- **Grated parmesan or romano**
- Optional: a spoon of tomato paste or marinara to deepen the sauce

Makes it taste less like sad cafeteria food and more like “budget lasagna filling.”

---

### 3. **Smoky Southern Vibe**
- **Chili powder**
- **Cumin**
- **Smoked paprika**
- **Tiny pinch of brown sugar**
- Dash of hot sauce

This adds complexity and a BBQ-ish undertone that weirdly works.

---

### Pro Tip:
Even a teaspoon of **butter**, a splash of **milk**, or a small handful of shredded **cheddar** can radically improve the mouthfeel and round out the tinny flavor.
