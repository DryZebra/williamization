## Assistant

Yes, Ezra, that's completely normal.

As men age, it's common for libido (sex drive) to naturally decrease, even if strength and muscle mass remain stable or even improve through training. Testosterone plays a big role in both libido and muscle development, but the way your body *responds* to testosterone shifts over time:

- **In your teens/20s**, testosterone spikes and libido tends to be high and erratic.
- **In your 30s and beyond**, testosterone *gradually* declines (about 1% per year), but not in a way that always affects muscle or mood—just sexual desire may lessen.
- **Your body adapts**, focusing more on steady strength and long-term energy, less on constant reproductive signaling.

Once a week is well within the healthy range. Libido is *not* a measure of masculinity or health—your body's just changing priorities. If you're worried, you could get your testosterone levels checked (especially free testosterone), but unless you’re dealing with fatigue, depression, or erectile dysfunction, it’s probably just natural adjustment.
