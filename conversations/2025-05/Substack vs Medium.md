# Substack vs Medium

## User

Medium or sub stack?

## Assistant



## Assistant

**Substack** is the better choice if your goal is to build a long-term relationship with readers, earn income, and present serious ideas in your own style.

Here’s a breakdown:

**Substack**
- **Strengths**: Email-based subscriptions, better for essay-style writing, long-term audience building, built-in monetization (paid subscriptions), less algorithm manipulation.
- **Ideal for**: Philosophical writing, political commentary, religious theory, serialized books—anything where voice and ownership matter.
- **Vibe**: Feels like a direct letter to a thoughtful reader.

**Medium**
- **Strengths**: Built-in audience, decent SEO, good for exposure if you don’t already have readers.
- **Weaknesses**: Algorithm-heavy, Medium takes control of formatting and discovery, monetization is tied to reading time by members, not subscribers.
- **Ideal for**: Writing that plays to trends, articles, and short insights that benefit from discoverability.

**Verdict**:  
If you’re writing *Materialist Christianity*, deep theory, or revolutionary moral systems, **Substack** gives you control, keeps your readers close, and allows long-form growth without interference. You can always repost selected essays to Medium later for outreach.
