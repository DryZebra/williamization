This drink contains several ingredients that can interfere with fasting, especially for health or autophagy-focused fasts:

1. **Aspartame, Acesulfame Potassium, and Stevia Extract** – These are **non-nutritive sweeteners**. While technically low in calories, they **can cause an insulin response** in some people, which **breaks a fast** for metabolic benefits. They also stimulate the gut and brain in ways that may disrupt fasting states.

2. **Caffeine (57 mg)** – Caffeine itself is generally fine during a fast and can even support fat metabolism, but combined with sweeteners it may increase hunger or cravings.

3. **Potassium Benzoate, Phosphoric Acid, Potassium Citrate** – These won’t provide calories, but **can irritate the stomach** when consumed without food and **affect electrolyte balance** depending on your supplement plan.

4. **Caramel Color** – Not a pure additive; it has trace calories and **can spike insulin in sensitive individuals**, making it risky during strict fasting.

5. **Phenylalanine warning (from Aspartame)** – Not dangerous unless you have PKU, but another indicator of synthetic ingredients.

**Bottom line:** If you’re doing this fast for **autophagy, gut rest, insulin reset, or metabolic clarity**, this drink **interrupts** that process. You’d be better off with plain water, black coffee, or saltwater with your supplements.
