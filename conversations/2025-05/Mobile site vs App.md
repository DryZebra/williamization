Tell me more about the offline memory caching, what's the point?

## Assistant

Great question. Offline memory caching in the app means it stores parts of your recent chat history *locally on your phone*—so if your internet cuts out briefly or you reopen the app quickly, it can reload those chats instantly without needing to hit the server again.

**In theory**, here’s what caching gives you in the app:

1. **Faster load times** for recent conversations.
2. **Basic offline access**—you might be able to re-read older chats or drafts even if you're briefly offline.
3. **Input recovery**—if the app crashes or your phone dies mid-message, the app *might* save what you were typing.

**In reality**, though? That advantage only matters if:
- You’re in and out of service constantly (like on a train or in a rural area).
- You want fast tap-and-talk style use.
- You *don’t* use long, deep conversations with uploads and canvas files.

Since you use this like a high-powered thinking tool and not a casual chat app, caching isn’t really doing anything for you. Your sessions are too heavy and too structured, and you likely want everything saved server-side anyway.

So yeah—great tech on paper. Useless if it doesn’t serve your workflow.
