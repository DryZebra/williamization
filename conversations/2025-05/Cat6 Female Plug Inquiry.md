In your second image, the jacks are branded **SYSTIMAX**, which is a line by CommScope. These are typically **Category 6 (Cat6) UTP Keystone Jacks**, punch-down style, used for terminating twisted pair cabling in patch panels or wall plates.

> **"Cat6 SYSTIMAX RJ45 Keystone Jacks (unshielded or shielded, depending on your install), preferably the MGS600 series if they have it."**

### CommScope SYSTIMAX Shielded Jack Series:

#### 1. **MGS600-XXX-SH (Shielded)**
- Rare but does exist — it's a **shielded variant** of the MGS600 series.
- May be listed with “SH” or “S” at the end (e.g., **MGS600-241-S**).
- Has a metal housing similar to what you’re holding.

#### 2. **GS8E Series** (older shielded)
- Fully shielded with metal clamshell body
- Grounding clip on the side
- Tool-assisted termination (not toolless like the TC-K06C6A)

#### 3. **SYSTIMAX Z-MAX SHIELD**
- High-performance Cat6A and Cat7-rated shielded jacks
- Typically used in higher-end enterprise or data center environments
- Part numbers like **ZC6A-SH**, **ZC7-SH**
