# Ferret Attraction Sounds

## User

I'm trying to find my ferret I think he's asleep somewhere. Is there a sound I can play from my phone speaker to wake and attract him?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Yes! Ferrets have sharp hearing and tend to react to high-pitched, fast-moving sounds. Try playing one of the following from your phone speaker:

1. **Squeaky toy sounds** – Search “squeaky toy sound” on YouTube or a soundboard app. These often trigger curiosity.

2. **Ferret chittering/squeaking noises** – Other ferret sounds can attract them, especially playful or excited ones.

3. **Crinkling bag sounds** – Like a treat bag or rustling plastic. If he associates that with food, it might wake him.

4. **Clicking or kissing noises** – Make a repetitive “tsk tsk” or kissing sound yourself; many ferrets respond strongly to this.

Also, try gently tapping his food bowl or shaking a treat jar.
