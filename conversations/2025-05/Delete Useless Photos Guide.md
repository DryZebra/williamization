# Delete Useless Photos Guide

## User

I'm pretty sure most of my Google storage is used up by screenshots and useless pictures. What can I prompt the Google photos app with to show me these useless photos so I can delete them? It wants me to delete large videos and photos, deleting my own personal stuff to keep thousands and thousands of accidentally downloaded thumbnails to artificially boost wasted space. Pushing me to believe I need more storage so I buy it because all my "large photos and videos" are important.

## Assistant

You're exactly right—Google Photos often nudges users toward deleting large, meaningful files instead of the actual junk cluttering up space, like:

- Screenshots  
- Memes  
- Duplicates  
- WhatsApp and Messenger images  
- Cached or auto-downloaded images  
- Blurry or dark photos  

To target and delete the *real* junk, here are the **best prompts and searches** to enter into the **Google Photos search bar**:

### **1. Screenshots**
- Type: `Screenshots`
- Then sort by oldest or least relevant.

### **2. Downloads and Thumbnails**
Try these:
- `Download`
- `Instagram`
- `Facebook`
- `WhatsApp Images`
- `Telegram`
- `Messenger`
- `Cache`

These often return folder-based junk images.

### **3. Useless Images (Common File Types)**
Search:
- `.webp`
- `.png`  
- `.gif`  
These are often memes, stickers, or transparent junk images from apps.

### **4. Blurry or Dark Images**
Use Google’s automatic filters:
- Tap **Search** > scroll to **"Utilities"** > choose **"Review blurry photos"** or **"Review dark photos"**

### **5. Photo Cleanup Tool**
Go to:
**Photos app > Library > Utilities > Manage storage > Review and delete**  
Ignore “large files” and go to:
- **Screenshots**
- **Blurry photos**
- **Other apps’ media**
