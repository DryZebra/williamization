# Dopamine Learning Apps

## Assistant

Yes—but it’s rare, and probably not branded as a “get smarter instead of TikTok” app. Most of those are self-help grifts or bland habit trackers. You’re looking for:

- **Real dopamine** (instant feedback, novelty, beauty, maybe mastery)
- **Actual value** (you genuinely get smarter or gain skill)
- **Retention** (you *want* to come back without guilt)

Here are a few that *might* hit the sweet spot:

---

### 1. **Brilliant**
- **Why it works:** Interactive, gamified learning in math, logic, physics, AI.
- **Dopamine:** Immediate rewards for solving things, sleek interface, streaks.
- **You’ll stay if:** You enjoy puzzle-solving or are curious about STEM.

---

### 2. **Socratic / Perplexity / Poe (AI Chat Apps)**
- **Why it works:** Ask anything, get surprising, mind-expanding answers.
- **Dopamine:** Instant novelty and control—you shape the experience.
- **You’ll stay if:** You ask weird, personal, or philosophical questions.

---

### 3. **Chess.com or Lichess**
- **Why it works:** Competitive, social, strategic mastery.
- **Dopamine:** Immediate win/loss feedback, puzzle rush mode is addicting.
- **You’ll stay if:** You want to feel clever and improve a skill.

---

### 4. **Reddit (with curated feeds)**
- **Why it works:** Still has that TikTok-like scroll, but can be tuned for niche learning.
- **Dopamine:** Constant novelty, tailored subreddits.
- **You’ll stay if:** You moderate your feed (e.g., r/AskHistorians, r/DepthHub).

---

### 5. **Tandem or HelloTalk**
- **Why it works:** Real conversations with people around the world while learning a language.
- **Dopamine:** Social novelty, mini rewards, fun mistakes, actual human feedback.
- **You’ll stay if:** You like chatting and want to learn a language.

---
