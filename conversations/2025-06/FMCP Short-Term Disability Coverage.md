Yes – all FMCP plans *can* include short‑term disability (STD), but whether it’s actually part of *your* plan depends on your employer’s specific agreement with FMCP.

---

## 🧾 What the SPDs Say

- **Plan 15 SPD** clearly includes a **“Weekly Disability Benefits”** section. However, it states:
  
  > “You are only entitled to Weekly Disability Benefits **if these benefits are included with your employer’s agreement with the Plan**.” citeturn0search0turn0search1

  This means if your employer opted into the STD option, Plan 15 includes it; if not, you won’t have STD coverage under this plan.

- I wasn’t able to locate a similar statement for **Plan 14** in the documents yet, but given the Statement of Material Modifications (SMM) notice updates to Plans 14, 15, 16, and 18, it’s likely their structure is similar: STD is optional and tied to your employer's election citeturn0search5.

---

## 📌 Plans Potentially Without STD

Because STD coverage is an **add-on based on employer agreement**, any plan (14, 15, 16, 17, 18, etc.) **could be missing STD** if your employer didn’t include it.

---

### ✅ How to Confirm If Your Plan *Doesn't* Have STD

1. **Inspect your SPD** (e.g., Plan 15 PDF) and look for a section titled *“Weekly Disability Benefits”*.
2. **Search within the SPD** for lines like:
   - *“You are only entitled to Weekly Disability Benefits if…”* – meaning it's optional citeturn0search1.
3. **Contact the FMCP Benefit Office** with your employer name or work status:
   - 📞 1‑877‑937‑9602 or ✉️ FMCP_Customer_Service@nifmcp.com  
   Ask:  
   > “Does my employer’s agreement include Weekly Disability Benefits under [Plan X]?”

---

## ✅ Summary

- STD coverage **is possible under any plan**, but each employer must *opt into it*.
- **If your SPD mentions “Weekly Disability Benefits”**, but adds the clause about employer election – that means it’s not guaranteed.
- **Check your SPD and/or ask the Benefit Office** to confirm whether your plan *actually includes* STD coverage.
