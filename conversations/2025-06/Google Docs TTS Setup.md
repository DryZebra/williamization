# Google Docs TTS Setup

## User

How can I get Google docs to read out loud to me like an audio book?

## Assistant



## Assistant

To get **Google Docs** to read text out loud like an audiobook, you can use built-in screen readers or text-to-speech (TTS) tools depending on your device. Here's how to do it:

---

### ✅ **On Android (easiest method)**
Use **Select to Speak**, built into Android:

1. **Go to Settings > Accessibility > Select to Speak**  
2. **Turn it on**  
3. Open your **Google Docs** document  
4. Tap the **Accessibility icon**, then drag to highlight the text  
5. It will start reading out loud

---

### ✅ **On Desktop (Chrome)**
Use **ChromeVox** or an extension like **Read Aloud**:

#### Method 1: **Read Aloud Chrome Extension**  
1. Install [**Read Aloud**](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/read-aloud-a-text-to-speec/hdhinadidafjejdhmfkjgnolgimiaplp)  
2. Open your Google Doc (must be in **view mode** or **preview**)  
3. Click the Read Aloud icon > It starts reading  
4. You can change the voice in the settings

> 📝 Tip: Use “File > Download > PDF” then open in Chrome if your document has complex formatting or images. It's easier for TTS to parse.

---

### ✅ **On iPhone/iPad**
Use **Speak Screen**:

1. Go to **Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Speak Screen** (turn it on)  
2. Open the Docs app or use Safari to open the doc  
3. Swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen  
4. It will begin reading aloud

---

### 🔁 Want it to feel like an audiobook?
To simulate an audiobook:
- Use a **natural-sounding voice** (like Google's WaveNet or a premium voice in Read Aloud)
- Adjust reading **speed** and **pitch** in the settings
- Put your document into **read-only** or exported **PDF** mode to avoid editing distractions
