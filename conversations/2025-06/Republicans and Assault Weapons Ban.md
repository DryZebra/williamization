- Passed **235–195**

### 🔫 **The Mulford Act (1967)**

- **Banned the public open carry of loaded firearms** in California.
- Signed into law by **Governor Ronald Reagan**, a Republican.
- Specifically targeted the **Black Panther Party**, which had begun **legally patrolling police** with **loaded rifles and shotguns** to monitor for brutality.

---

### 🏛️ Why It Was Passed

- In response to the **Black Panther Party’s armed demonstrations**, especially their famous **May 2, 1967 march into the California State Capitol**, where they legally carried loaded weapons.
- The image of armed Black men walking into a government building **terrified** California lawmakers — including both Republicans and Democrats.
- Reagan said, *“There’s no reason why on the street today a citizen should be carrying loaded weapons.”*

---

### 🧠 Key Irony

- Many Republicans who **opposed federal gun control** supported this state-level restriction.

---

### 🔍 Was it an Assault Weapons Ban?

- **Not exactly** — it didn’t focus on "assault weapons" as we define them today (like AR-15s).
- But it **set the precedent**: it was one of the first major U.S. laws restricting public gun carry — specifically **semi-automatic rifles and shotguns in public spaces**.
