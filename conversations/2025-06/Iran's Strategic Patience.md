1. **Internal legitimacy** – If they don’t respond, they look weak to their own people.

- Symbolic strikes (like missile attacks on bases after Soleimani)  
- Cyber attacks  
- Attacks through proxies (Houthis, Hezbollah, etc.)  
- Diplomatic outrage  
- Propaganda and memes to keep morale and narrative strong
