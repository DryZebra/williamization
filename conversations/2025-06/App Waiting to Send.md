# App Waiting to Send

## User

What does it mean when your app says "waiting to send?"

## Assistant



## Assistant

When your app says **"waiting to send,"** it usually means that the message or file you're trying to send is queued but hasn’t been transmitted yet. This can happen for a few reasons:

### 🕸️ Common Causes:
1. **No Internet Connection**  
   Your phone might be offline or in a low-signal area (Wi-Fi or mobile data issues).

2. **Poor Network Conditions**  
   You're connected, but the network is too slow or unstable to send data right away.

3. **Background Restrictions**  
   On Android, apps sometimes get background-restricted or battery-optimized, which can delay sending until the app is reopened or the phone is unlocked.

4. **App-Specific Queueing**  
   Some apps (like messaging or cloud sync apps) hold items in a queue until the prior tasks complete or until the app gets permission to send.

5. **Permissions or Sync Errors**  
   The app might lack permission to use background data or might be facing a sync failure with its server.

---

### ✅ What You Can Do:
- Toggle **Airplane Mode** off and on to reset the connection.
- Make sure **Wi-Fi or mobile data** is active and strong.
- Open the app to the foreground if it’s trying to run in the background.
- Go to **Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Battery** and disable optimizations or allow background usage.
- Reboot your phone if it’s stuck in that state.
