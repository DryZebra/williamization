# Metaphysical vs Materialist Christ

## User

I'm talking to a metaphysical Christian in my Church and he's pointing to John 8:39-40 as the evidence and proof of Jesus literal existence from the beginning of time. I'm trying to explain to him why this isn't sufficient proof, or alternatively, maybe I'm wrong and he's right in an ignorant way. Like how "bad air" is right in a wrong way.

## Assistant

Great framing—“right in a wrong way” like *miasma theory* is a sharp comparison. Let's start by looking at the actual verses:

> **John 8:39–40 (KJV)**  
> _They answered and said unto him, Abraham is our father. Jesus saith unto them, If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham. But now ye seek to kill me, a man that hath told you the truth, which I have heard of God: this did not Abraham._

From this, your metaphysical friend is likely drawing on Jesus’ *presence* in the conversation about Abraham as some kind of eternal, pre-existent being. But that reading requires importing metaphysics **into** the text—it’s not self-evident from the words.

Here’s the key: this verse doesn’t claim Jesus existed with Abraham—it claims *Abraham wouldn’t have killed someone telling him the truth*. It’s about *moral lineage*, not temporal lineage.

Now, Jesus does say something more radical later in the same chapter:

> **John 8:58**  
> _Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am._

This is where the metaphysical crowd hangs their hat, because “I am” is seen as a reference to **Exodus 3:14**—God’s name. That’s the *real* battleground verse. John 8:39–40 is more about behavior and morality.

### So is your friend “right in a wrong way”?

Yes—potentially. He's sniffing around **John 8:58** and using **8:39–40** as a foundation. But 8:39–40 doesn’t prove Jesus’ preexistence. It proves:

1. **Jesus sees the moral lineage of Abraham as behavioral**, not genetic.
2. **The claim to Abraham is invalid if you don’t act like him**, which is a major theme of Materialist Christianity: you are not what you say—you are what you do.
3. **Jesus is speaking as a man**, even identifying himself as one ("a man that hath told you the truth").

So the metaphysical reading is an extrapolation built on a bad foundation. Like saying “bad air” causes disease—not because of the air, but because they’re detecting a real pattern *without understanding the actual mechanism*.

### Dialectical takeaway:
- He sees a truth: there is a continuity between Jesus and Abraham that transcends time.
- But he resolves it metaphysically: Jesus *literally* existed before Abraham.
- Materialist Christianity resolves it historically: the **Spirit** of truth carried from Abraham to Jesus is **real, material, and visible in action**, not metaphysical existence.

You could ask: “Do you think someone who lived like Abraham but never heard of him would be his spiritual descendant?” If he says yes, he already believes in what *you* mean by eternal truth.
