# Meaning of Privily

## User

What does privily mean in the KJV Matthew 1?

## Assistant



## Assistant

In the King James Version (KJV), *Matthew 1:19* says:

> “Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away **privily**.”

The word **"privily"** is an archaic form of **"privately"** — it means *in secret* or *quietly, without public knowledge*. In this context, Joseph planned to break off the engagement to Mary **discreetly**, without publicly shaming or punishing her (which could have resulted in severe consequences under the law).

So Joseph was:
- **Just (righteous)** – meaning he cared about what was right before God,
- But also **merciful** – not wanting to publicly disgrace Mary, whom he assumed had committed adultery.

He sought a way to uphold moral law *and* protect her from harm, by ending the engagement *privily*.
