# Ticks with dots safety

## User

Are the ticks with the dots the ones that are bad?

## Assistant

Yes—**ticks with a white dot on their back** are often the ones people are most concerned about.

Here’s a quick breakdown:

### ✅ **Ticks with a white dot on their back:**
- **Lone Star Tick (Amblyomma americanum)**  
  - **Adult females** have a single white dot ("lone star") on their back.
  - **Known to transmit**:
    - **Ehrlichiosis**
    - **Tularemia**
    - **Southern tick-associated rash illness (STARI)**
    - And possibly linked to **Alpha-gal syndrome** (a red meat allergy)

---

### ❌ **But not all ticks with dots are “the bad ones”**
- The **dot itself doesn’t guarantee danger**, but it’s a helpful visual clue.
- **Other dangerous ticks**, like the **Blacklegged Tick (Deer Tick)** that spreads **Lyme disease**, **don’t** have dots—they're just small and dark.

---

### What to do if you see a tick:
- If it **has a white dot**, treat it seriously.
- Remove it **safely** with fine tweezers.
- **Save the tick** in a bag or container in case symptoms develop and you need to ID it.
- Watch for symptoms like **fever, rash, fatigue, or allergic reactions**.
