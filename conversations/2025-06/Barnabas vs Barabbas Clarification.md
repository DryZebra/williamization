# Barnabas vs Barabbas Clarification

## User

Is Barnabas from Acts 15 the same one that was freed for Jesus?

## Assistant



## Assistant

No, **Barnabas** from *Acts 15* is **not** the same person as **Barabbas**, the criminal who was released instead of Jesus.

Here’s the difference:

### 1. **Barnabas** (Acts 15)
- Real name: **Joseph**, but nicknamed *Barnabas*, meaning “son of encouragement” (Acts 4:36).
- He was a **Levite** from Cyprus.
- A key **early Christian**, he worked alongside **Paul** on missionary journeys.
- In Acts 15, Barnabas and Paul argue over whether to take John Mark with them on a second journey.
- **He was a follower of Jesus** and helped spread the Gospel.

### 2. **Barabbas** (Released instead of Jesus)
- A **criminal** mentioned in all four Gospels (e.g., Matthew 27:16–26).
- He was **imprisoned for insurrection and murder**.
- The crowd demanded his release instead of Jesus at Passover.
- His name, ironically, means **“son of the father”** (Bar-Abbas).

So:  
**Barnabas = Christian missionary, friend of Paul.**  
**Barabbas = Criminal released instead of Jesus.**  

Different men, despite the similar-sounding names.
