Here’s a way to say it simply to a Muslim while meeting them on common ground:

> "Materialist Christianity isn’t trying to replace your faith—it’s showing that Christianity was part of the same historical process Islam emerged from. It’s not about claiming Jesus was God, but about understanding how his life and message revealed moral truth through history. If you believe God works through real events, then maybe Christianity had a purpose too—even if it was only ever meant to prepare the way."

This reframes Christianity not as a threat to Islam’s finality, but as a necessary chapter in the same divine unfolding. It shifts the debate from “which religion is true” to “what moral motion did each produce.” If they ask why this matters, you can follow up with:

> "Because we live in the ruins of Christendom. Whether we like it or not, the world was shaped by Christian morality. If we want to understand where we are—and where to go next—we have to understand what Christianity really *did*, not just what it claimed."
