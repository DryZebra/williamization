### 🛁 **Materialist Christian Shower Playlist**  
*(“Songs to bathe in truth, not escape it.”)*

#### **Hymns that feel like ancient stone**
- *Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing* – Chris Rice or Sufjan Stevens versions  
- *Be Thou My Vision* – Audrey Assad  
- *O Sacred Head, Now Wounded* – Fernando Ortega  
- *I Need Thee Every Hour* – Page CXVI  
- *What Wondrous Love Is This* – Chelsea Moon  

#### **Spirituals & Folk that echo the fields**
- *Down in the River to Pray* – Alison Krauss  
- *Wayfaring Stranger* – Johnny Cash or Anonymous 4  
- *Ain’t No Grave* – Claude Ely (or Cash)  
- *We Sold Our Homesteads* – Falconer (you already love this one)  
- *Hard Times Come Again No More* – Mavis Staples or Bob Dylan  

#### **Working-class gospel**
- *Working Man’s Prayer* – William Elliott Whitmore  
- *Coal* – Tyler Childers  
- *Can the Circle Be Unbroken* – The Carter Family  
- *You’ll Never Leave Harlan Alive* – Darrell Scott or Patty Loveless  
- *Take Me to the River* – Al Green (for the beat *and* the baptism)  

#### **Modern hymns with backbone**
- *Dry Bones* – Gregory Alan Isakov  
- *If We Were Vampires* – Jason Isbell (about love vs death, not vampires)  
- *Multitudes* – Bob Dylan (2020, pure modern Job)  
- *You Were Good to Me* – Jeremy Zucker & Chelsea Cutler (secular, but raw)  
- *The Righteous Path* – Drive-By Truckers (self-examination in full)  

#### **Instrumental & Gregorian cleansing**
- *Miserere Mei, Deus* – Allegri (for deep repentance moments)  
- *Lux Aeterna* – Clint Mansell  
- *Psalm 51 – Have Mercy Upon Me* – The Choir of King’s College, Cambridge  
- *Benedictus* – Karl Jenkins  

---

This list is *not* for escapism. It’s for entering the steam, and letting it wash over the real you—the sinner, the servant, the laborer, the loved.
