## Assistant

The IBEW Constitution covers special elections in **Article XVI, Section 10**. Here’s what it says:

If any elected Local Union officer position becomes vacant **for more than six months before the regular election**, the Executive Board **must hold a special election** to fill it. That special election has to follow the regular rules for nominations and elections, unless the International President grants permission for a different process. The elected person will serve out the remainder of the unexpired term.

So, if an officer resigns or is removed early, and there's still over six months left in their term, a special election is required .

## Assistant

Local union officers can be removed early through the **IBEW's trial procedures**. Here's how it works:

### 1. **Filing Charges**
- Any IBEW member can file formal charges against a local officer.
- Charges must be filed **within 60 days** of the alleged offense.
- Charges must be in writing, specific, and filed with the local union recording secretary or president【16:2†2022-IBEW-Constitution_copy.pdf】.

### 2. **Trial Board**
- The **Local Union Executive Board** acts as the trial board unless the International President decides otherwise.
- The accused has the right to a fair trial, including being present, confronting witnesses, and presenting a defense【16:3†2022-IBEW-Constitution_copy.pdf】.

### 3. **Penalties**
- If found guilty, penalties can include:
  - **Removal from office**
  - **Suspension of membership**
  - **Expulsion from the IBEW**
  - **Fines**

### 4. **Automatic Removal**
- Officers can also be automatically removed if they:
  - **Fail to attend meetings** as required by local bylaws.
  - **Are convicted of violating the IBEW Constitution or duties of their office.**

### 5. **Appeal**
- Members found guilty can appeal the decision to the International President, and from there to the International Executive Council.
