# Target Time Ideas

## User

My girlfriend is at target for 3 hours waiting for our dog's haircut. What can she do for us? How can she wisely spend her time?

## Assistant



## Assistant

Three hours at Target is basically an epic quest! Here are some fun, productive, and even relationship-building ways your girlfriend can use that time—either solo or to do something sweet for you and the pup:

### Productive Ideas
- **Meal Prep Scouting:** Check out new snacks or healthy groceries for the week, maybe even send you pics for approval.
- **Household Restock:** Walk the aisles for things you might be low on—think cleaning supplies, TP, pet stuff, first aid, batteries, etc.
- **Gift Recon:** Scope out ideas for birthdays, anniversaries, or just-because surprises for you or friends/family.
- **Book/Journal Browsing:** Target usually has a book section—maybe pick up a new read or a journal for reflections, couple’s notes, or doggy milestones.

### Fun or Relaxing Ideas
- **Coffee or Treat:** Grab a Starbucks or snack and just people-watch, listen to music, or read.
- **Mini Spa Trip:** Get some face masks, lotions, or bath bombs for a relaxing night later.
- **Try-On Fashion Show:** Try on some clothes and send you silly or serious pics to vote on.
- **Game Section:** Explore new board games, card games, or puzzles you could play together.

### For the Dog
- **Toy/Treat Shopping:** Find a new chew, toy, or treat for your pup’s post-haircut reward.
- **Pet Section Recon:** Compare brands/prices for food, bedding, accessories.

### For You as a Couple
- **Surprise Find:** Pick something small that’s “just us”—a silly mug, matching socks, or inside joke item.
- **Scavenger Hunt:** Text you pics of weirdest/strangest/cutest thing she finds. Make it a game!
- **Plan a Date:** Use the Target time to plan out a next date night, picking up supplies or inspiration as she goes.

### If She Gets Bored
- **Get Steps In:** Walk laps, track steps, get some movement in.
- **Playlist Making:** Curate a playlist for you both, or for the dog!
- **Photo Album:** Take funny selfies or photos of the most “Target” moments for a mini scrapbook later.

Anything on that list sound like it would make your day (or your pup’s)? Want something more romantic, silly, or practical?
