# Avocado Introduction to America

## User

Who's the guy who brought avocado to America?

## Assistant



## Assistant

There isn’t a single person definitively credited with "bringing avocado to America," because avocados are **native to the Americas**—specifically **southern Mexico and Central America**—and were cultivated by Indigenous peoples thousands of years before European contact.

However, if you're asking **who introduced avocados to the U.S. commercial market**, that would likely be:

### **Henry Perrine**
- A U.S. horticulturist and diplomat in the 1830s.
- He promoted tropical plants like avocados in Florida.
- He’s one of the first people credited with **introducing avocado trees to Florida** for agricultural use.

Another key figure:

### **Rudolph Hass**
- He **patented the Hass avocado in 1935**, which is now the dominant avocado variety globally.
- He bought a seedling (likely a Guatemalan-Mexican hybrid) and grafted it to produce a unique fruit.
- The **Hass avocado revolutionized avocado farming**, especially in California.

So:  
- **Avocados are indigenous to the Americas**—they weren't "brought" here.
- **Henry Perrine** helped spread them in the U.S.
- **Rudolph Hass** gave us the most popular modern variety.
