# Deadly Nightshade Identification

## User

What's that plant that looks like tomato leaves but will kill you?

## Assistant



## Assistant

You're probably thinking of **deadly nightshade**, also known as **belladonna** (*Atropa belladonna*). It belongs to the **Solanaceae** family—**the same family as tomatoes**—which is why its leaves look similar.

Here are key features:

- **Leaves:** Oval, pointed, and slightly fuzzy—very similar to tomato leaves.
- **Berries:** Shiny, dark purple or black berries that look tempting but are **extremely toxic**.
- **Flowers:** Bell-shaped, purplish-brown flowers.

**Toxicity:** All parts of the plant—especially the berries and roots—contain **tropane alkaloids** like **atropine, scopolamine, and hyoscyamine**, which can cause **hallucinations, delirium, paralysis, and death** even in small doses.

Other similar-looking toxic plants in the nightshade family include:

- **Black nightshade** (*Solanum nigrum*) – less toxic, still dangerous.
- **Horse nettle** (*Solanum carolinense*) – common in the southeastern U.S.
- **Jimsonweed** (*Datura stramonium*) – different leaves, but also toxic and in the same family.
