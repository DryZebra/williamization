## 🕯️ **STEP 1: Learn the Building Blocks**

### 📜 **Key Vocabulary Differences**
| Modern | KJV Equivalent |
|--------|----------------|
| you     | **thou / thee / ye** |
| your    | **thy / thine** |
| are     | **art** |
| have    | **hast** |
| will    | **wilt / shalt** |
| do      | **dost / doeth** |
| does    | **doth** |
| did     | **didst** |
| was     | **wast** |
| were    | **wert** |
| is      | **is / be / hath** |

> 🔹 **"Thou"** = singular subject ("Thou art a light.")  
> 🔹 **"Thee"** = singular object ("I give this unto thee.")  
> 🔹 **"Ye"** = plural subject ("Ye are the salt of the earth.")  
> 🔹 **"Thy/Thine"** = possessive ("Thy kingdom come"; "Thine is the glory.")

---

## 🏰 **STEP 2: Sentence Structure Practice**

KJV English often **inverts** modern order:

- Modern: *You have heard the truth.*  
- KJV: *Ye have heard the truth.*

- Modern: *God will bless you.*  
- KJV: *The Lord shall bless thee.*

> 🔸 **“Shall”** and **“hath”** carry more gravity than “will” or “has.”

Try to speak with:
- **Formality** (no contractions like "don’t" or "can’t")
- **Poetic cadence** (let your phrases breathe)

## 📚 What *Kind* of English is KJV?
KJV English is not quite “Old English” (like *Beowulf*), nor truly “Middle English” (like Chaucer), but rather:

### ➤ **Early Modern English**
- Spoken from ~1500–1700
- Same period as **Shakespeare**
- Fully intelligible to modern readers, just more formal and poetic

So yes, it’s technically *Early Modern English*, not “Old English.” The vocabulary is mostly familiar, but the grammar and style are elevated and sacred.

## 🏛️ What Is Anglish?
**Anglish** is English with all Latin/French/Greek words removed—replaced by Germanic roots.

- *“Language”* → **tongue**
- *“Forgive”* → **forbear** or **wipe away**
- *“Glory”* → **bright fame**
- *“Spirit”* → **ghost**
- *“Church”* → **Godhouse**

---

## 🕯️ What Is KJV English?
KJV English keeps Latin-root words, but adds:
- **Thou/thee/ye**
- **-eth** and **-est** endings
- Biblical cadence
- Poetic formality

---
