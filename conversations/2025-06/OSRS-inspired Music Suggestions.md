#### 🎼 **Murgrind** – *Journey Through the Mountain*  
- A little more epic, but still very rooted in medieval ambient.

#### 👻 **Til Det Bergens Skyggene** – by Old Tower
- Grim and moody, like Morytania’s swamps or the haunted houses of OSRS.

- Pure Tolkien-esque dungeon synth. Darker, with that fantasy “walking simulator” feel.

#### 🐉 **Jeremy Soule** – *The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion OST*
- Has a similar emotional warmth and loopable quality.
- Less quirky than OSRS, but good if you want to scale up from “MIDI goblin town” to “orchestral goblin town.”

#### 🧚 **Lena Raine** – *Celeste* and *Minecraft Caves & Cliffs*
- She captures whimsy and solitude with minimalism and melody.
- The RuneScape part of you that finds joy in cutting willows in Lumbridge will resonate with this.
