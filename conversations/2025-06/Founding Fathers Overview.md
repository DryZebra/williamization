# Founding Fathers Overview

## User

Who are the founding fathers?

## Assistant



## Assistant

The "Founding Fathers" of the United States refers to the group of leaders who played central roles in the American Revolution, the founding of the nation, and the drafting of key documents like the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution.

### Most commonly recognized Founding Fathers include:

#### **1. George Washington**
- Commander-in-Chief of the Continental Army
- President of the Constitutional Convention
- First President of the United States

#### **2. Thomas Jefferson**
- Primary author of the **Declaration of Independence**
- Third U.S. President
- Advocate for individual liberty and states' rights

#### **3. John Adams**
- Advocate for independence in Congress
- Helped draft the Declaration of Independence
- Second U.S. President

#### **4. Benjamin Franklin**
- Diplomat, inventor, and intellectual
- Helped secure French support during the Revolution
- Attended the Constitutional Convention

#### **5. James Madison**
- “Father of the Constitution”
- Key author of the Federalist Papers
- Fourth U.S. President

#### **6. Alexander Hamilton**
- First Secretary of the Treasury
- Chief author of the Federalist Papers
- Advocate for a strong central government

#### **7. John Jay**
- First Chief Justice of the Supreme Court
- Co-author of the Federalist Papers
- Negotiated the Treaty of Paris (1783)

---

Other significant contributors include **Samuel Adams**, **Patrick Henry**, **George Mason**, **Thomas Paine**, and **Gouverneur Morris**, among others. While not all held national office, they each played essential roles in independence, revolution, or constitution-building.
