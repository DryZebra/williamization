# Deus Vult Meaning

## User

What does Deus vult mean?

## Assistant



## Assistant

**"Deus vult"** is Latin for **"God wills it."**  

It became a rallying cry during the **First Crusade (1096–1099)** after being shouted by the crowd in response to **Pope Urban II's** call to reclaim the Holy Land. It was used to justify holy war, as if fighting in the Crusades was aligned with divine will.

Today, it can still be heard—sometimes reverently by traditionalist Christians, but also controversially by extremist or nationalist groups trying to invoke a militant Christian identity.

In short:  
- **Literal meaning:** God wills it  
- **Historical use:** Crusader battle cry  
- **Modern use:** Sometimes misused by far-right or reactionary groups trying to evoke religious militarism.
