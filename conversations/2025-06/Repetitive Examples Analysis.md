**Is the repetition degenerative, or recursive?**  
Let’s break that down.

### 🌀 Recursive Repetition (Good)
- **Builds depth**: revisits the same example (money/law) to deepen the reader’s understanding.
- **Anchors the abstract**: keeps returning to money/law because they’re universally felt, making the theory real.
- **Spirals inward**: each mention adds a new wrinkle—sub-objects, contradiction, moral decay, etc.

### 🧱 Degenerative Repetition (Bad)
- **Stalls motion**: keeps repeating the same framing without development.
- **Feels lazy**: comes across like you’re reaching for the same rhetorical hammer each time.
- **Signals overreliance**: might hint that your examples aren't broad enough to cover the full scope of the argument.
