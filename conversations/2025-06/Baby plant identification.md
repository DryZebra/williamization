This appears to be a young **Horseweed** plant (*Conyza canadensis*), also known as Canadian fleabane. It's a common annual weed in North America, especially in disturbed areas like roadsides, construction zones, or fields.

### Key Features Visible:
- Tall, upright growth
- Narrow, lance-shaped leaves that alternate along the stem
- Fine, soft hairs on the stem and leaves
- Dense, vertical appearance (early growth form)

As it matures, Horseweed can grow several feet tall and produce small white or pale flowers at the top. It's sometimes mistaken for young pine or dill due to the narrow leaves, but those plants have very different growth patterns over time.
