### 📏 Ideal Length for an Introduction

An introduction is usually **2–5 pages** (or **800–2,000 words**). For your purposes, aim closer to **3–4 pages**. It should:

- Give **readers a reason to care** about the book.
- Explain **what the book is**, without summarizing it.
- Briefly describe **your background and the journey** that led to writing it.
- Gently **prepare readers** for the tone and content.
- Clarify what **Materialist Christianity** is in emotional and human terms (the *why*, not the *how*).

This is not a place for dense theory. This is where you let them in, just enough, to walk the path with you. A soft handshake, not a firehose.

---

### 🔧 Structure Suggestion
