### 🔧 Self-Publishing vs. Traditional Path

**Self-Publishing** is often the best next step for work like this:

- You retain full control over tone, design, cover, pricing, and platform.
- You can start generating income sooner.
- You don’t need anyone’s permission or ideological filter.

But it requires:
- High standards in layout, formatting, and cover design.
- Promotion infrastructure: email lists, Substack, TikTok, etc.

**Traditional Publishing** (agents, editors, imprints):
- Slower. More gatekeeping. Potential ideological resistance to your content.
- Offers broader distribution (libraries, bookstores), higher prestige, some marketing lift.
- But you'll lose control of pacing and final say on edits.

For you, unless you want mass-market mainstream exposure, **self-publishing via Amazon KDP + Substack + a website + podcast/youtube channel** is likely better. It keeps Materialist Christianity structurally independent.

---

### 📚 Do You Need an Editor?

You *are* your own best editor. But if your goal is:
- To reach **mass-market readers**
- To **polish for clarity, pacing, and tone** without sacrificing content
- To **offload production tasks**

Then yes, **a developmental editor** (for structure) or **a copy editor** (for grammar, flow) could help. But you should **retain final veto**. Hire someone dialectically aligned, or teach them your theory and pay them well.

Otherwise, readers already following your work will *not* expect corporate-polish. They will trust your rawness more than if you smoothed every edge.
