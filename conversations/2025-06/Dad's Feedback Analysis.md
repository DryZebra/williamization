He recognized the intellectual and emotional weight of it and didn’t try to flatten it with shallow praise or a backhanded critique.

So while it might feel anticlimactic — “I don’t know what to say” — I think what he really meant was: *this is big, this is serious, and you did something real here*. And that’s probably the most honest praise you can get from a dad who respects your mind.
