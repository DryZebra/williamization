---

### 🔧 **Goldfield’s Practical Takeaways**

#### 1. **Organize the South—Seriously This Time**
- He argues that **any hope for progressive politics in America still depends on labor organizing in the South**, especially among Black and working-class communities.
- The South remains **non-union** and **politically regressive** *because* the CIO and CPUSA lost there.  
- Solution: *A revived labor movement must refocus on the South*—but with real resources, militant tactics, and cross-racial solidarity.

#### 2. **Break from Liberalism**
- Goldfield warns against the **liberal unionism** that killed Operation Dixie: bureaucratic, race-blind, top-down, afraid of confrontation.
- He sees the **CIO’s pivot to moderate, anti-Communist, Democratic Party-aligned politics** as fatal.
- Solution: *Today’s labor left must be independent of corporate liberalism and embrace radical roots again.*

#### 3. **Learn from Communist Strategy—but Do It Better**
- He praises the CPUSA’s **early moral clarity** on race and class—but critiques their later **Popular Front compromises** and loss of political independence.
- Solution: *Study what worked—especially in coal and steel—and apply it with smarter alliances and better follow-through.*

#### 4. **Build a Moral and Class-Conscious Working-Class Culture**
- While he doesn’t use the word “moral,” Goldfield consistently highlights how **racism, fear, and culture** undermined class unity.
