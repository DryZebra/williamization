Got it. You want **something engaging**, but not **addictive** or shallow. Here's a list of meaningful, non-dopamine-drip apps to pass time on your phone—ideally things that feel productive, calming, or mentally engaging without being designed to hook you:

---

### 🧠 **Intellectual Engagement**
1. **Readwise Reader** – Turn your phone into a high-efficiency reading hub. Load it with long articles, PDFs, or highlights from books. It’s like a second brain for reading.

2. **Deepstash** – Curated ideas from books and articles, broken into digestible insights. You can read or explore by topic without falling into a rabbit hole.

3. **AnkiDroid** – Build or download flashcards (like for Russian, Scripture, electrical code) and review them with spaced repetition. Feels like leveling up.

4. **Brilliant** – Learn math, physics, logic, or computer science via interactive lessons. Focused, no fluff.

---

### 🛠️ **Skill Building**
1. **Tayasui Sketches / Infinite Painter** – If you ever wanted to draw, these are great for peaceful sketching or studying form.

2. **Perfect Ear / Functional Ear Trainer** – Build musical pitch recognition or learn to read music. Super focused.

3. **Simply Piano / Yousician (free version only)** – If you’ve got an instrument nearby, these can help develop real musical skills without going full gamer dopamine mode.

---

### 📚 **Story & Exploration Without Addictiveness**
1. **Libby / Audible / Moon+ Reader** – Load it up with books you’ve been meaning to read or re-read. Offline access, no social element.

2. **Reigns: Her Majesty / Cultist Simulator** – Thought-provoking card/story-based games. Single purchase, no gacha.

3. **Tormentum: Dark Sorrow / Rusty Lake** – Point-and-click puzzles with deep or eerie storytelling.

---

### 🧘 **Mindful or Meditative**
1. **Oak / Balance / Insight Timer** – Great for breathwork, guided meditation, or stoic-style quiet.

2. **Atom / Notion / Obsidian Mobile** – Turn your phone into a philosophy, theology, or personal journaling station.

3. **Slow Roads** – A gentle, endless driving simulator with no goals. You just *drive*. It’s gorgeous, peaceful, no ads.

---

### 🧩 **Logical and Offline Games**
1. **Mini Metro / Mini Motorways** – Minimalist traffic simulators that exercise planning and efficiency, not dopamine.

2. **Chess.com or Lichess (no rating games)** – Treat it as study or exploration, not competition.

3. **Sokoban / Puzzle Retreat / The Room** – Thoughtful puzzles, often offline. No score-chasing.
