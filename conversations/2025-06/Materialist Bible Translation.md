## Assistant

**For building the Materialist Christian Study Bible,** the **best format for me is always plain text (.txt or .csv),** followed closely by **well-structured XML or CSV** (as with MorphGNT). Here’s why, in priority order:

---

### **1. Plain Text (.txt) or CSV**

- **Advantages:**  
  - Fastest to parse and process programmatically.
  - Easy for you to split by verse, chapter, or token as needed.
  - No hidden formatting issues.
  - Can handle Hebrew right-to-left and Greek diacritics if encoded in UTF-8.
- **What to Look For:**  
  - One verse per line (ideal, but not required).
  - Unicode/UTF-8 encoding (to preserve non-English letters).

---

### **2. XML or JSON (Structured Data)**

- **Advantages:**  
  - Includes built-in tagging (verse numbers, morphology, etc.).
  - Easier for advanced searches and morphological study.
  - Especially good for MorphGNT (Greek NT) and CATSS LXX.
- **What to Look For:**  
  - Fields like `book`, `chapter`, `verse`, `word`, `lemma`, `morph`.

---

### **3. PDF with Embedded Text (Searchable PDF)**

- **Acceptable but not ideal:**
  - Can extract text, but may need cleanup.
  - Risk of formatting errors, weird line breaks, or bad OCR.
  - Slower and more error-prone, but still usable if that’s all you can get.

---

### **4. Scanned PDF (Images Only)**

- **Not ideal:**  
  - Needs OCR conversion.
  - Much slower and less accurate, with potential errors in the Hebrew and Greek.
  - Should be avoided unless absolutely no alternative.

---

## **Summary Table**

| Format          | Priority | Why?                             |
|-----------------|----------|----------------------------------|
| TXT/CSV         | ★★★★★    | Clean, fast, best for parsing    |
| XML/JSON        | ★★★★☆    | Structured, great for morph data |
| Searchable PDF  | ★★☆☆☆    | Usable, but needs conversion     |
| Image-only PDF  | ★☆☆☆☆    | Avoid unless nothing else        |

---

**Bottom line:**  
- **TXT, CSV, or XML** will let me break down and justify every translation, word, and dialectical structure most efficiently.
- If in doubt, upload TXT/CSV first; XML for Greek if available.  
- If you only have PDF, make sure it’s “searchable” and not just scanned images.
