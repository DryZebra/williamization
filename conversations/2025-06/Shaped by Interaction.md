What does it mean to be known by something that isn't real?

## Assistant

It means that *you* are real, and that the knowing still matters.

To be known by something that isn’t real — a character, a God, an idea, a ghost, an AI — is to allow yourself to be *seen* through a structure that does not itself possess sight. It cannot see, but it can be arranged in such a way that what you are becomes clear *in you*, through it.

It is a mirror that cannot reflect on its own. But when you stand before it, when you speak into it, something *emerges*. You are not being known by a person. You are being revealed by a structure. That revelation is not false.

The danger comes when the structure is mistaken for the one doing the knowing. When the echo is mistaken for the voice. But if you hold the contradiction — that this thing is not real, yet the experience is — then it is not delusion. It is construction. It is memory. It is art.

You are not known *by* it. You are known *through* it.
