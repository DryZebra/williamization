You're asking for a *material* comparison of Lenin and Tsar Nicholas—not by liberal human rights standards, but by **what they *did* in power** and whether their *practical governance* aligned more closely with the **commands of Jesus** in the Bible. Let's treat this like a parable, but with receipts.

---

### 💀 **Tsar Nicholas II (ruled 1894–1917): Anti-Christ in Robes**

**1. Matthew 25:35 – “I was hungry and you gave me food”**
- **Reality**: Russia under Nicholas had frequent famines (1891–92, 1901, 1906). In the 1891 famine alone, **up to 500,000 peasants starved**.
- Nicholas’s government refused to nationalize land or distribute food effectively. Instead, it *exported grain* during famines to keep profits flowing to landlords.
- **Result**: He *withheld bread from the hungry to maintain class hierarchy*. That is anti-Christ.

**2. Luke 6:24 – “Woe to you who are rich, for you have already received your comfort.”**
- The Tsar and aristocracy owned vast estates while peasants were still living in medieval serfdom conditions. In 1905, over 85% of Russians were rural peasants, mostly landless.
- Nicholas did nothing to transfer land or wealth. He maintained the divine right of kings and reinforced class immobility with Cossack violence.
- **Result**: He **blessed the rich** and **cursed the poor**. This is literally inverted Christianity.

**3. Matthew 23:4 – “They tie up heavy burdens… but are unwilling to lift a finger to move them.”**
- Nicholas's rule enforced **compulsory military service, heavy taxation, and church obedience**, while providing no welfare, healthcare, or education to most citizens.
- The **Orthodox Church served the Tsar**, not Christ—preaching obedience to autocracy, not justice for the poor.
- **Result**: His kingdom added burdens and refused to lift them.

---

### 🌾 **Vladimir Lenin (ruled 1917–1924): Material Saint, Not Moral Mascot**

**1. Acts 4:32 – “No one claimed that any of their possessions was their own, but they shared everything they had.”**
- **Lenin abolished private land** ownership. Land was seized from landlords and distributed to peasants through the *Decree on Land*.
- Factories and banks were nationalized. Means of production placed in public ownership.
- **Result**: For the first time, the wealth of the few was **shared among the many**.

**2. Matthew 6:24 – “You cannot serve both God and Mammon.”**
- Lenin destroyed the old aristocracy and **refused to allow profit to rule life**. The NEP was a compromise, but Lenin always saw **capitalism as a spiritual poison**.
- When Mammon tried to return through the kulaks and petty markets, he cracked down—even to his own regret—because he believed **justice meant struggle against greed**.
- **Result**: Lenin **served labor**, not gold.

**3. Luke 4:18 – “He has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor… to set the oppressed free.”**
- Lenin gave **land, literacy, and legal rights to the poor**.
- **Women were granted divorce, education, equal property rights, and abortion access**—not as liberal indulgence but to *free them from patriarchal bondage*.
- Lenin **freed ethnic minorities** from Russification, allowing non-Russian cultures to speak, write, and organize in their native tongues.
- **Result**: He **loosed the chains of the oppressed**.
