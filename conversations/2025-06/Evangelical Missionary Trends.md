## 🌍 Global Mission Footprint by Evangelical/Christian Denomination

### 1. **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter‑day Saints**  
- Though not typically classed as evangelical, they deploy around **82,000 full‑time proselytizing missionaries**, plus ~10,000 humanitarian/service missionaries citeturn0search1.  
- Their system is highly organized and volunteer‑oriented—few other churches come close in raw numbers per capita.

---

### 2. **South Korean Evangelicals**  
- South Korea is second only to the U.S. in missionaries sent, with approximately **13,000 South Korean missionaries**, representing one of the highest **per-capita** sending rates citeturn0search22turn0search5.  
- Korean churches, especially Presbyterian and Pentecostal, strongly emphasize missions globally citeturn0search22.

---

### 3. **Latin American Evangelicals**  
- Over recent decades, Latin American evangelicals have become a major missionary source. By the 2000s, they comprised **nearly half of all missionaries worldwide**, marking a significant shift from historic Euro-American dominance citeturn0news20.

---

### 4. **Highly‑sending African Denominations**  
- **Evangelical Church Winning All (ECWA)** in Nigeria fields around **1,600 missionaries** and operates a large mission society citeturn0search21turn0search22.  
- This represents substantial sending effort from African evangelical bodies.

---

### 5. **Mainline & Wesleyan‑Tradition Churches**  
- **Presbyterian denominations** in the U.S.:  
  - PCA sends ~18 foreign missionaries per 10,000 members.  
  - EPC sends ~10 per 10,000 citeturn0search16.  
  - PCUSA lags behind significantly in proportional terms.  
- Organizations like **Church of the Nazarene** send around **700 salaried missionaries**, plus thousands of short‑term volunteers citeturn0search26.  
- **TMS Global**, originally United Methodist, supports ~225 missionaries in 30+ countries citeturn0search24.

## 📊 Broad Global Missionary Trends

- Total foreign missionaries worldwide: ~450,000–430,000 citeturn0search2turn0search3turn0search4.  
- About **7.6%** of full-time Christian workers serve abroad; if evangelicals mirror that, that's ~59,000 evangelicals overseas citeturn0search2turn0search4.  
- Proportionally, **only ~0.01%** of all Christians are foreign missionaries citeturn0search8turn0search2.

## 🔎 Conclusion

- **Absolute numbers**: U.S. evangelicals lead, with South Korea, Latin America, and Nigeria following.  
- **Per-capita intensity**: LDS far outpace evangelicals; among evangelicals, **South Korean** followed by **Latin American** and **African** churches show the strongest missionary engagement.  
- Among **U.S. mainline denominations**, **PCA and EPC** have significantly higher missionary-to-member ratios than PCUSA.
