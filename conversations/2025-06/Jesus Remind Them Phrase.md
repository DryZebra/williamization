# Jesus Remind Them Phrase

## User

Where does Jesus say "remind them of this"

## Assistant



## Assistant

Jesus does not use the exact phrase **"remind them of this"** anywhere in the Gospels.

However, similar ideas of *remembering* or *reminding* are present in related phrases, often used by **apostles** (especially Paul and Peter) rather than Jesus himself. Examples:

---

### 🔹 Closest Ideas by **Jesus**:
- **John 14:26 (KJV)**  
  > *But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, **and bring all things to your remembrance**, whatsoever I have said unto you.*

  🔍 Here, Jesus promises the Spirit will remind the disciples of His teachings.

---

### 🔹 From the **Apostles** (Explicit "remind them"):
- **2 Timothy 2:14 (KJV)** – Paul to Timothy  
  > *Of these things **put them in remembrance**, charging them before the Lord...*

- **Titus 3:1 (KJV)** – Paul  
  > *Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers...*

- **2 Peter 1:12–13 (KJV)** – Peter  
  > *...I will not be negligent to put you always in remembrance of these things...*

---

So while **Jesus never says the phrase directly**, the apostles took it upon themselves to **remind the church** of His words, and Jesus **promised** the Holy Spirit would do that spiritually.

If you're looking for a version of Jesus saying something like:  
> *“Tell them again. Don’t let them forget.”  

—the tone is **implied** in His commissions and teachings, but not quoted in that wording.

Ah — now we're getting very close to the *real passage you're remembering*.

You're likely thinking of **2 Timothy 2:14 (KJV)**:

> **"Of these things put them in remembrance, charging them before the Lord that they strive not about words to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers."**

This is **Paul** writing to **Timothy**, and the key phrase is:  
> **“put them in remembrance”** — not **teach from scratch**, but **remind**.

---

### 🔹 What’s the deeper meaning?
Paul isn’t telling Timothy to go “preach new things.” He’s saying:
> "They already know. Remind them. Re-ground them. Don’t argue about words — anchor them in what they already know is true."

---

This aligns closely with **Romans 15:15 (KJV)** as well:
> *"Nevertheless, brethren, I have written the more boldly unto you in some sort, as putting you in mind..."*

---

### 🔹 Materialist Christian Perspective?
This fits beautifully:  
It implies that **truth is not hidden**, but that people need to be **reminded**, re-awakened, reoriented.  
You’re not converting — you’re recalling.

Like Jesus said in **Luke 17:21**:  
> *"The kingdom of God is within you."*

Reminding, not inventing.
