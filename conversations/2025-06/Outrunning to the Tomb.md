# Outrunning to the Tomb

## User

What bible verse is it that someone out runs the other to Jesus tomb

## Assistant



## Assistant

The verse you're thinking of is **John 20:4**. It describes the moment when **Peter** and **"the disciple whom Jesus loved"** (traditionally understood to be **John**) run to the tomb after hearing from Mary Magdalene that Jesus' body is missing:

> **John 20:4 (KJV)**  
> "So they ran both together: and the other disciple did outrun Peter, and came first to the sepulchre."

This moment is often pointed to as a subtle, almost humorous flex by the author—John making sure to note that he was *faster* than Peter.

## Assistant

Ah, that clears things up — it sounds like you’re looking at **1 John and 2 John**, which are letters (epistles) near the end of the New Testament. What you need is the **Gospel of John**, which is a completely different book found earlier in the New Testament, right after **Luke** and before **Acts**.

Here's how to find it:

- Go to the New Testament.
- Look for: **Matthew → Mark → Luke → John** (this is the **Gospel of John**).
- Then flip to **chapter 20, verse 4**.

That’s where the line about outrunning Peter to the tomb is. The letters of John (1 John, 2 John, 3 John) come much later and are not narrative—they’re short, pastoral letters.
