## 📱 Android

### **AndBible (Bible Study)**  
- Completely open-source and offline with no ads or accounts.  
- Offers split views, Strong’s integration (Hebrew/Greek), commentaries, workspaces, advanced search, bookmarks, and notes citeturn0search6turn0search15.

### **Quick Bible**  
- Lightweight, ad‑free, no account, offline capable.  
- Features advanced search, split-screen for comparing translations, cross references, notes, bookmarks citeturn0search0turn0search16.

---

## 💻 Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux)

### **Xiphos**  
- GUI Bible reader front‑end for the open-source SWORD library.  
- Works offline, supports modules in dozens of languages, and has strong search and multi-translation comparison citeturn0search2turn0search24.

### **BibleDesktop / BibleTime / xulsword**  
- SWORD-based cross-platform tools with GUIs on Windows/macOS/Linux.  
- Provide search, multiple translations, commentaries, and advanced features citeturn0search24.

### **Eloquent (Mac)**  
- Open-source macOS app (formerly MacSword).  
- Supports translations, commentaries, dictionaries, offline search, note-taking citeturn0search24.

### **OpenBible (GitHub)**  
- Cross-platform (Android, iOS, PC) MIT-licensed .NET MAUI app.  
- Still in development but functional for reading public-domain Bibles offline citeturn0search13.

### ✅ What to Choose?

- **Android users**: Start with **AndBible** or **Quick Bible**—no sign‑ups, tons of tools, fully offline.
- **Desktop users (especially on macOS/Linux/Windows)**: Try **Xiphos** or **BibleDesktop/BibleTime**—rich study features via SWORD modules.
- **macOS-only**: **Eloquent** gives a native experience.
- **If you like bleeding‑edge cross‑platform**: **OpenBible** is promising, though still evolving.
