### 1. **Federal Law – Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)**  
The FLSA requires that all overtime **must be paid at 1.5× the regular rate of pay** after 40 hours in a week.  
> **29 CFR §778.108** – *“The ‘regular rate’ of pay includes all remuneration for employment paid to, or on behalf of, the employee…”*  
That means **if your wage includes bonuses, differentials, or other regular earnings (like over-scale or hostage pay), it must be included in the OT calculation**.

🔗 [Link to official regulation](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-778/section-778.108)

---

### 2. **CBA + IBEW Recovery Agreement Violation**  
The CBA and Recovery Agreement both state:
> “The overtime rate shall be paid at time and one-half (1 ½) the regular straight-time rate.”【12†current recovery 2025.pdf】
