### Why it’s so sparse

1. **Off-shoring wiped out 98 % + of U.S. footwear jobs** in the 1980-2000 window. Most factories that survived (e.g., Thorogood, Red Wing, New Balance’s five New England plants) either focus on boots or premium lines. citeturn1search2  
2. **Union density in footwear collapsed with the factories.** Thorogood’s UFCW Local 688 is literally the last large union shoe shop left; NB, SAS, Okabashi, etc. are all non-union family firms.  
3. **Mass retailers chase price first.** A $25 Chinese sneaker lands on the shelf for \$50; a $150 Wisconsin sneaker would retail near \$300. Chains simply won’t allocate space for it.

---

- **Okabashi/Oka-B** flips or slides for around \$20 will at least keep summer feet domestic.
