In North Carolina (including Orange County), your employer **can require** you to wear a uniform, and you're generally **allowed to pay** for it—but only under certain conditions:

- Under federal law (FLSA), if you’re paid minimum wage, your employer **cannot** make you buy your own uniform or deduct its cost from your pay. If you earn more than minimum wage, they *can* deduct uniform costs—but **only** as long as your after-deduction earnings don’t dip below minimum wage or overtime entitlements citeturn0search4turn0search5turn0search13.
- Under North Carolina state law (N.C.G.S. § 95‑25.8), your employer must get your **written authorization** before deducting a known uniform cost—and the deduction must be agreed to *before* the relevant pay period citeturn0search0turn0search9turn0search17.

**To summarize**:
1. **If you make minimum wage or less**, they **cannot** make you pay for uniforms or deduct their cost.
2. **If you earn above minimum wage**, they *may* deduct the cost—but:
   - You must give **written consent** before the deduction.
   - The deducted amount must not reduce your pay below minimum wage or applicable overtime.
   - You must be told **in advance** of the deduction amount and have a chance to withdraw consent if not already signed.
3. They aren’t required to **free-issue** uniforms, unless you’re paid minimum wage (because then they can’t shift the cost onto you).

### ✅ What you can do now:
- **Check your paycheck**: Did your employer deduct uniform costs? And do you earn minimum wage?
- **Check your contract or handbook**: Was there a **signed agreement** stating the uniform cost and authorization?
- If you **aren’t minimum-wage**, and there *is* a clear, signed authorization and your pay stays above minimum wage post-deduction, this arrangement is **legal**.
- If you earn **minimum wage**, any requirement to pay for the uniform or deduction is **not allowed**.
- **If you suspect a violation**, you can file a complaint with the **NC Department of Labor’s Wage & Hour Bureau** or seek legal advice.

---

### 🧾 Bottom line:
- You’re **not necessarily owed free uniforms**, but your employer must follow strict rules to deduct the cost legally.
- If those rules aren’t followed—especially for minimum-wage workers—you have the right to challenge it.
