Cricket locked my coworkers iPhone but now he's not a cricket customer. This appears to be a common issue, but he can't login to the cricket app. 

## Assistant



## Assistant

Yeah, this is unfortunately a pretty common issue with Cricket Wireless phones. Here's what's going on and how your coworker can fix it:

---

### 🔒 **What Happened?**
Cricket locks their phones to their network, even after the customer has left. If your coworker got the phone through Cricket (or it's just a Cricket-branded model), it's likely **carrier-locked** and won't accept non-Cricket SIM cards — **even if the account is inactive**.

Since he’s **no longer a Cricket customer**, he can’t just use the app or website to unlock the device like a current customer would.

---

### ✅ **What He Needs To Unlock It**
1. **IMEI number** of the iPhone (found in Settings > General > About).
2. **Cricket Wireless device unlock eligibility:**
   - The phone was **active on Cricket’s network for 6 months** (this is the biggest requirement).
   - It's **not reported as lost/stolen** or associated with fraud.
   - He has the **original account info**, including the phone number or account number it was activated under.

---

### 🔧 **How To Unlock It (Without Logging In)**
Since he can’t use the app:

#### Option 1: **Call Cricket Support**
- 📞 Dial **1-800-CRICKET (274-2538)**
- Tell them he needs to **request a device unlock** and he **no longer has an active Cricket account.**
- Give them the **IMEI number** and **previous Cricket phone number/account info**.
- Be persistent — sometimes reps won’t help unless pushed a bit.

#### Option 2: **Use Cricket’s Unlock Portal**
Even without logging in:
- Visit: [https://www.cricketwireless.com/support/account-management/device-unlock.html](https://www.cricketwireless.com/support/account-management/device-unlock.html)
- Scroll down to "Current and Former Customers"
- Click "Unlock your device" > then “No, I am not a current customer”
- Enter the required information manually.

---

### 🚨 **If All Else Fails**
If Cricket won’t help and the device is eligible, he can:
- File an **FCC complaint** here: [https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov/](https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov/)
- This often forces Cricket to comply within a few business days.

Got it — that’s a **huge detail**. If your coworker **bought the iPhone 13 directly from the Apple Store**, it should **NOT be locked to Cricket**… **unless one of two things happened**:

---

### 🔍 So What Could’ve Happened?

#### ✅ 1. **He set it up with a Cricket SIM**
When you set up a new iPhone with a **SIM card inserted**, Apple sometimes **auto-locks** the phone to that carrier — especially if it’s the first activation.

> 🔒 Even if the phone was bought **SIM-free and unlocked**, inserting a Cricket SIM during initial setup can **trigger a lock to Cricket**.

---

#### 🚫 2. **It was incorrectly locked due to Cricket’s activation system**
Sometimes even SIM-free models get locked if **Apple receives a lock request** through a carrier activation process. This is **rare**, but it **does happen**.

---

### 📱 How to Confirm the Lock Status
Have him check the iPhone’s SIM lock status:
- Go to: **Settings > General > About**
- Scroll to **"Carrier Lock"**
  - If it says **"No SIM restrictions"**, it’s unlocked.
  - If it says **"SIM locked"** or **"Carrier Lock – Cricket"**, it’s locked to Cricket.

---

### 🛠️ What to Do Next

#### ✅ If it says **"SIM locked"** or "Carrier Lock: Cricket":
He should do the following:

1. **Contact Apple Support directly**:
   - Go to [https://getsupport.apple.com](https://getsupport.apple.com)
   - Choose **iPhone > Device Performance > The topic is not listed > Unlock request**
   - Provide the **IMEI** and explain:
     > “I bought this phone directly from the Apple Store SIM-free, but it’s locked to Cricket due to initial activation. I’m no longer a Cricket customer.”

   Apple **can verify this** and often **remove the lock** if it was an auto-lock from setup.

2. **If Apple says they can’t unlock it:**
   - Then **Cricket** must do it.
   - Use [this page](https://www.cricketwireless.com/support/account-management/device-unlock.html), select **“I am not a Cricket customer”**, and request an unlock using the **IMEI**.
   - If they stonewall him, file a **free FCC complaint**:
     > [https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov](https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov)

   The FCC complaint usually gets action **within 48–72 hours**.
