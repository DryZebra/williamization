## 🔧 **Foundations: Dialectical and Historical Materialism (for structure)**

### 🎧 **Audiobooks**
- **Karl Marx – *Capital, Vol. I*** (Narrated by LibriVox or Audible edition)
  - Not just economic theory—this lays the groundwork for seeing moral and spiritual life as materially produced.
- **Frederick Engels – *The Origin of the Family, Private Property, and the State***
  - Great for understanding how patriarchal structures emerged from material evolution, which aligns with your analysis of love and family.

### 🎙️ **Podcasts**
- **Rev Left Radio**
  - Especially episodes on *Dialectical Materialism*, *Religion & Revolution*, and *Marxist Humanism*.
- **Proles of the Round Table**
  - Clear breakdowns of theory with strong class emphasis. Check their episodes on Hegel and Lenin for understanding motion and contradiction.

## 📖 **Religious Moral Frameworks in Motion (for biblical synthesis)**

### 🎧 **Audiobooks**
- **E.P. Sanders – *Paul: A Very Short Introduction***
  - Understand Paul not as a mystic, but as a historical moral reformer in motion against imperial power.
- **Reza Aslan – *Zealot: The Life and Times of Jesus of Nazareth***
  - Strips Jesus of supernatural baggage while retaining his revolutionary moral motion. Raw, useful, flawed—but dialectically rich.

### 🎙️ **Podcasts**
- **The Bible for Normal People** (Pete Enns & Jared Byas)
  - Liberal-leaning but occasionally hits materialist moral truths without realizing it.
- **The History of the Early Church** (Podcast)
  - Rich context for understanding how Christian morality *changed through rupture*—your specialty.

## 🔥 **Directly Relevant: Synthesizing Faith, Politics, and Class**

### 🎧 **Audiobooks**
- **Howard Thurman – *Jesus and the Disinherited***
  - Not Marxist, but shows how the oppressed materially reinterpret Jesus.
- **Terry Eagleton – *Reason, Faith, and Revolution***
  - Atheist literary critic defending the *material power* of Christian moral legacy.

### 🎙️ **Podcasts**
- **The Magnificast**
  - Catholic Marxists. Liberal at times, but consistently explore *what Christianity materially does*, especially in class struggle.
- **Red Theology (YouTube/Podcast)**
  - Eastern Orthodox Marxist reflections. Sometimes mystical, but often rooted in motion, not metaphysics.
