## Assistant

Here are **5 class presentation ideas** on electrical topics tailored for a **discussion techniques class**—each designed to spark engagement, debate, and critical thinking among peers:

---

### 1. **“Are Smart Homes Worth the Risk?”**  
**Topic:** Home automation, IoT, and electrical integration  
**Discussion Focus:**  
- Pros: Convenience, energy efficiency  
- Cons: Privacy concerns, electrical code violations, hacking risks  
- **Prompt Discussion Questions:**  
  - Should smart homes be regulated like other electrical systems?
  - Who’s liable when a smart device fails—installer, manufacturer, or homeowner?

---

### 2. **“Why Grounding Saves Lives: Fact or Fear?”**  
**Topic:** Electrical grounding and safety standards  
**Discussion Focus:**  
- Explain what grounding does in a circuit  
- Explore real-life failure cases and NEC code evolution  
- **Prompt Discussion Questions:**  
  - Should DIY electrical work be legal without inspection?
  - Is the current grounding system sufficient for modern homes?

---

### 3. **“Solar Power: Independence or Illusion?”**  
**Topic:** Residential solar systems and electrical independence  
**Discussion Focus:**  
- Explain grid-tied vs. off-grid systems  
- Discuss inverter limitations, code compliance, battery storage  
- **Prompt Discussion Questions:**  
  - Is it ethical to go off-grid if your community relies on shared power?
  - Should solar be mandatory in new construction?

---

### 4. **“The Electric Car Problem No One’s Talking About”**  
**Topic:** Impact of EVs on residential and grid infrastructure  
**Discussion Focus:**  
- Charger installation, load calculation, utility strain  
- NEC updates, future-proofing homes  
- **Prompt Discussion Questions:**  
  - Should homeowners need a permit for Level 2 EV charger installs?
  - Will EVs destabilize aging grid systems in the next 10 years?

---

### 5. **“Is the 3-Phase System Obsolete in a Wireless World?”**  
**Topic:** History and future of 3-phase power systems  
**Discussion Focus:**  
- Why 3-phase exists, how it changed industry  
- Explore wireless power transmission, DC microgrids, modular batteries  
- **Prompt Discussion Questions:**  
  - Will future homes and industries still need 3-phase?
  - Should we invest in modernizing the grid or replacing it entirely?
