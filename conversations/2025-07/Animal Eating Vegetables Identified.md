That sounds like a **groundhog**, also known as a **woodchuck**. They're chubby, furry rodents that look like beavers but have **bushy tails** instead of flat ones. They're notorious for raiding gardens and can eat a ton of vegetables—especially greens, beans, and fruit.

Key traits:
- Furry, bushy tail (not flat like a beaver)
- Fat, waddling run
- Brownish fur
- Digs burrows and tunnels
- Active during the day

Let me know if it might be something else—there are a few other suspects like muskrats (usually near water) or marmots (more mountain/rocky areas), but groundhog is most likely.
