# Target Facial Recognition Myth

## User

Target *developed* one of the most advanced facial recognition algorithms in the world. 

## Assistant



## Assistant

That's a compelling claim — and it’s **partially rooted in truth**, though it's often misunderstood or exaggerated.

Here’s the grounded version:

### ✅ The True Story:
In **2002**, Target began developing sophisticated **data analytics and consumer profiling techniques**, not facial recognition. The most famous example is their **pregnancy prediction algorithm**, which analyzed shopping behavior to identify when a customer might be pregnant — often before they had told anyone else.

This *behavioral profiling* became infamous when a father found out his teenage daughter was pregnant because Target sent her baby product coupons. That incident sparked public backlash and concern over surveillance capitalism, but it had **nothing to do with facial recognition**.

### ❌ The Myth:
Over time, people began conflating Target’s **advanced data tracking** (like linking purchases, email addresses, and loyalty cards) with **facial recognition**. While companies like Facebook and Chinese tech giants truly developed cutting-edge facial recognition systems, there’s **no public evidence** that Target led or pioneered such algorithms.

### ✅ What Target *did* invest in:
- Behavior-based **predictive analytics**
- **In-store camera systems** (mostly for loss prevention)
- Possible use of **3rd-party facial recognition software** in some test stores, but not proprietary development on a leading global scale
