---

### ⚔️ 1. **Order of Saint Lazarus (Hospitaller)**
- **Origin**: 12th century, originally cared for lepers in Jerusalem.
- **Templar connection**: While not the same as the Knights Templar, it operated alongside them in the Crusader states and had military functions.
- **Status today**: Exists as a chivalric order with branches like the **Order of Saint Lazarus of Jerusalem**. Often engages in **charity and humanitarian work**.
- **Legitimacy**: Disputed. Various groups claim continuity with the original order.

---

### ⚔️ 2. **Sovereign Military Order of Malta (SMOM)**
- **Formerly**: Knights Hospitaller.
- **Templar relation**: Not Templars, but a fellow Crusader military order with similar vows and structure. When the Templars were dissolved, many members joined the Hospitallers.
- **Status today**: A **sovereign entity** (has diplomatic relations with over 100 countries), and runs hospitals and disaster relief missions globally.
- **Legitimacy**: Unquestioned; officially recognized by the Vatican.

---

### ⚔️ 3. **Ordo Supremus Militaris Templi Hierosolymitani (OSMTH)**
- **English**: Supreme Military Order of the Temple of Jerusalem.
- **Origin**: Modern (1804), founded in France after the French Revolution as a **revival of the Templar spirit**.
- **Status today**: International non-governmental organization (NGO) with consultative status at the UN.
- **Legitimacy**: Not a direct continuation of the medieval Templars, but one of the **most respected modern Templar-inspired groups**.
- **Focus**: Human rights, interfaith dialogue, Christian values.

---

### ⚔️ 4. **Freemasonry and the Knights Templar Degrees**
- **Connection**: Masonic Templars are part of certain Masonic rites (especially the **York Rite**), where "Knight Templar" is the highest order.
- **Historical link**: None confirmed. The association began in the 18th century, based on **romantic ideas** about Templar survival and secret knowledge.
- **Status**: Widespread globally, but purely fraternal and symbolic.

---

### ⚔️ 5. **The Militia Templi (Poor Knights of Christ)**
- **Founded**: 1979, Italy.
- **Mission**: Return to original Templar monasticism and liturgy, using the 1962 Roman Missal (pre-Vatican II).
- **Recognition**: Private association of faithful under the Roman Catholic Church.
- **Notable**: They live a disciplined religious life, not a fraternal one.

---

### Final Notes:
- There is **no universally accepted direct successor** to the original Knights Templar.
- Many modern Templar orders are either **religious communities, fraternal societies, or charitable NGOs**, not warrior-monks.
- Be cautious—some groups claiming Templar lineage are **outright scams** or **pseudo-orders** selling titles or knighthoods.
