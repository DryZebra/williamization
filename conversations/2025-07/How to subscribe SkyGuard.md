## Assistant

The alert in the image is from **AccuWeather’s SkyGuard®** service, which is a **paid professional weather warning system** used by businesses, government agencies, and critical infrastructure operations. It's designed to deliver **hyper-local, site-specific severe weather alerts**, often used for **employee safety, operations planning, and emergency response**.

### Here's how you can get subscribed or set it up for your business:

---

### ✅ **Step-by-Step to Subscribe to AccuWeather SkyGuard**

#### 1. **Visit the Official Page**
Go to [**https://www.accuweather.com/business/skyguard**](https://www.accuweather.com/business/skyguard)  
or just Google **"SkyGuard AccuWeather"**

#### 2. **Contact Sales**
You’ll need to reach out directly. Look for buttons like:
- **"Request a Demo"**
- **"Contact Sales"**
- Or call their business solutions number (often listed at the bottom of the page)

#### 3. **Provide Business Info**
Be prepared to give:
- Your name and organization
- Locations you want covered
- Type of operations (construction, manufacturing, school, etc.)
- Preferred alert channels (email, text, call, etc.)

#### 4. **Pricing & Deployment**
Pricing varies depending on:
- Number of locations
- Types of alerts and integrations (API, mobile, dashboard access)
- Employee safety features (e.g., automatic notifications)

---

### 🔒 Why Businesses Use SkyGuard
- **Faster than public alerts**: Warnings are often issued before the National Weather Service
- **Custom geofencing**: Alerts are only sent for YOUR location (no irrelevant warnings)
- **24/7 meteorologist monitoring**
- Can integrate with security systems, email, text, or apps

---

### 🧠 Want an Alternative (Personal Use)?
If you're not a business but want similar lightning or severe weather alerts:
- **MyLightningTracker**, **WeatherBug**, or **RadarScope** for mobile
- **NOAA Weather Radio** for emergencies
- Or set up **IFTTT** or **Zapier** with National Weather Service feeds
