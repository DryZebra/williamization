Yes, **you can play Diablo in split-screen co-op AND invite two friends online**—but it depends on which **Diablo game** you're playing and what **platform** you're on.

Here’s the breakdown for each game:

---

### 🔥 **Diablo IV**
- ✅ **Local (split-screen) co-op**: Only supports **2 players**, and **only on consoles** (PS5, Xbox Series X/S).
- ✅ **Online co-op**: Supports up to **4 players**.
- ❌ **Split-screen + online**: Yes, but **only 2 local players** + **up to 2 online** = **4 total**.
- So **YES**, you can play **you + friend in split-screen**, and invite **two more friends online**.

---

### 🔥 **Diablo III (console version)**
- ✅ **Local co-op**: Up to **4 players on the same console** (split-screen style).
- ✅ **Online co-op**: Up to 4 players.
- ❌ **Mix of local + online**: **Not supported**. If two people are playing locally, you **can't** invite others online.
- So **NO**, you can’t mix local and online in D3 on console.

---

### 🔥 **Diablo II: Resurrected**
- ❌ **No split-screen support at all**.
- ✅ **Online multiplayer** only.
- So **NO**, not possible.

---

### ✅ Summary for Diablo IV:
| Setup                          | Diablo IV (PS5/Xbox) |
|-------------------------------|-----------------------|
| 2-player local split-screen   | ✅ Yes                |
| 4-player online               | ✅ Yes                |
