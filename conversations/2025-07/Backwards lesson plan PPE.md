## **Lesson Plan: Electrical Safety & PPE Mastery**

### **Stage 1: Desired Results**
**Goal:**  
Apprentices will *independently* and *proactively* work safely in labs and jobsites without needing to be reminded or prompted.

**Essential Understanding:**  
- Safety is the personal responsibility of every electrician.  
- PPE is not just a requirement—it’s a lifesaving tool.  
- Recognizing and mitigating hazards is as important as the technical work itself.

**Key Knowledge & Skills:**  
- Identify and explain the function of common PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, arc-rated clothing, hearing protection, boots, respirators, etc.  
- Understand and follow OSHA 10, OSHA 30, and NFPA 70E safety protocols.  
- Complete Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs) and hazard sheets accurately.  
- Spot and mitigate specific hazards on real or simulated job sites.  
- Take initiative to act safely without prompts from instructors or journeymen.

---

### **Stage 2: Evidence of Learning**

**Performance Tasks (Authentic Assessments):**  
- PPE Identification & Use Test (hands-on): Students will demonstrate donning/doffing PPE and explaining use cases.  
- NFPA 70E Compliance Scenario: Students will read a simulated electrical task and determine required arc-flash PPE.  
- Job Hazard Analysis (JHA) Sheet Completion: Students will fill out a real JHA based on a task like changing a breaker or terminating in a panel.  
- Jobsite Hazard Recognition Walkthrough: Students will analyze staged hazards in lab or via video/photos and document risks and mitigation.  
- Final Safety Audit: Instructor observes apprentices working in lab settings without prompts—checking for PPE use, awareness, and safe practices.

**Supporting Evidence:**  
- Completion and certification of OSHA 10 (Yr 1), OSHA 30 (Yr 2+), and NFPA 70E training.  
- Quizzes on safety procedures and PPE.  
- Group discussion reflections and peer evaluations.

---

### **Stage 3: Learning Plan (Instruction)**

| **Week** | **Topic** | **Activities** | **Assessment** |
|----------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| 1        | Introduction to Safety Culture | Safety mindset lecture. Watch real incident videos. Group discussion: “What went wrong?” | Exit ticket: List 3 ways to prevent what happened. |
| 2        | PPE Types & Use | PPE demo with hands-on stations. Try on gear, match PPE to tasks. | PPE Identification & Use Test |
| 3        | OSHA 10 Certification | Self-paced OSHA 10 online modules in class/lab. | Certificate earned |
| 4        | Hazard Identification | Lab walkthrough with staged hazards. Hazard bingo/scavenger hunt. | Group worksheet on identified hazards |
| 5        | NFPA 70E Basics | Arc flash videos. Labeling systems. Clothing ratings. Hands-on arc-rated suit demo. | Scenario card: Pick correct PPE for task. |
| 6        | JHA / Hazard Sheets | Practice filling out hazard sheets from real-life tasks. | Submit completed JHA |
| 7        | OSHA 30 (Year 2) or NFPA 70E Certification | Support for completion of higher-level safety certs. | Certificate earned |
| 8        | Final Safety Walkthrough | In-lab observation of student work without reminders. | Rubric-based evaluation |
| 9        | Reflection and Reinforcement | Group roundtable: “What does safety mean to you now?” | Safety Reflection Essay |
