# Puppy food duration

I'm getting that puppy. How long should I feed puppy food?

## Assistant

Congrats on the new pup! 🐶

In general:

- **Small to medium breeds** (under ~50 lbs adult weight):  
  Feed puppy food until about **12 months old**.

- **Large breeds** (over ~50 lbs adult weight):  
  Feed **large breed puppy food** until about **12–18 months old**, depending on growth rate.

Puppy food is higher in calories, protein, and nutrients like calcium and DHA to support growth. Once your dog reaches their expected adult size and growth slows down, you can switch to adult food.

When you do switch:
- Transition slowly over **5–7 days** to avoid stomach upset.
- Mix increasing amounts of adult food with decreasing amounts of puppy food.

If you're unsure how big your pup will get or what breed mix they are, I can help estimate if you give me their current age, weight, and what you know about their breed.
