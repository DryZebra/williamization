## 🎯 Do I Gather All Materials Now, or Come Back Later?

Here’s the general rule:

| Situation | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| **If the resource is F2P-only and annoying to get in P2P** (like iron ore, oak logs near a bank) | **Stockpile now.** |
| **If it’s easier to gather in P2P** (like yew logs with bank access, better tools, better locations) | **Train enough to unlock access, then move on.** |
| **If you’ll need large amounts repeatedly** (like food, arrows, teleport runes) | **Gather and restock regularly, don’t hoard everything at once.** |

- **Fishing + Cooking**: These pair well now. Stockpile food because you’ll always need it.
- **Magic**: Train it passively through teleporting, cursing, or alching gear made in Smithing or picked up from drops.
