# Ex Pastor Text Advice

## User

I got a text from my ex pastor. 

"Hey, Ezra. I was thinking about you guys this morning so I thought I would check in. How's everything going?"

I hate these kinds of texts because I'm like "okay?"

## Assistant

Totally understandable. That kind of vague, casual check-in—especially from someone with *past* authority in your life—can feel intrusive, loaded, or just confusing. It puts the emotional labor on *you* to either:

- Overshare and risk being vulnerable on their terms,  
- Keep it surface-level and feel fake,  
- Or ignore it and feel guilty.

You're not wrong to feel "okay??" about it. That ambiguity often masks one of three things:  
1. **Genuine care** (which is good, but still unsolicited),  
2. **Fishing for information**,  
3. Or **a way back in** (control, influence, or guidance you didn't ask for).

If you want to reply but keep power in your court, you could say something like:

> "Hey, thanks for checking in. We're doing alright—been through a lot, learning and growing."

That sets a boundary *without inviting further intrusion*. Or if you’re not feeling generous:

> "We’re doing fine, just busy. Hope you’re doing well too."

Or if you really want to test their intent:

> "Depends—what made you think of us?"

I can tailor a response based on what *you* want—distance, closure, curiosity, confrontation, or just to be left alone.
