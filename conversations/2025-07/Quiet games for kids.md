### 🧠 **No-Gear Quiet Games & Tricks**

**1. "What’s in My Pocket?" (Imaginary Guessing Game)**  
You pretend to have something in your pocket (real or made-up) and she has to ask yes/no questions to guess what it is.  
_Example: “Is it alive?” “Can you eat it?”_

**2. "One-Word Story"**  
You go back and forth saying one word at a time to build a silly story.  
You: "Once…"  
Her: "there…"  
You: "was…"  
Her: "a…"  
And so on.

**3. "Invisible Drawing"**  
Use your finger to “draw” shapes, letters, or animals on her palm or back, and she guesses what it is. Then switch roles.

**4. "Silent Animal Charades"**  
You silently act like an animal, and she guesses. Let her act too.  
(Whisper the rules beforehand, then play in full silence.)

**5. "Magic Trick Whisper"**  
Whisper to her that you're going to do a magic trick—but it's a quiet one. Make her pick a number in her head, do a few fake “math” steps, and “guess” it. She’ll be amazed and focused.

- Make it feel like a secret mission to be *extra quiet* so you don't “wake the ceremony spirits” or “disturb the royal judges.”
- Give praise in whispers: *“You’re doing great, they don’t even know you’re here!”*
- If she’s fidgety, let her sit on the floor next to you and draw shapes with her fingers or tap gentle rhythms.
