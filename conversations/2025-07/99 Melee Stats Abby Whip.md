# 99 Melee Stats Abby Whip

## User

With pretty standard gear at 80/84/80 atk str def how long will it take for me to get 99 in atk str and def using the controlled lash shared xp Abby whip?

## Assistant



## Assistant

At 80 Attack, 84 Strength, and 80 Defence, using an **Abyssal whip on controlled/shared XP** means you’re training all three melee stats **simultaneously**—at one-third XP each. This slows down the rate at which any one stat levels compared to focusing on it alone.

---

### 📊 XP Required
Here’s how much XP you need to reach 99 from your current levels:

- **Attack (80 → 99)** = 11,824,010 XP
- **Strength (84 → 99)** = 10,668,179 XP
- **Defence (80 → 99)** = 11,824,010 XP  
- **Total XP needed across all three** = **34,316,199 XP**

Since shared XP divides all gained XP equally across the three stats, you'll need to earn **~102.95 million combat XP** total on controlled style to reach 99 in all three stats.

---

### ⚔️ XP Rates (Abyssal Whip, Shared XP)
- At your levels, you’ll average **55k–70k XP/hour** total on controlled, depending on location:
  - **NMZ (Absorption/Rock Cake Method)**: ~70k–80k/hr (including all 3 stats)
  - **Slayer Training**: ~50k–60k/hr
  - **AFK Sand Crabs**: ~45k–55k/hr

For calculation, we’ll average it at **65k XP/hour (shared)**, which means about **21.6k XP/hour per stat**.

---

### 🕒 Time Estimate
To gain 34.3M XP at 65k/hour:
- `34,316,199 / 65,000 ≈ 528.7 hours`

So you'll need **~530 hours** of training on controlled with the Abyssal whip to hit 99 in all three melee stats.
