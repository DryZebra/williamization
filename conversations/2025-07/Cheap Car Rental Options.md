---

## 🔧 **1. Drop-Off Fee (Logistics Penalty)**  
When you rent one-way, the company **has to get the car back** (or reassign it locally). That’s:
- Labor to transport it or
- Loss of inventory in that market

So they slap on a **"drop fee"**—sometimes as high as $300.  
Even if you only drive the car for 10 hours.

> **This fee is invisible until checkout** or buried in small print.

---

## 💰 **2. Location Supply and Demand**
- **Raleigh → Detroit**: Cars in NC are cheap because supply is high and demand is low.
- **Detroit → Raleigh**: Higher rental demand in Detroit, so prices are higher.

That means:
- Your outbound leg is a “cheap car + big drop fee”
- Your return leg is “expensive car + big drop fee”

---

## 🔁 **3. Round Trip Rentals Incentivize Return**
If you bring the car back, the company:
- Keeps it in their rotation
- Doesn’t need extra labor
- Can offer you **lower total cost for more days**

That's why **7 days round trip is cheaper than 2 days one-way.** It's ***insane***, but real.
