# Strength Faith Build Stats

## User

What should my final stats look like for a strength faith build in elden ring?

## Assistant

For a Strength-Faith build in *Elden Ring*, you'll want to balance your stats to take full advantage of both powerful weapons and Faith-based incantations. Here's a recommended stat spread by the time you reach level 150 (which is a common endgame level target):

### Primary Stats
1. **Vigor:** 50-60  
   - High Vigor is essential to survive tough enemies and bosses. Aim for around 50-60 to ensure you have enough health.

2. **Mind:** 20-30  
   - This will give you enough FP to cast powerful incantations without running out too quickly.

3. **Endurance:** 25-35  
   - Endurance is crucial for maintaining stamina, especially when wielding heavy weapons and armor. It also increases your Equip Load, allowing you to wear better gear without suffering from slow rolls.

4. **Strength:** 40-60  
   - As a Strength-Faith build, you'll want to lean heavily into Strength. A higher Strength stat lets you wield the heaviest weapons and deal significant physical damage.

5. **Dexterity:** 15-20  
   - This stat should be kept relatively low, just enough to meet weapon requirements if necessary. You won’t need much Dexterity for a pure Strength-Faith build.

6. **Faith:** 40-60  
   - Faith is your primary stat for casting incantations. With Faith in the 40-60 range, you can cast powerful offensive and support spells.

### Secondary Stats
1. **Intelligence:** 10-15  
   - Keep Intelligence at the base level unless a specific weapon or spell you want to use requires more. It generally won't be useful for a Strength-Faith build.

2. **Arcane:** 10-20  
   - Arcane is generally not a priority unless you are using weapons or incantations that scale with it. Keep it at base or just enough for item discovery or certain spells.

### Final Notes
- **Weapon Choices:** Focus on weapons that scale well with both Strength and Faith, like the *Blasphemous Blade* or *Golden Order Greatsword*. Greatswords, Colossal Weapons, and Hammers work particularly well with this build.
  
- **Incantations:** Prioritize offensive Faith spells like *Lightning Spear*, *Black Flame*, and *Elden Stars*. Also, consider buff spells like *Golden Vow* or healing incantations for versatility.

- **Armor:** Aim for armor that provides good poise without exceeding your Equip Load limit, keeping you in the medium roll range for better mobility.

This stat allocation will allow you to dish out heavy physical damage while also being a capable caster with powerful Faith-based abilities, making you versatile and resilient in both PvE and PvP settings.
