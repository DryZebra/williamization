### Action Plan for Community Engagement

#### Step 1: Initial Investigation
1. **Identify Local Leaders:** Each member identifies key figures in their local communities (e.g., community leaders, religious leaders, school principals).
2. **Conduct Surveys:** Develop a simple survey to gather information on community concerns. Distribute these surveys through local events, social media, and door-to-door interactions.
3. **Attend Community Meetings:** Attend town hall meetings, city council meetings, and school board meetings to observe and record community issues.

#### Step 2: Data Collection and Analysis
1. **Compile Data:** Collect and compile the data from surveys, meetings, and personal interactions.
2. **Analyze Concerns:** Identify common themes and specific needs within each community.
3. **Create a Report:** Summarize findings in a report to share with the team.

#### Step 3: Hypothesis and Planning
1. **Develop Hypotheses:** Based on the data, create hypotheses about the most effective ways to address community concerns.
2. **Plan Initial Actions:** Develop action plans for small-scale initiatives. Examples could include:
   - Organizing park cleanups
   - Hosting community discussions
   - Partnering with local organizations to address specific needs

#### Step 4: Implementation
1. **Local Initiatives:** Each member implements their local action plans, starting with small, manageable projects.
2. **Gather Feedback:** Collect feedback from the community on the effectiveness of these initiatives.
3. **Document Results:** Record outcomes, challenges, and successes of each project.

#### Step 5: Iterative Improvement
1. **Assess Results:** Analyze feedback and results to assess the impact of the initiatives.
2. **Refine Strategies:** Adjust and refine strategies based on the assessment.
3. **Expand Efforts:** Plan for larger-scale initiatives based on refined strategies and successful outcomes.

#### Step 6: Statewide Coordination
1. **Regular Meetings:** Schedule regular meetings (e.g., monthly or quarterly) to discuss progress, share insights, and coordinate efforts.
2. **Group Events:** Organize larger group events in major cities across NC to increase visibility and impact. Possible activities include:
   - Hosting community fairs
   - Partnering with local organizations for larger projects
   - Conducting workshops on community engagement

#### Step 7: Continuous Learning and Growth
1. **Continuous Feedback Loop:** Maintain a continuous feedback loop with the community to stay updated on evolving needs.
2. **Ongoing Education:** Encourage team members to participate in training on community organizing and engagement.
3. **Adapt and Scale:** Continuously adapt approaches based on new learnings and scale successful initiatives to other areas.

By following this structured approach, we can ensure that our efforts are rooted in the actual needs of our communities, allowing us to make a meaningful and sustainable impact.

### Local Investigation Guide

#### Step 1: Preparation
1. **Define Objectives:** Clearly outline what you aim to discover through your investigation. For example, understanding the primary concerns of residents, identifying community resources, or recognizing gaps in services.
2. **Prepare Materials:** Create survey forms, question lists, and any necessary informational pamphlets about your initiative. Ensure you have notebooks or digital devices for recording observations and feedback.

#### Step 2: Identify Key Areas and Groups
1. **Map Your Community:** Identify key areas such as neighborhoods, parks, community centers, schools, and local businesses.
2. **Identify Key Groups:** Recognize important community groups including religious organizations, civic groups, school boards, and local clubs.

#### Step 3: Conduct Surveys and Interviews
1. **Door-to-Door Surveys:**
   - **Plan Your Route:** Choose specific neighborhoods to visit.
   - **Introduce Yourself:** Clearly explain who you are and the purpose of your visit.
   - **Ask Open-Ended Questions:** Use questions like “What are the biggest challenges you face in this community?” or “What changes would you like to see?”
   - **Record Responses:** Take detailed notes on the feedback you receive.

2. **Public Place Interactions:**
   - **Visit Busy Locations:** Parks, community centers, and local events are good places to meet people.
   - **Engage in Conversation:** Approach people in a friendly manner, explain your mission, and ask for their input.
   - **Distribute Surveys:** Hand out or collect surveys from people willing to participate.

3. **Online Engagement:**
   - **Join Local Groups:** Participate in Facebook groups, Nextdoor, and other local online communities.
   - **Monitor Discussions:** Observe what topics are frequently discussed and what concerns are raised.
   - **Post Surveys:** Share your survey link and encourage group members to participate.

4. **Interviews with Key Figures:**
   - **Schedule Appointments:** Arrange meetings with community leaders, local business owners, and heads of organizations.
   - **Prepare Questions:** Have a set of specific questions tailored to their role and perspective.
   - **Listen Actively:** Take note of their insights and any suggestions they offer.

#### Step 4: Attend Community Meetings
1. **Research Events:** Find out when town hall meetings, city council meetings, school board meetings, and other public gatherings occur.
2. **Participate and Observe:**
   - **Take Notes:** Record the main issues discussed and the community’s response.
   - **Engage if Possible:** Ask questions or provide input if the format allows for public participation.

#### Step 5: Compile and Analyze Data
1. **Organize Information:** Sort the data collected from surveys, interviews, and meetings into categories based on common themes.
2. **Identify Key Issues:** Highlight the most frequently mentioned concerns and needs.
3. **Create a Summary Report:** Compile your findings into a clear and concise report, summarizing key issues and potential opportunities for action.

#### Step 6: Share Findings and Plan Next Steps
1. **Team Discussion:** Share your report with your team and discuss the findings.
2. **Develop Action Plans:** Based on the identified needs, create specific action plans to address the community’s concerns.
3. **Engage the Community:** Keep the community informed about your findings and planned actions, seeking further input and involvement.

By following these steps, you can conduct a thorough local investigation to understand the needs and concerns of your community, laying the groundwork for effective and targeted community engagement initiatives.
