# Lifetime Deductible Explained

## User

What is a lifetime deductible?

## Assistant



## Assistant

A lifetime deductible is a one-time amount that you must pay out-of-pocket before your insurance company begins to cover your claims. Unlike an annual deductible, which resets every year, a lifetime deductible is paid only once, and once it's met, it typically doesn't need to be paid again for the duration of the policyholder's life or the life of the policy. This term is more commonly associated with certain types of insurance, like some health or long-term care insurance policies.
