# Unity of Opposites

## User

What's the dialectical materialist idea that opposites become each other? What am I thinking of?

## Assistant

The concept you're thinking of is likely the idea of **"the unity and interpenetration of opposites,"** which is a key principle in dialectical materialism. This idea is most notably associated with the philosopher **Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel** and was later developed further by **Karl Marx** and **Friedrich Engels** within the framework of dialectical materialism.

The principle suggests that all things contain within them contradictory elements, or opposites, which are interconnected and dependent on each other. These contradictions drive the process of change and development. Over time, these opposites can transform into each other, or at least into a synthesis that resolves the tension between them, leading to a new stage of development.

For example, in the dialectical process, something like "freedom" might contain its opposite, "constraint," and through their interaction, a new form of freedom could emerge that resolves this contradiction in a more advanced way.

This idea is central to dialectical thinking and is used to explain how social, economic, and natural processes evolve over time through the resolution of contradictions.
