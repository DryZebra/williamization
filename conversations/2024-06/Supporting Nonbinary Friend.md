## Assistant

It's wonderful that your friend is opening up to you about their feelings. Here are a few ways you can support them:

1. **Listen and Validate**: Let them know that you are there to listen and that their feelings are valid. You might say something like, "Thank you for sharing this with me. It's completely valid to have those feelings and thoughts."

2. **Ask Open-Ended Questions**: Encourage them to explore their feelings further by asking open-ended questions. For example, "Can you tell me more about what your character makes you feel or think about?"

3. **Offer Support**: Assure them that you are there for them no matter what. You could say, "I'm here for you and support you in exploring whatever this means for you."

4. **Educate Yourself**: Take the time to learn more about nonbinary identities and experiences. This can help you be a better ally and offer informed support.

5. **Avoid Making Assumptions**: Everyone's journey with their gender identity is unique. Avoid making assumptions about what they are experiencing or what it might mean.

6. **Respect Their Pace**: Let them explore their feelings at their own pace. Offer your support without pushing them to label themselves or make decisions before they are ready.

A supportive response might be something like:

"Thank you for sharing that with me. It sounds like your character has helped you discover something important about yourself. I'm here for you as you explore these feelings. If you ever want to talk more about it, I'm here to listen."
