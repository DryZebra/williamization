Here is a recipe for a tasty and filling stir-fry using the ingredients you provided:

Ingredients:
- 1 cup rice
- 1 bag frozen broccoli stir fry
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons all purpose flour
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons dry roasted peanuts
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook the rice according to package instructions.
2. In a skillet or wok, melt the butter over medium-high heat.
3. Add the frozen broccoli stir fry to the skillet and cook until tender, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Crack the eggs into the skillet and scramble them with the vegetables.
5. Add the soy sauce and mix well.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour and chicken broth until there are no lumps.
7. Add the chicken broth mixture to the skillet and stir until the sauce thickens, about 2-3 minutes.
8. Add the dry roasted peanuts and mix well.
9. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
10. Serve the stir-fry over the cooked rice.

Enjoy your delicious and easy two-ingredient stir-fry!
