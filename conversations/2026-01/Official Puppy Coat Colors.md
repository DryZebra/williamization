## 🌞 **Thousand Sunny**
*(the slightly darker, warmer-toned pup)*

**Official color terms:**
- **Apricot** *(primary / most accurate)*
- Sometimes registered as **Dark Cream** depending on breeder/registry

**Why:**
- Warm golden–peach undertone
- Noticeable saturation compared to the other pup
- Consistent warmth across different lighting (doesn’t wash out to white)

This is the classic **apricot doodle tone** — very stable as they age.

## 🌌 **Aurora**
*(the lighter, cooler-toned pup)*

**Official color terms:**
- **Cream** *(primary)*
- May be described as **Light Cream** or **Champagne Cream**

**Why:**
- Much paler base coat
- Cooler, almost ivory cast
- In brighter light, edges toward off-white but still clearly pigmented (not true white)

This is the color that often *appears white indoors* but shows warmth outdoors.
