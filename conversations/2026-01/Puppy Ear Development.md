### 🧠 Underdeveloped nervous system
At birth, a puppy’s **auditory system isn’t ready**. The ear canal is sealed with tissue because the inner ear and the neural pathways for hearing are still forming. Sound processing is actually one of the *later* senses to come online.

### 🛡️ Protection during birth & early life
An open ear canal would be:
- vulnerable to **infection**
- vulnerable to **debris**
- overstimulating while the brain is still wiring itself

Nature basically says: *“No input yet, brain’s under construction.”*

### 🌡️ Helps with warmth & survival
Sealed ears (and eyes) help reduce heat loss in newborns. For tiny mammals, **temperature regulation is life or death**, especially in the first week.

### ⏱️ When do they open?
- **Ears:** usually around **10–14 days**
- **Eyes:** roughly the same window

When the ears open, hearing starts **gradually**, not all at once. At first they mostly respond to vibrations and louder sounds.

### 🐾 Why this matters practically
Until their ears open:
- Loud noises won’t “train” or desensitize them
- Gentle handling, warmth, and smell matter way more than sound
- Mom’s heartbeat, warmth, and scent are the primary regulators

Given what you’re dealing with right now (winter + newborns), this sealed-up phase is **normal, healthy, and exactly what you want to see**.
