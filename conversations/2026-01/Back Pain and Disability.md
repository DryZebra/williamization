## Second: disability is NOT about “having something wrong”

This is the biggest misconception.

**Disability is not granted because a scan looks bad.**  
**Disability is granted because you cannot sustain substantial gainful employment.**

The system asks:
- Can you work **consistently**?
- Can you work **full time**?
- Can you do so **without excessive pain, flare-ups, or absence**?
- Can you do *any* job, not just your trade?

A lot of people with real pain never qualify.  
A lot of people with “mild” imaging findings *do* qualify — because function matters more than pictures.
