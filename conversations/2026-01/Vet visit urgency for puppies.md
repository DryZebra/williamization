## Assistant

Short answer: **yes, it’s okay to wait** — *as long as* everyone truly looks stable. You’re not being negligent here. You’re triaging correctly.

Let me be very clear and calm this down for you.

### What the **initial vet visit** is *actually* for
In the first few days, that visit is mostly:
- A **baseline exam**
- Checking mom for **retained placenta, infection, mastitis**
- Confirming puppies are viable and roughly normal

It is **not** urgent medical intervention *if*:
- Mom is eating, drinking, resting
- Puppies are warm, nursing, gaining weight, vocalizing normally
- No discharge that smells foul
- No lethargy, seizures, labored breathing, or fading pups

In a perfect world, vets like to see them early.  
In the *real world*, **many responsible breeders wait 1–2 weeks** unless there’s a problem.

### What is **more important right now**
Given **dead winter + power risk + newborns**, your priorities are exactly right:

1. **Warmth (non-negotiable)**
   - 85–90°F for newborns
   - Draft-free
   - Layers + insulation
   - Backup heat (generator) ✔️

2. **Mom stability**
   - Eating
   - Drinking
   - Calm, attentive
   - No fever, no foul discharge

3. **Puppy monitoring**
   - Nursing frequently
   - Gaining weight daily
   - Normal whining (not constant screaming, not silence)

A freezing car ride + stress + exposure is **more dangerous** than delaying a routine check by a few days.

### When you **must go immediately**
Drop everything and go if you see **any** of these:
- Mom stops eating or drinking
- Green/black foul-smelling discharge
- Hot, painful, red mammary glands
- Puppy becomes cold, limp, stops nursing, or goes silent
- Constant high-pitched screaming from a pup
- Labored breathing, tremors, seizures

If none of that is happening — **you are within reason to wait**.

### What I’d recommend (balanced, not paranoid)
- Finish your **storm + heat prep first**
- Do **daily weights** on puppies (huge early warning system)
- Schedule the vet visit for **7–10 days out**
- Call the vet office and **tell them exactly what you told me** — they’ll likely reassure you and lock in a date

You are acting like someone who understands **risk management**, not someone being careless.  
Right now, **warmth and stability preserve life more than a clipboard exam**.

- How many days old exactly
- Approx room temp
- Breed size (roughly)

I can sanity-check your setup and make sure nothing obvious is missing.
