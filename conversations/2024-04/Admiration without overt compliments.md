# Admiration without overt compliments

## User

My girlfriend says she hates compliments but I want her to know that she's the greatest person of all time. What can I say?

## Assistant

If your girlfriend doesn't like compliments, you could express your admiration for her in a more subtle and genuine way. You could say something like, "I admire your strength, kindness, and intelligence. You're truly remarkable." This way, you're acknowledging her without using overt compliments that she may not appreciate.
